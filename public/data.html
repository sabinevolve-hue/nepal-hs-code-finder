Nepal trade data you can download

The same official figures the site’s pages use, as plain CSV files. Free to use; please credit the Department of Customs, Nepal as the source and link to customsnepal.com.

FileWhat it contains
Nepal trade by partner country, FY 2082/83Imports, exports, balance and customs revenue for each of 144 trading partners, in rupees.
Nepal trade by partner country, FY 2079/80 to 2082/83Imports and exports per partner per fiscal year (576 rows).
Nepal trade by customs office, FY 2082/83Imports and exports cleared at each of 28 customs offices, with shares.

Amounts are in Nepali rupees (not thousands). “customs_revenue_npr” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports on imports from that country: customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border.
