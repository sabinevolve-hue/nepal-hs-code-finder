Nepal export guides

Nepal exported about $1.2B of merchandise in 2024. These guides cover its signature exports — who buys them around the world, the price they fetch, and how to export from Nepal — with a live, interactive Export Explorer for the full country-by-country, product-by-product data.

10Guides
69% of Nepal’s exports go to India — much of it refined soybean & palm oil re-exported after processing, a duty-driven flow rather than domestic production. The real manufactured and agri exports below are spread across the US, the EU and beyond.
