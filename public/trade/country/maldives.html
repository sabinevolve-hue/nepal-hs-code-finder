About this page’s data

Figures are from the Department of Customs Foreign Trade Statistics for FY 2082/83 (values in Rs. thousand, customs/assessable value). Trade values are not the same as retail prices and exclude under/over-invoicing and informal trade. Use them as direction and scale, not exact accounting.
