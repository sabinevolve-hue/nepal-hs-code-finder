Who buys it

India takes about 97% of these exports — a heavy dependence on one market. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: India · Romania · United States · China · Japan · Bhutan.

When it ships

Exports peaked in Jestha (Rs 1.44 arba) and were lowest in Bhadra (Rs 13.3 crore). Nepali months start mid-month: Shrawan is mid-July to mid-August.

Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much iron and steel products does Nepal export?
Nepal exported iron and steel products worth Rs 6.15 arba in FY 2082/83, 1.9% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s iron and steel products?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 5.99 arba), Romania (Rs 7.9 crore), United States (Rs 1.8 crore), China (Rs 1.7 crore).
What are Nepal’s biggest iron and steel products exports?
By value: Table, kitchen or household articles of st inless… (Rs 2.43 arba); Rolled iron/steel, width >=600mm, painted, varnis… (Rs 1.62 arba); Rolled iron/steel, width >=600mm, plated o coated… (Rs 65.9 crore); Waste and scrap of stainless steel (Rs 39.8 crore).
Are Nepal’s iron and steel products exports growing?
Exports were Rs 6.15 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 16.36 arba the year before, a change of -62%.
Does Nepal also import iron and steel products?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 1.70 kharba in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, Japan.
