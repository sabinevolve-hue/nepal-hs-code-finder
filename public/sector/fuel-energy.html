Who supplies it

India is the largest supplier with about 97% of the sector’s imports; the top three together supply about 99%. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: India · United States · China · South Africa · United Arab Emirates · Malaysia.

Country figures add up each product’s six largest sources, so small flows from other countries are not counted and the true shares can be slightly higher.

Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 30%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s fuel and energy import market?
Nepal imported fuel and energy worth Rs 3.93 kharba in FY 2082/83, 18.7% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much fuel and energy does Nepal export?
Nepal exported fuel and energy worth Rs 2.0 crore in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s fuel and energy?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 3.82 kharba), United States (Rs 4.73 arba), China (Rs 2.44 arba), South Africa (Rs 74.1 crore).
What are the biggest fuel and energy imports?
By value: Diesel (Rs 1.72 kharba); Petrol (Rs 77.27 arba); Liquidified Petrolium Gas (LPG) (Rs 60.28 arba); ATF (Rs 26.65 arba).
How much does the government collect on fuel and energy imports?
Customs collected Rs 1.30 kharba on these imports in FY 2082/83, about Rs 33.0 for every Rs 100 of goods (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
