Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much yarn and fabric does Nepal export?
Nepal exported yarn and fabric worth Rs 19.23 arba in FY 2082/83, 6.1% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s yarn and fabric?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 17.84 arba), Turkey (Rs 1.35 arba), United States (Rs 87 lakh), Yemen (Rs 87 lakh).
What are Nepal’s biggest yarn and fabric exports?

Are Nepal’s yarn and fabric exports growing?
Exports were Rs 19.23 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 17.48 arba the year before, a change of +10%.
Does Nepal also import yarn and fabric?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 17.23 arba in FY 2082/83, mainly from China, India, Thailand.
