Who supplies it

India is the largest supplier with about 62% of the sector’s imports; the top three together supply about 97%. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: India · China · Thailand · Kenya · Japan · Cameroon.

Country figures add up each product’s six largest sources, so small flows from other countries are not counted and the true shares can be slightly higher.

When it comes in

Imports peaked in Jestha (Rs 15.43 arba) and were lowest in Shrawan (Rs 8.02 arba). Nepali months start mid-month: Shrawan is mid-July to mid-August.

Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 80%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin. Excise duty also applies to vehicles, alcohol and tobacco and is often the largest part of the bill.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s vehicles, motorcycles and EVs import market?
Nepal imported vehicles, motorcycles and EVs worth Rs 1.24 kharba in FY 2082/83, 5.9% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much vehicles, motorcycles and EVs does Nepal export?
Nepal exported vehicles, motorcycles and EVs worth Rs 4.0 crore in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s vehicles, motorcycles and EVs?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 77.04 arba), China (Rs 39.89 arba), Thailand (Rs 3.08 arba), Kenya (Rs 1.49 arba).
