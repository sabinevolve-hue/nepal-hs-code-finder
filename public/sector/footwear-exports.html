Who buys it

India takes about 99% of these exports — a heavy dependence on one market. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: India · Denmark · Japan · Austria · United Arab Emirates · China.

When it ships

Exports peaked in Asar (Rs 33.4 crore) and were lowest in Falgun (Rs 19.9 crore). Nepali months start mid-month: Shrawan is mid-July to mid-August.

Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much footwear does Nepal export?
Nepal exported footwear worth Rs 3.19 arba in FY 2082/83, 1.0% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s footwear?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 3.16 arba), Denmark (Rs 1.4 crore), Japan (Rs 99 lakh), Austria (Rs 37 lakh).

Are Nepal’s footwear exports growing?
Exports were Rs 3.19 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 2.04 arba the year before, a change of +56%.
Does Nepal also import footwear?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 10.14 arba in FY 2082/83, mainly from China, India, Viet Nam.
