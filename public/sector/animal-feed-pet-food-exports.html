Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much animal feed, oil cake and pet food does Nepal export?
Nepal exported animal feed, oil cake and pet food worth Rs 10.28 arba in FY 2082/83, 3.3% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s animal feed, oil cake and pet food?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 5.43 arba), United States (Rs 4.20 arba), Canada (Rs 31.6 crore), United Kingdom (Rs 11.7 crore).
What are Nepal’s biggest animal feed, oil cake and pet food exports?
By value: Dog or cat food, put up for retail sale (Rs 4.81 arba); Oil-cake of low erucic acid rape or colza seeds (Rs 3.80 arba); Other-Brans, sharps and other residues except res… (Rs 92.7 crore); Brans, sharps and other residues of other cereals (Rs 61.3 crore).
Are Nepal’s animal feed, oil cake and pet food exports growing?
Exports were Rs 10.28 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 8.45 arba the year before, a change of +22%.
Does Nepal also import animal feed, oil cake and pet food?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 29.02 arba in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, Bangladesh, United States.
