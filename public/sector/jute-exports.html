Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much jute goods does Nepal export?
Nepal exported jute goods worth Rs 11.02 arba in FY 2082/83, 3.5% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s jute goods?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 10.82 arba), United States (Rs 11.4 crore), Germany (Rs 1.5 crore), United Kingdom (Rs 1.5 crore).
What are Nepal’s biggest jute goods exports?
By value: Unbleached woven fabrics of jute or of oth r text… (Rs 7.14 arba); Sacks and bags of jute (Rs 2.74 arba); Multiple or cabled yarn of jute or of othe textil… (Rs 77.2 crore); Sacks and bags, used for packing goods, of cotton (Rs 20.6 crore).
Are Nepal’s jute goods exports growing?
Exports were Rs 11.02 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 8.83 arba the year before, a change of +25%.
Does Nepal also import jute goods?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 6.80 arba in FY 2082/83, mainly from Bangladesh, India, China.
