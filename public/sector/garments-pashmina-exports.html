Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much readymade garments and pashmina does Nepal export?
Nepal exported readymade garments and pashmina worth Rs 13.07 arba in FY 2082/83, 4.1% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s readymade garments and pashmina?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were United States (Rs 2.84 arba), Germany (Rs 2.77 arba), United Kingdom (Rs 1.44 arba), France (Rs 77.8 crore).
What are Nepal’s biggest readymade garments and pashmina exports?
By value: Shawls, scarves, mufflers, mantillas, veil , etc,… (Rs 3.77 arba); Women's or girls' trousers, breeches, etc, of cot… (Rs 2.73 arba); Jerseys,pullovers,cardigans,waistcoats&sim lar ar… (Rs 1.84 arba); Women's or girls' dresses of cotton . (Rs 82.4 crore).
Are Nepal’s readymade garments and pashmina exports growing?
Exports were Rs 13.07 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 11.98 arba the year before, a change of +9%.
Does Nepal also import readymade garments and pashmina?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 44.34 arba in FY 2082/83, mainly from China, India, Indonesia.
