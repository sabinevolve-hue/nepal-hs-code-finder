Who buys it

India takes about 99% of these exports — a heavy dependence on one market. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: India · China · United States · Korea, Republic of · Sri Lanka · Italy.

When it ships

Exports peaked in Chaitra (Rs 1.16 arba) and were lowest in Bhadra (Rs 43.7 crore). Nepali months start mid-month: Shrawan is mid-July to mid-August.

Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much plywood, veneer and wood products does Nepal export?
Nepal exported plywood, veneer and wood products worth Rs 9.87 arba in FY 2082/83, 3.1% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s plywood, veneer and wood products?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 9.82 arba), China (Rs 1.9 crore), United States (Rs 1.1 crore), Korea, Republic of (Rs 66 lakh).
What are Nepal’s biggest plywood, veneer and wood products exports?

Are Nepal’s plywood, veneer and wood products exports growing?
Exports were Rs 9.87 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 9.43 arba the year before, a change of +5%.
Does Nepal also import plywood, veneer and wood products?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 5.34 arba in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, Indonesia.
