Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much juices, noodles and packaged food does Nepal export?
Nepal exported juices, noodles and packaged food worth Rs 12.11 arba in FY 2082/83, 3.8% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s juices, noodles and packaged food?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 10.03 arba), Australia (Rs 56.0 crore), United States (Rs 35.8 crore), Japan (Rs 26.1 crore).
What are Nepal’s biggest juices, noodles and packaged food exports?
By value: Mixtures of juices, unfermented, not containing a… (Rs 4.76 arba); Other pasta, nes (Rs 3.97 arba); Frozen orange juice, unfermented, not containing… (Rs 1.39 arba); Pineapple juice of a brix value <=20 (Rs 86.2 crore).
Are Nepal’s juices, noodles and packaged food exports growing?
Exports were Rs 12.11 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 10.30 arba the year before, a change of +17%.
Does Nepal also import juices, noodles and packaged food?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 5.42 arba in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, Malaysia, Korea, Republic of.
