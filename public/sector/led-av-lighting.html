Who supplies it

China is the largest supplier with about 59% of the sector’s imports; the top three together supply about 83%. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: China · India · Israel · Not_Specified · Indonesia · Qatar.

Country figures add up each product’s six largest sources, so small flows from other countries are not counted and the true shares can be slightly higher.

Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 30%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s LED displays, audio-visual and lighting import market?
Nepal imported LED displays, audio-visual and lighting worth Rs 15.79 arba in FY 2082/83, 0.8% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much LED displays, audio-visual and lighting does Nepal export?
Nepal exported LED displays, audio-visual and lighting worth Rs 5.5 crore in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s LED displays, audio-visual and lighting?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were China (Rs 9.32 arba), India (Rs 2.70 arba), Israel (Rs 1.02 arba), Not_Specified (Rs 55.6 crore).
