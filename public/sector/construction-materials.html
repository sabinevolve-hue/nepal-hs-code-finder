Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 30%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s iron, steel and construction materials import market?
Nepal imported iron, steel and construction materials worth Rs 1.82 kharba in FY 2082/83, 8.7% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much iron, steel and construction materials does Nepal export?
Nepal exported iron, steel and construction materials worth Rs 9.22 arba in FY 2082/83, 2.9% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s iron, steel and construction materials?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 1.48 kharba), China (Rs 28.41 arba), Japan (Rs 3.54 arba), Malaysia (Rs 1.19 arba).
