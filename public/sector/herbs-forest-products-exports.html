Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much herbs, essential oils and forest products does Nepal export?
Nepal exported herbs, essential oils and forest products worth Rs 9.79 arba in FY 2082/83, 3.1% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s herbs, essential oils and forest products?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 8.43 arba), Hong Kong (Rs 23.1 crore), Japan (Rs 18.3 crore), France (Rs 16.9 crore).
What are Nepal’s biggest herbs, essential oils and forest products exports?
By value: Cetechu of acacia (Kattha) (Rs 2.34 arba); Rosin and resin acids (Rs 2.23 arba); Other plants or parts, of a kind used in perfumer… (Rs 1.74 arba); Broom grass (Amriso) (Rs 86.7 crore).
Are Nepal’s herbs, essential oils and forest products exports growing?
Exports were Rs 9.79 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 9.27 arba the year before, a change of +6%.
Does Nepal also import herbs, essential oils and forest products?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 2.23 arba in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, Indonesia.
