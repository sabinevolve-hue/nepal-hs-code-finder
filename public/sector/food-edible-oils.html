Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 30%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s food and edible oils import market?
Nepal imported food and edible oils worth Rs 3.91 kharba in FY 2082/83, 18.6% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much food and edible oils does Nepal export?
Nepal exported food and edible oils worth Rs 1.65 kharba in FY 2082/83, 52.3% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s food and edible oils?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 1.33 kharba), Argentina (Rs 1.16 kharba), China (Rs 34.89 arba), Australia (Rs 19.04 arba).
What are the biggest food and edible oils imports?
By value: Crude soya-bean oil (Rs 1.33 kharba); Crude sunflower oil (Rs 21.77 arba); Others Paddy (Rs 17.85 arba); Low erucic acid rape or colza seeds (Rs 16.80 arba).
How much does the government collect on food and edible oils imports?
Customs collected Rs 52.14 arba on these imports in FY 2082/83, about Rs 13.3 for every Rs 100 of goods (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
