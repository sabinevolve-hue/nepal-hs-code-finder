Who supplies it

China is the largest supplier with about 55% of the sector’s imports; the top three together supply about 95%. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: China · India · Bangladesh · Thailand · New Zealand · Indonesia.

Where Nepal sells it

When it comes in

Imports peaked in Asoj (Rs 13.42 arba) and were lowest in Poush (Rs 7.51 arba). Nepali months start mid-month: Shrawan is mid-July to mid-August.

Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 20%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s textiles, garments and carpets import market?
Nepal imported textiles, garments and carpets worth Rs 1.23 kharba in FY 2082/83, 5.9% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much textiles, garments and carpets does Nepal export?
Nepal exported textiles, garments and carpets worth Rs 62.10 arba in FY 2082/83, 19.7% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s textiles, garments and carpets?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were China (Rs 68.20 arba), India (Rs 44.88 arba), Bangladesh (Rs 3.56 arba), Thailand (Rs 1.82 arba).
What are the biggest textiles, garments and carpets imports?
By value: Dyed kintted or crocheted fabrics of synth tic fi… (Rs 9.72 arba); Synthetic staple fibres, of polyesters, no carded… (Rs 8.26 arba); Raw Jute (Rs 5.88 arba); T-shirts, singlets & other vests of other extiles… (Rs 4.80 arba).
How much does the government collect on textiles, garments and carpets imports?
Customs collected Rs 30.10 arba on these imports in FY 2082/83, about Rs 24.5 for every Rs 100 of goods (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
