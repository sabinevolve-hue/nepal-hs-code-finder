Who supplies it

India is the largest supplier with about 74% of the sector’s imports; the top three together supply about 90%. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: India · Indonesia · Viet Nam · Guatemala · China · Bulgaria.

Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 30%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s tea, coffee, spices and herbs import market?
Nepal imported tea, coffee, spices and herbs worth Rs 10.74 arba in FY 2082/83, 0.5% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much tea, coffee, spices and herbs does Nepal export?
Nepal exported tea, coffee, spices and herbs worth Rs 20.82 arba in FY 2082/83, 6.6% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s tea, coffee, spices and herbs?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 7.98 arba), Indonesia (Rs 98.7 crore), Viet Nam (Rs 68.3 crore), Guatemala (Rs 31.5 crore).
What are the biggest tea, coffee, spices and herbs imports?
By value: Seeds of cumin neither crushed nor ground . (Rs 2.75 arba); Dried, neither, crushed or ground piper & capsicum (Rs 1.25 arba); Seeds of coriander neither crushed nor groud (Rs 99.1 crore); Dried pepper neither crushed nor ground (Rs 77.4 crore).
How much does the government collect on tea, coffee, spices and herbs imports?
Customs collected Rs 2.60 arba on these imports in FY 2082/83, about Rs 24.2 for every Rs 100 of goods (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
