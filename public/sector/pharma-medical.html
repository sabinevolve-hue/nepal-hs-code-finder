Who supplies it

India is the largest supplier with about 65% of the sector’s imports; the top three together supply about 76%. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: India · China · Germany · France · Korea, Republic of · United States.

Country figures add up each product’s six largest sources, so small flows from other countries are not counted and the true shares can be slightly higher.

Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 15%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s pharmaceuticals and medical equipment import market?
Nepal imported pharmaceuticals and medical equipment worth Rs 62.43 arba in FY 2082/83, 3.0% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much pharmaceuticals and medical equipment does Nepal export?
Nepal exported pharmaceuticals and medical equipment worth Rs 2.24 arba in FY 2082/83, 0.7% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s pharmaceuticals and medical equipment?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 40.66 arba), China (Rs 4.35 arba), Germany (Rs 2.28 arba), France (Rs 1.48 arba).
What are the biggest pharmaceuticals and medical equipment imports?
By value: Other - Medicaments put up in measured doss or in… (Rs 29.15 arba); Others instruments and appliances (Rs 6.12 arba); Vaccines for human medicine (Rs 3.39 arba); Medicaments of other antibiotics, for retail sale (Rs 2.01 arba).
How much does the government collect on pharmaceuticals and medical equipment imports?
Customs collected Rs 4.83 arba on these imports in FY 2082/83, about Rs 7.7 for every Rs 100 of goods (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
