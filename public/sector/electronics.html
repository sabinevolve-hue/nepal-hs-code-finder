Who supplies it

China is the largest supplier with about 58% of the sector’s imports; the top three together supply about 92%. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: China · India · Viet Nam · Not_Specified · United States · Thailand.

Country figures add up each product’s six largest sources, so small flows from other countries are not counted and the true shares can be slightly higher.

Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 30%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s electronics and appliances import market?
Nepal imported electronics and appliances worth Rs 1.77 kharba in FY 2082/83, 8.4% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much electronics and appliances does Nepal export?
Nepal exported electronics and appliances worth Rs 11.3 crore in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s electronics and appliances?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were China (Rs 1.02 kharba), India (Rs 57.66 arba), Viet Nam (Rs 2.72 arba), Not_Specified (Rs 2.01 arba).
