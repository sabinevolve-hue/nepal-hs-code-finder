Who supplies it

United Arab Emirates is the largest supplier with about 74% of the sector’s imports; the top three together supply about 93%. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: United Arab Emirates · Hong Kong · China · India · Qatar · Saudi Arabia.

Country figures add up each product’s six largest sources, so small flows from other countries are not counted and the true shares can be slightly higher.

When it comes in

Imports peaked in Magh (Rs 12.18 arba) and were lowest in Asar (Rs 1.89 arba). Nepali months start mid-month: Shrawan is mid-July to mid-August.

Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 30%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s gold, silver and jewellery import market?
Nepal imported gold, silver and jewellery worth Rs 64.67 arba in FY 2082/83, 3.1% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much gold, silver and jewellery does Nepal export?
Nepal exported gold, silver and jewellery worth Rs 48.4 crore in FY 2082/83, 0.2% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s gold, silver and jewellery?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were United Arab Emirates (Rs 47.88 arba), Hong Kong (Rs 9.43 arba), China (Rs 2.74 arba), India (Rs 1.89 arba).
