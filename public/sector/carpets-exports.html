Selling abroad

Exports from Nepal need an EXIM code, a commercial invoice and packing list, a certificate of origin and, for food and plant products, the relevant quality or plant-health certificate. Goods going to SAARC countries can claim the buyer’s lower SAFTA rate with a SAFTA certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How much carpets does Nepal export?
Nepal exported carpets worth Rs 11.79 arba in FY 2082/83, 3.7% of the country’s total exports, according to the Department of Customs.
Which countries buy Nepal’s carpets?
The largest buyers in FY 2082/83 were United States (Rs 6.86 arba), India (Rs 1.24 arba), Germany (Rs 56.2 crore), France (Rs 49.3 crore).
What are Nepal’s biggest carpets exports?
By value: Carpets and other textile floor coverings, of woo… (Rs 10.39 arba); Carpets and floor covering of felt (Jamoth) (Rs 83.7 crore); carpet & other textile floor coverings of other m… (Rs 29.1 crore); Of wool or fine animal hair (Rs 11.8 crore).
Are Nepal’s carpets exports growing?
Exports were Rs 11.79 arba in FY 2082/83 against Rs 12.25 arba the year before, a change of -4%.
Does Nepal also import carpets?
Yes. Nepal imported goods under the same HS codes worth Rs 70.6 crore in FY 2082/83, mainly from China, India, Thailand.
