Who supplies it

India is the largest supplier with about 58% of the sector’s imports; the top three together supply about 89%. Open a country for everything Nepal trades with it: India · China · Canada · Italy · Not_Specified · Germany.

Country figures add up each product’s six largest sources, so small flows from other countries are not counted and the true shares can be slightly higher.

Duty and landed cost

General customs duty on the main products in this sector runs from 5% to 15%. VAT of 13% applies on top unless the product is exempt, and goods from SAARC countries can qualify for the lower SAFTA rate with a certificate of origin.

Quick answers

How big is Nepal’s machinery and industrial equipment import market?
Nepal imported machinery and industrial equipment worth Rs 1.14 kharba in FY 2082/83, 5.4% of the country’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much machinery and industrial equipment does Nepal export?
Nepal exported machinery and industrial equipment worth Rs 1.12 arba in FY 2082/83, 0.4% of total exports.
Which countries supply Nepal’s machinery and industrial equipment?
The largest suppliers in FY 2082/83 were India (Rs 65.91 arba), China (Rs 29.91 arba), Canada (Rs 5.64 arba), Italy (Rs 2.85 arba).

How much does the government collect on machinery and industrial equipment imports?
