Customs duty on Machine for production of Medicines in Nepal

Machine for production of Medicines is classified under HS code 8479.89.50 (chapter 84, Nuclear reactors, boilers, machinery and mechanical appliances; parts thereof). It attracts 5% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
