Who sells it to Nepal

For HS 5407.20.00 (Woven fabrics obtained from strip or the like), Nepal’s biggest suppliers by import value in FY 2081/82 were China (73.1%), India (13.1%), Saudi Arabia (12.3%). Open a country to see everything Nepal buys from it.
