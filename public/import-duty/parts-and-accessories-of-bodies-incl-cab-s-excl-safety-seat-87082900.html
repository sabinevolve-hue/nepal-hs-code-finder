Customs duty on Parts and accessories of bodies (incl cab s , (excl safety seat belt) in Nepal

Parts and accessories of bodies (incl cab s , (excl safety seat belt) is classified under HS code 8708.29.00 (chapter 87, Vehicles other than railway or tramway rolling-stock, and parts and accessories thereof). It attracts 20% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
