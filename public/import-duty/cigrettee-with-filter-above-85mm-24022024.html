Customs duty on Cigrettee with filter above 85MM in Nepal

Cigrettee with filter above 85MM is classified under HS code 2402.20.24 (chapter 24, Tobacco and manufactured tobacco substitutes; products, whether or not containing nicotine, intended for inhalation without combustion; other nicotine containing products intended for the intake of nicotine into the human body). It attracts Per thousand sticks Rs. 6000 customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
