Customs duty on Printed woven fabrics of synthetic filamen yarn, >=85% nylon in Nepal

Printed woven fabrics of synthetic filamen yarn, >=85% nylon is classified under HS code 5407.44.00 (chapter 54, Man-made filaments; strip and the like of man-made textile materials). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

The chip is customs duty. “Total” under it is everything charged at the border (customs duty, excise, VAT and other fees) as a share of CIF value, for goods from other countries and from India. Declaring a lower-duty line that does not match the goods leads to reclassification, the duty difference and a fine. When unsure, ask the Department of Customs for an advance ruling.

Nepal’s imports of Printed woven fabrics of synthetic filamen yarn, >=85% nylon

Nepal imported about Rs 1.26 Crore of Printed woven fabrics of synthetic filamen yarn, >=85% nylon in FY 2082/83, mainly from Indonesia, India, China.
