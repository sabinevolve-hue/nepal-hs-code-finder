Antenna import duty in Nepal

How much customs duty and tax you pay to import antenna (Satellite Dish) into Nepal — the rate, the HS code it falls under, and a worked landed-cost example.

antenna नेपालमा आयात गर्दा साधारण भन्सार दर 10% र त्यसमाथि १३% भ्याट लाग्छ।

10%Customs duty
13%VAT (on value + duty)
Rs 1,24,300Landed cost on Rs 1,00,000

On a Rs 1,00,000 import you would pay about Rs 10,000 customs duty and Rs 14,300 VAT — landing at Rs 1,24,300. Try your own value →

Which HS code antenna falls under

HS codeDescriptionGeneralSAARC
8529.10.00Aerials and aerial reflectors of all kinds; parts suitable f10%6%
Importing from India or another SAARC country? The SAARC column may be lower under SAFTA with a valid Certificate of Origin. From China, the US or elsewhere the general rate applies in full.

About this page’s data

Duty rates: Nepal Customs Tariff 2026/27 (general and India/SAARC rates). VAT is 13% on (customs value + duty). Actual duty depends on the exact HS sub-heading, the declared customs value and any excise; confirm with the Department of Customs before you import.
