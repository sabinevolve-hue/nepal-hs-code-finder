Customs duty on Gin and Geneva of an alcoholic strength 15UP (48.5%) in Nepal

Gin and Geneva of an alcoholic strength 15UP (48.5%) is classified under HS code 2208.50.91 (chapter 22, Beverages, spirits and vinegar). It attracts Per ltr. Rs. 2000 or 100 Percent whichever is higher customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
