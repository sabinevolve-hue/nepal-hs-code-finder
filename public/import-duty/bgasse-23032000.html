Customs duty on Bgasse in Nepal

Bgasse is classified under HS code 2303.20.00 (chapter 23, Residues and waste from the food industries; prepared animal fodder). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 6% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

6%India & SAARC (SAFTA)

Importing from India or SAARC under SAFTA (with a certificate of origin) cuts the duty from 10% to 6% — about Rs 4,520 less on this Rs 100,000 example, once the VAT on the saved duty is counted. How SAFTA & the certificate of origin work →

Same family: every line under heading 2303

Residues of starch manufacture and similar residues, beet-pulp, bagasse and other waste of sugar manufacture, brewing or distilling dregs and waste, whether or not in the form of pellets — 3 lines. The bar is the general customs duty; the text is what decides between them, in the tariff’s own words.

Ways the duty can be lower, and the condition for each

  • Origin: made in India or another SAARC country, with a certificate of origin, this line pays 6% instead of 10% under SAFTA. How SAFTA works

The HS code is set by what the goods are, not chosen. Declaring a lower-duty line that does not match the goods leads to reclassification, the duty difference, a fine and possible seizure. If you are unsure, ask for an advance ruling from the Department of Customs.
