Customs duty on Women's or girls' dresses of wool or fine nimal hair in Nepal

Women's or girls' dresses of wool or fine nimal hair is classified under HS code 6204.41.00 (chapter 62, Articles of apparel and clothing accessories, not knitted or crocheted). It attracts 20% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Nepal’s imports of Women's or girls' dresses of wool or fine nimal hair

Nepal imported about Rs 43.7 Lakh of Women's or girls' dresses of wool or fine nimal hair in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, United Kingdom.
