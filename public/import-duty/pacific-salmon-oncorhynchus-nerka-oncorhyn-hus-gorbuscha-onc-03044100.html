Customs duty on Pacific salmon (Oncorhynchus nerka,Oncorhyn hus gorbuscha, Oncorhynchus keta, Onc in Nepal

Pacific salmon (Oncorhynchus nerka,Oncorhyn hus gorbuscha, Oncorhynchus keta, Onc is classified under HS code 0304.41.00 (chapter 03, Fish and crustaceans, molluscs and other acquatic invertebrates). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 9% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Import permits for this product

Beyond customs duty, this is a controlled import. On top of the universal requirements (an EXIM code, a payment route and a customs declaration), it is likely to need: Animal Quarantine (Dept. of Livestock Services) — Veterinary import permit + animal health / quarantine certificate; Food Technology & Quality Control (DFTQC) — Food-import recommendation + laboratory testing / quality certificate.
