Customs duty on Other Bicycle & cycle (including trycycle) not motorized in Nepal

Other Bicycle & cycle (including trycycle) not motorized is classified under HS code 8712.00.90 (chapter 87, Vehicles other than railway or tramway rolling-stock, and parts and accessories thereof). It attracts 5% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Nepal’s imports of Other Bicycle & cycle (including trycycle) not motorized

Nepal imported about Rs 2.10 Arab of Other Bicycle & cycle (including trycycle) not motorized in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, Indonesia.
