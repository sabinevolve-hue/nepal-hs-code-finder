Customs duty on Petrol in Nepal

Petrol is classified under HS code 2710.12.10 (chapter 27, Mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation; bituminous substances; mineral waxes). It attracts Per KL Rs. 25200 customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
