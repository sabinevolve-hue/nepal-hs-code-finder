Customs duty on Valve and stent implanted in the heart in Nepal

Valve and stent implanted in the heart is classified under HS code 9021.90.30 (chapter 90, Optical, photographic, cinematographic, measuring, checking, precision, medical or surgical instruments and apparatus; parts and accessories thereof). It is imported duty-free (0% customs duty), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value.
