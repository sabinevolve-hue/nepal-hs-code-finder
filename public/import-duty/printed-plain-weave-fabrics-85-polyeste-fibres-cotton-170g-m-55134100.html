Customs duty on Printed plain weave fabrics, <85% polyeste fibres + cotton, =<170g/m2 in Nepal

Printed plain weave fabrics, <85% polyeste fibres + cotton, =<170g/m2 is classified under HS code 5513.41.00 (chapter 55, Man-made staple fibres). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
