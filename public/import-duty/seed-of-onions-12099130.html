Customs duty on Seed of Onions in Nepal

Seed of Onions is classified under HS code 1209.91.30 (chapter 12, Oil seeds and oleaginous fruits; miscellaneous grains, seeds and fruit; industrial or medicinal plants; straw and fodder). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 6% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Closest lines with lower duty, and the condition for each

The chip is customs duty. “Total” under it is everything charged at the border (customs duty, excise, VAT and other fees) as a share of CIF value, for goods from other countries and from India. Declaring a lower-duty line that does not match the goods leads to reclassification, the duty difference and a fine. When unsure, ask the Department of Customs for an advance ruling.

Nepal’s imports of Seed of Onions

Nepal imported about Rs 91.1 Lakh of Seed of Onions in FY 2082/83, mainly from South Africa, India, Japan.
