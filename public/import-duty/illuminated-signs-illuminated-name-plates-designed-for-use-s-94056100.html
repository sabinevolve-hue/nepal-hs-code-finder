Customs duty on Illuminated signs, illuminated name-plates Designed for use solely with (LED) light in Nepal

Illuminated signs, illuminated name-plates Designed for use solely with (LED) light is classified under HS code 9405.61.00 (chapter 94, Furniture; bedding, mattresses, mattress supports, cushions and similar stuffed furnishings; luminaires and lighting fittings, not elsewhere specified or included; illuminated signs, illuminated name-plates and the like; prefabricated buildings). It attracts 20% customs duty (general), or 6% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
