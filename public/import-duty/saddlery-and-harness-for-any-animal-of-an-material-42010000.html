Customs duty on Saddlery and harness for any animal, of an material in Nepal

Saddlery and harness for any animal, of an material is classified under HS code 4201.00.00 (chapter 42, Articles of leather; saddlery and harness; travel goods, handbags and similar containers; articles of animal gut (other than silk-worm gut)). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), or 7.25% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
