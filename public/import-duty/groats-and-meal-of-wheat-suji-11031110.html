Customs duty on Groats and meal of wheat (Suji) in Nepal

Groats and meal of wheat (Suji) is classified under HS code 1103.11.10 (chapter 11, Products of the milling industry; malt; starches; inulin; wheat gluten). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 9% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

9%India & SAARC (SAFTA)

Landed cost — worked example

For a shipment with a CIF value of Rs 100,000 (cost + insurance + freight to the Nepal border), the customs duty and VAT work out as:

Importing from India or SAARC under SAFTA (with a certificate of origin) cuts the duty from 10% to 9% — about Rs 1,130 less on this Rs 100,000 example, once the VAT on the saved duty is counted. How SAFTA & the certificate of origin work →

Import permits for this product

Beyond customs duty, this is a controlled import. On top of the universal requirements (an EXIM code, a payment route and a customs declaration), it is likely to need: Plant Quarantine — NPPO Nepal — Import permit + a phytosanitary certificate from the exporting country; Food Technology & Quality Control (DFTQC) — Food-import recommendation + laboratory testing / quality certificate.
