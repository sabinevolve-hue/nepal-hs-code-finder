Customs duty on Boards equipped with two or more appara tus of 8535 or 8536, voltage >1000 V in Nepal

Boards equipped with two or more appara tus of 8535 or 8536, voltage >1000 V is classified under HS code 8537.20.00 (chapter 85, Electrical machinery and equipment and parts thereof; sound recorders and reproducers, television image and sound recorders and reproducers, and parts and accessories of such articles). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 6% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
