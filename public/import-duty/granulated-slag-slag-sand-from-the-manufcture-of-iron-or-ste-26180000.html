Customs duty on Granulated slag (slag sand) from the manufcture of iron or steel in Nepal

Granulated slag (slag sand) from the manufcture of iron or steel is classified under HS code 2618.00.00 (chapter 26, Ores, slag and ash). It attracts Per MT Rs. 1000 customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Per MT Rs. 1000General duty
Per MT Rs. 1000India & SAARC (SAFTA)
13%VAT
MTCustoms unit

Duty on this product

This product carries a specific duty — Per MT Rs. 1000 — rather than a flat percentage, so the amount depends on quantity. Enter your shipment in the calculator for the exact figure; 13% VAT still applies on the CIF value plus duty.

Nepal’s imports of Granulated slag (slag sand) from the manufcture of iron or steel

Nepal imported about Rs 16.15 Crore of Granulated slag (slag sand) from the manufcture of iron or steel in FY 2082/83, mainly from India.
