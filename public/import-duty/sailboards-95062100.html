Customs duty on Sailboards in Nepal

Sailboards is classified under HS code 9506.21.00 (chapter 95, Toys, games and sports requisites; parts and accessories thereof). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 5% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

5%India & SAARC (SAFTA)
13%VAT
