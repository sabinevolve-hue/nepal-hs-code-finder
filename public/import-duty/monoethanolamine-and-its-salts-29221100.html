Customs duty on Monoethanolamine and its salts in Nepal

Monoethanolamine and its salts is classified under HS code 2922.11.00 (chapter 29, Organic Chemicals). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 6% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

6%India & SAARC (SAFTA)
