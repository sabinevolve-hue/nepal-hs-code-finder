Customs duty on Tubes, pipes etc, with fittings, reinfo rc d or combined with other material in Nepal

Tubes, pipes etc, with fittings, reinfo rc d or combined with other material is classified under HS code 4009.42.00 (chapter 40, Rubber and articles thereof). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), or 7.25% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

7.25%India & SAARC (SAFTA)

Landed cost — worked example

For a shipment with a CIF value of Rs 100,000 (cost + insurance + freight to the Nepal border), the customs duty and VAT work out as:
