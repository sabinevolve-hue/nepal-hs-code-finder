Customs duty on Dyed woven cotton fabrics, with >=85% cott n, >=200g/m2 by weight, nes in Nepal

Dyed woven cotton fabrics, with >=85% cott n, >=200g/m2 by weight, nes is classified under HS code 5209.39.00 (chapter 52, Cotton). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), or 5% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
