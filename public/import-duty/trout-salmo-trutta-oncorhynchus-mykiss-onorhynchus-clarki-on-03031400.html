Customs duty on Trout (Salmo trutta, Oncorhynchus mykiss,Onorhynchus clarki, Oncorhynchus aguabo in Nepal

Trout (Salmo trutta, Oncorhynchus mykiss,Onorhynchus clarki, Oncorhynchus aguabo is classified under HS code 0303.14.00 (chapter 03, Fish and crustaceans, molluscs and other acquatic invertebrates). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 9% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

9%India & SAARC (SAFTA)

Ways the duty can be lower, and the condition for each

  • Origin: made in India or another SAARC country, with a certificate of origin, this line pays 9% instead of 10% under SAFTA. How SAFTA works

The HS code is set by what the goods are, not chosen. Declaring a lower-duty line that does not match the goods leads to reclassification, the duty difference, a fine and possible seizure. If you are unsure, ask for an advance ruling from the Department of Customs.
