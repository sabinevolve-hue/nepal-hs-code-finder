Customs duty on Unbleached woven fabrics of jute or of oth r textile bast fibre of heading 5303 in Nepal

Unbleached woven fabrics of jute or of oth r textile bast fibre of heading 5303 is classified under HS code 5310.10.00 (chapter 53, Other vegetable textile fibres; paper yarn and woven fabrics of paper yarn). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
