Customs duty on Other garments, women's or girls of manmad fibres in Nepal

Other garments, women's or girls of manmad fibres is classified under HS code 6211.43.90 (chapter 62, Articles of apparel and clothing accessories, not knitted or crocheted). It attracts 20% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
