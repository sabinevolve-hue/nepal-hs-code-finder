Customs duty on Automatic pianos, harpsichords and other k yboard instruments, nes in Nepal

Automatic pianos, harpsichords and other k yboard instruments, nes is classified under HS code 9201.90.00 (chapter 92, Musical instruments; parts and accessories of such articles). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), or 5% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Ways the duty can be lower, and the condition for each

  • Origin: made in India or another SAARC country, with a certificate of origin, this line pays 5% instead of 15% under SAFTA. How SAFTA works

The HS code is set by what the goods are, not chosen. Declaring a lower-duty line that does not match the goods leads to reclassification, the duty difference, a fine and possible seizure. If you are unsure, ask for an advance ruling from the Department of Customs.

Nepal’s imports of Automatic pianos, harpsichords and other k yboard instruments, nes

Nepal imported about Rs 1.64 Crore of Automatic pianos, harpsichords and other k yboard instruments, nes in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, Indonesia.
