Customs duty on Beer made from malt ( upto 5% alcohol) in Nepal

Beer made from malt ( upto 5% alcohol) is classified under HS code 2203.00.10 (chapter 22, Beverages, spirits and vinegar). It attracts Per ltr. Rs. 200 or 80 Percent whichever is higher customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Duty on this product

This product carries a specific duty — Per ltr. Rs. 200 or 80 Percent whichever is higher — rather than a flat percentage, so the amount depends on quantity. Enter your shipment in the calculator for the exact figure; 13% VAT still applies on the CIF value plus duty.

Nepal’s imports of Beer made from malt ( upto 5% alcohol)

Nepal imported about Rs 3.60 Crore of Beer made from malt ( upto 5% alcohol) in FY 2082/83, mainly from China, Malaysia, Mexico.
