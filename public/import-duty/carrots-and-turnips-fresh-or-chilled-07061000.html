Customs duty on Carrots and turnips, fresh or chilled in Nepal

Carrots and turnips, fresh or chilled is classified under HS code 0706.10.00 (chapter 07, Edible Vegetables and certain Roots and Tubers). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 9% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

10%General duty
9%India & SAARC (SAFTA)
13%VAT
kgCustoms unit

Landed cost — worked example

For a shipment with a CIF value of Rs 100,000 (cost + insurance + freight to the Nepal border), the customs duty and VAT work out as:

Importing from India or SAARC under SAFTA (with a certificate of origin) cuts the duty from 10% to 9% — about Rs 1,130 less on this Rs 100,000 example, once the VAT on the saved duty is counted. How SAFTA & the certificate of origin work →

Nepal’s imports of Carrots and turnips, fresh or chilled

Nepal imported about Rs 73.08 Crore of Carrots and turnips, fresh or chilled in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, Saudi Arabia.
