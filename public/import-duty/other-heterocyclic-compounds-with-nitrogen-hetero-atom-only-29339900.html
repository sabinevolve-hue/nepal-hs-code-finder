Customs duty on Other - Heterocyclic compounds with nitrogen hetero-atom only nes in Nepal

Other - Heterocyclic compounds with nitrogen hetero-atom only nes is classified under HS code 2933.99.00 (chapter 29, Organic Chemicals). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 6% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

6%India & SAARC (SAFTA)
