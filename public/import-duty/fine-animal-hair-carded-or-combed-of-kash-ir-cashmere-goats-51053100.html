Customs duty on Fine animal hair, carded or combed of Kash ir (cashmere) goats in Nepal

Fine animal hair, carded or combed of Kash ir (cashmere) goats is classified under HS code 5105.31.00 (chapter 51, Wool, fine or coarse animal hair; horsehair yarn and woven fabric). It attracts 5% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
