Customs duty on Woven fabric obtained from high tenacity y rn of nylon or other polymides or of in Nepal

Woven fabric obtained from high tenacity y rn of nylon or other polymides or of is classified under HS code 5407.10.00 (chapter 54, Man-made filaments; strip and the like of man-made textile materials). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Nepal’s imports of Woven fabric obtained from high tenacity y rn of nylon or other polymides or of

Nepal imported about Rs 30.96 Crore of Woven fabric obtained from high tenacity y rn of nylon or other polymides or of in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, Taiwan, Province of China.
