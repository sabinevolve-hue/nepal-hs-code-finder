Customs duty on Other unsweetened waters;ice and snow in Nepal

Other unsweetened waters;ice and snow is classified under HS code 2201.90.00 (chapter 22, Beverages, spirits and vinegar). It attracts 30% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
