Customs duty on Turnings, shavings, chips,milling waste,sa dust,filings,trimming of iron or st in Nepal

Turnings, shavings, chips,milling waste,sa dust,filings,trimming of iron or st is classified under HS code 7204.41.00 (chapter 72, Iron and steel). It attracts 5% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

The chip is customs duty. “Total” under it is everything charged at the border (customs duty, excise, VAT and other fees) as a share of CIF value, for goods from other countries and from India. Your line already has the lowest general duty in its family. Declaring a lower-duty line that does not match the goods leads to reclassification, the duty difference and a fine. When unsure, ask the Department of Customs for an advance ruling.
