Customs duty on Boring-milling machines for metal, numeric lly controlled, nes in Nepal

Boring-milling machines for metal, numeric lly controlled, nes is classified under HS code 8459.31.00 (chapter 84, Nuclear reactors, boilers, machinery and mechanical appliances; parts thereof). It attracts 5% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
