Customs duty on Buttons of plastics, not covered with text le material in Nepal

Buttons of plastics, not covered with text le material is classified under HS code 9606.21.00 (chapter 96, Miscellaneous manufactured articles). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), or 7.25% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

7.25%India & SAARC (SAFTA)

Landed cost — worked example

For a shipment with a CIF value of Rs 100,000 (cost + insurance + freight to the Nepal border), the customs duty and VAT work out as:

Importing from India or SAARC under SAFTA (with a certificate of origin) cuts the duty from 15% to 7.25% — about Rs 8,758 less on this Rs 100,000 example, once the VAT on the saved duty is counted. How SAFTA & the certificate of origin work →
