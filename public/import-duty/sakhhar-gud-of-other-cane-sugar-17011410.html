Customs duty on Sakhhar (Gud) of other cane sugar in Nepal

Sakhhar (Gud) of other cane sugar is classified under HS code 1701.14.10 (chapter 17, Sugars and sugar confectionery). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Beyond customs duty, this is a controlled import.
