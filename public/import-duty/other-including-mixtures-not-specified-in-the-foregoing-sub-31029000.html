Customs duty on Other including mixtures not specified in the foregoing Sub-headings in Nepal

Other including mixtures not specified in the foregoing Sub-headings is classified under HS code 3102.90.00 (chapter 31, Fertilisers). It is imported duty-free (0% customs duty), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value.
