Customs duty on Made up fishing nets of man-made textile m terials in Nepal

Made up fishing nets of man-made textile m terials is classified under HS code 5608.11.00 (chapter 56, Wadding, felt and nonwovens; special yarns; twine, cordage, ropes and cables and articles thereof). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), or 7.25% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
