Customs duty on Whiskies of an alcoholic strength 30UP (39.94%) in Nepal

Whiskies of an alcoholic strength 30UP (39.94%) is classified under HS code 2208.30.93 (chapter 22, Beverages, spirits and vinegar). It attracts Per ltr. Rs. 2000 or 100 Percent whichever is higher customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
