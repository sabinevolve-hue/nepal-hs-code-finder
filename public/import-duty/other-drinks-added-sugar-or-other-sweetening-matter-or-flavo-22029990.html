Customs duty on Other drinks (added sugar or other sweetening matter or flavoured) in Nepal

Other drinks (added sugar or other sweetening matter or flavoured) is classified under HS code 2202.99.90 (chapter 22, Beverages, spirits and vinegar). It attracts 30% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
