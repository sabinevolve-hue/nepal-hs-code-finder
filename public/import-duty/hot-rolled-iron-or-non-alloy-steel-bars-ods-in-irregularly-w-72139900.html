Customs duty on Hot-rolled iron or non-alloy steel bars & ods, in irregularly wound coils, nes in Nepal

Hot-rolled iron or non-alloy steel bars & ods, in irregularly wound coils, nes is classified under HS code 7213.99.00 (chapter 72, Iron and steel). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
