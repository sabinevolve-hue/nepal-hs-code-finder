Customs duty on Packing or wrapping machinery, (incl heat - hrink wrapping machinery)nes in Nepal

Packing or wrapping machinery, (incl heat - hrink wrapping machinery)nes is classified under HS code 8422.40.00 (chapter 84, Nuclear reactors, boilers, machinery and mechanical appliances; parts thereof). It attracts 5% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
