Customs duty on Anhydrous Ethanol <>99% alcohol> in Nepal

Anhydrous Ethanol <>99% alcohol> is classified under HS code 2207.10.40 (chapter 22, Beverages, spirits and vinegar). It attracts Per ltr. Rs. 60 or 30 Percent whichever is higher customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
