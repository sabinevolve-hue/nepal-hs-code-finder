Customs duty on Of an output not exceeding 50 W in Nepal

Of an output not exceeding 50 W is classified under HS code 8501.71.00 (chapter 85, Electrical machinery and equipment and parts thereof; sound recorders and reproducers, television image and sound recorders and reproducers, and parts and accessories of such articles). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), or 7.25% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
