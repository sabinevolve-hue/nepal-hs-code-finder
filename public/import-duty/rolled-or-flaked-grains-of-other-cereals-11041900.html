Customs duty on Rolled or flaked grains of other cereals in Nepal

Rolled or flaked grains of other cereals is classified under HS code 1104.19.00 (chapter 11, Products of the milling industry; malt; starches; inulin; wheat gluten). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), or 9% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

Landed cost — worked example

For a shipment with a CIF value of Rs 100,000 (cost + insurance + freight to the Nepal border), the customs duty and VAT work out as:

Importing from India or SAARC under SAFTA (with a certificate of origin) cuts the duty from 10% to 9% — about Rs 1,130 less on this Rs 100,000 example, once the VAT on the saved duty is counted. How SAFTA & the certificate of origin work →

Import permits for this product

Beyond customs duty, this is a controlled import. On top of the universal requirements (an EXIM code, a payment route and a customs declaration), it is likely to need: Plant Quarantine — NPPO Nepal — Import permit + a phytosanitary certificate from the exporting country; Food Technology & Quality Control (DFTQC) — Food-import recommendation + laboratory testing / quality certificate. Nepal’s imports of Rolled or flaked grains of other cereals

Nepal imported about Rs 1.60 Crore of Rolled or flaked grains of other cereals in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, Poland.
