Customs duty on Unfrozen orange juice, unfermented, not containing added spirit in Nepal

Unfrozen orange juice, unfermented, not containing added spirit is classified under HS code 2009.19.00 (chapter 20, Preparations of vegetables, fruit, nuts or other parts of plants). It attracts Per ltr Rs. 60 customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
