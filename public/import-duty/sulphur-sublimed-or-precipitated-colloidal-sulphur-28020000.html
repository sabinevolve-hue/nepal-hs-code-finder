Customs duty on Sulphur, sublimed or precipitated; colloidal sulphur in Nepal

Sulphur, sublimed or precipitated; colloidal sulphur is classified under HS code 2802.00.00 (chapter 28, Inorganic chemicals; organic or inorganic compounds of precious metals, of rare-earth metals, of radioactive elements or of isotopes). It attracts 5% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
