Customs duty on X-ray tubes in Nepal

X-ray tubes is classified under HS code 9022.30.00 (chapter 90, Optical, photographic, cinematographic, measuring, checking, precision, medical or surgical instruments and apparatus; parts and accessories thereof). It attracts 5% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

5%India & SAARC (SAFTA)

Nepal’s imports of X-ray tubes

Nepal imported about Rs 22.77 Crore of X-ray tubes in FY 2082/83, mainly from Germany, United States, Japan.
