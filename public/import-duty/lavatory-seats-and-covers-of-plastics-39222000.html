Customs duty on Lavatory seats and covers of plastics in Nepal

Lavatory seats and covers of plastics is classified under HS code 3922.20.00 (chapter 39, Plastics and articles thereof). It attracts 30% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
