Customs duty on Black tea(fermented)&partly fermented,whether or not flavoured,in packings of <=3kg in Nepal

Black tea(fermented)&partly fermented,whether or not flavoured,in packings of <=3kg is classified under HS code 0902.30.00 (chapter 09, Coffee, tea, mate and spices). It attracts 30% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
