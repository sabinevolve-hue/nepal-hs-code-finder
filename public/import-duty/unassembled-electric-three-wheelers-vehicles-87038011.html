Customs duty on Unassembled Electric three wheelers vehicles in Nepal

Unassembled Electric three wheelers vehicles is classified under HS code 8703.80.11 (chapter 87, Vehicles other than railway or tramway rolling-stock, and parts and accessories thereof). It attracts 10% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
