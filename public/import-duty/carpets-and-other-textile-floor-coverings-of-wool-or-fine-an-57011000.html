Customs duty on Carpets and other textile floor coverings, of wool or fine animal hair, knotted in Nepal

Carpets and other textile floor coverings, of wool or fine animal hair, knotted is classified under HS code 5701.10.00 (chapter 57, Carpets and other textile floor coverings). It attracts 30% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
