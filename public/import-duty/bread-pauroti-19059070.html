Customs duty on Bread (Pauroti) in Nepal

Bread (Pauroti) is classified under HS code 1905.90.70 (chapter 19, Preparations of cereals, flour, starch or milk; pastrycooks' products). It attracts 30% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
