Customs duty on Hat-forms, hat bodies and hoods of felt; p ateaux and manchons of felt in Nepal

Hat-forms, hat bodies and hoods of felt; p ateaux and manchons of felt is classified under HS code 6501.00.00 (chapter 65, Headgear and parts thereof). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), or 7.25% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
