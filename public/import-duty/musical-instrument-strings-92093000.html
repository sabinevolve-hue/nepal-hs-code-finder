Customs duty on Musical instrument strings in Nepal

Musical instrument strings is classified under HS code 9209.30.00 (chapter 92, Musical instruments; parts and accessories of such articles). It attracts 5% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
