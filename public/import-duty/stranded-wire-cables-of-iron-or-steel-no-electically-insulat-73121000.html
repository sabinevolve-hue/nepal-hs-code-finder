Customs duty on Stranded wire, cables of iron or steel, no electically insulated in Nepal

Stranded wire, cables of iron or steel, no electically insulated is classified under HS code 7312.10.00 (chapter 73, Articles of iron and steel). It attracts 30% customs duty (general), or 7.25% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
