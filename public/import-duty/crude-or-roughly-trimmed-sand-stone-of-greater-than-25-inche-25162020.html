Customs duty on Crude or roughly trimmed sand stone of greater than 25 inches in size in Nepal

Crude or roughly trimmed sand stone of greater than 25 inches in size is classified under HS code 2516.20.20 (chapter 25, Salt; sulphur; earths and stone; plastering materials, lime and cement). It attracts 20% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
