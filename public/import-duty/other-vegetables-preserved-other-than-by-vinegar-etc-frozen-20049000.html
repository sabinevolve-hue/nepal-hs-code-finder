Customs duty on Other vegetables preserved other than by vinegar, etc, frozen,(excl. prod's of 20.06 in Nepal

Other vegetables preserved other than by vinegar, etc, frozen,(excl. prod's of 20.06 is classified under HS code 2004.90.00 (chapter 20, Preparations of vegetables, fruit, nuts or other parts of plants). It attracts 20% customs duty (general), or 18.5% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
