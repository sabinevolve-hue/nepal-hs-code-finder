Customs duty on Face Cream in Nepal

Face Cream is classified under HS code 3304.99.10 (chapter 33, Essential oils and resinoids; perfumery, cosmetic or toilet preparations). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
