Customs duty on Other Whiskies in Nepal

Other Whiskies is classified under HS code 2208.30.99 (chapter 22, Beverages, spirits and vinegar). It attracts Per ltr. Rs. 2000 or 100 Percent whichever is higher customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
