Customs duty on Cranberries (Vaccinium macrocarpon, Vaccinium oxycoccos); lingonberries (Vaccinium vi in Nepal

Cranberries (Vaccinium macrocarpon, Vaccinium oxycoccos); lingonberries (Vaccinium vi is classified under HS code 2008.93.00 (chapter 20, Preparations of vegetables, fruit, nuts or other parts of plants). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.
