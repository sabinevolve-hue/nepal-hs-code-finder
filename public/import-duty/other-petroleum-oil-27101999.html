Customs duty on Other petroleum Oil in Nepal

Other petroleum Oil is classified under HS code 2710.19.99 (chapter 27, Mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation; bituminous substances; mineral waxes). It attracts 30% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

30%General duty
30%India & SAARC (SAFTA)
13%VAT

Nepal’s imports of Other petroleum Oil

Nepal imported about Rs 1.40 Arab of Other petroleum Oil in FY 2082/83, mainly from India, China, United Kingdom.
