Customs duty on Preparations with a basis of extract, essences or concentrates or with a basis of in Nepal

Preparations with a basis of extract, essences or concentrates or with a basis of is classified under HS code 2101.12.00 (chapter 21, Miscellaneous edible preparations). It attracts 30% customs duty (general), or 11.25% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

11.25%India & SAARC (SAFTA)

From India or another SAARC country, with a certificate of origin, your line pays 11.25% instead of 30%. How SAFTA works. Your line already has the lowest general duty in its family. Declaring a lower-duty line that does not match the goods leads to reclassification, the duty difference and a fine. When unsure, ask the Department of Customs for an advance ruling.
