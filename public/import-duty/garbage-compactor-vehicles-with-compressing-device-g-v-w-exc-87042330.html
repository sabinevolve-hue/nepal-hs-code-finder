Customs duty on Garbage Compactor vehicles with compressing Device g.v.w. exceeding 20 tonnes in Nepal

Garbage Compactor vehicles with compressing Device g.v.w. exceeding 20 tonnes is classified under HS code 8704.23.30 (chapter 87, Vehicles other than railway or tramway rolling-stock, and parts and accessories thereof). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

13%VAT
