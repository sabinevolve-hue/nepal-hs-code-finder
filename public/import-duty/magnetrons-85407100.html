Customs duty on Magnetrons in Nepal

Magnetrons is classified under HS code 8540.71.00 (chapter 85, Electrical machinery and equipment and parts thereof; sound recorders and reproducers, television image and sound recorders and reproducers, and parts and accessories of such articles). It attracts 15% customs duty (general), or 7.25% from India & SAARC under SAFTA, plus 13% VAT on the CIF value and duty.

The chip is customs duty. “Total” under it is everything charged at the border (customs duty, excise, VAT and other fees) as a share of CIF value, for goods from other countries and from India. Declaring a lower-duty line that does not match the goods leads to reclassification, the duty difference and a fine. When unsure, ask the Department of Customs for an advance ruling.
