About

Mundra, in Gujarat, is India's largest private port (operated by the Adani group) and a growing west-coast gateway with its own dedicated rail corridors toward northern India.

Role for Nepal cargo

Like Nhava Sheva, Mundra serves Nepal cargo from Europe and the Gulf, offering an alternative west-coast entry with strong Concor rail connectivity toward the Nepal border and the Birgunj dry port.

How cargo reaches Nepal

Ocean vessel → Mundra Port → Rail (Concor) / road → Birgunj corridor → Birgunj ICD → Nepal market

Inland leg to Birgunj ICD: ~2,000 km · ~6–9 days (after port release). Mode: Rail (Concor) / road.
