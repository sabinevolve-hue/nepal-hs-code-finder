Chobhar Dry Port (Kathmandu)

Kathmandu Valley dry port — clearance near the capital's market.

Nepal dry portType
NITDBOperator
RoadMode to Nepal

About

Chobhar Dry Port, in the Kathmandu Valley (operational from 2020), was built to decongest border clearance and move customs closer to the capital's consumption centre.

What clears here

Cargo is trucked up from the border and Birgunj corridor; Chobhar clears goods destined for the Kathmandu market, easing pressure on the southern dry ports for capital-bound freight.

How cargo reaches Nepal

Indian gateway → Birgunj corridor → Road to Kathmandu → Chobhar Dry Port → Kathmandu market

Border crossing: fed overland from Birgunj / the southern border. Mode: Road.

Fed by: Birgunj ICD.
