Track a container to Nepal

Paste your container number — we’ll detect the shipping line and take you straight to its own tracker. We don’t store tracking data; the live status lives on the carrier’s site.

Because Nepal is landlocked, the full journey is usually tracked in two or three legs, each by a different party: 1. The ocean line (Maersk, MSC, …) — from the origin port to the Indian gateway (Kolkata / Vizag) or Bangladeshi port. 2. CONCOR / Indian Railways — the rail leg from the gateway port to Raxaul / the Nepal border. 3. Nepal Customs (ASYCUDA) — declaration and clearance at the Birgunj or other dry port.

Transit framework: Nepal–India Treaty of Transit and Nepal–Bangladesh transit arrangements. Dry ports are operated under the Nepal Intermodal Transport Development Board (NITDB); clearance is by the Department of Customs.
