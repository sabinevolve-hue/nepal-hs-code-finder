Biratnagar ICD

Road-based dry port serving eastern Nepal.

Nepal dry portType
NITDBOperator
RoadMode to Nepal

About

Biratnagar ICD, in eastern Nepal near the Jogbani (India) border, is a road-based inland clearance depot serving the eastern region and its industrial corridor.

What clears here

It handles cargo trucked from the Indian rail/road network and is part of Nepal's NITDB dry-port system, clearing goods destined for eastern Nepal without routing everything through Birgunj.

How cargo reaches Nepal

Indian gateway → Rail / road to border → Jogbani (border) → Biratnagar ICD → Eastern Nepal

Border crossing: Jogbani (India). Mode: Road.

Fed by: Kolkata, Haldia, Visakhapatnam.
