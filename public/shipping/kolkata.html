About

Kolkata Port (officially the Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, Kolkata) is Nepal's traditional and still-heavily-used sea gateway under the Nepal–India Treaty of Transit. It is a riverine port on the Hooghly, so vessel draft is limited and larger ships often work the downstream docks at Haldia.

Role for Nepal cargo

Containers for Nepal move from Kolkata by broad-gauge rail to Raxaul, across the border from Birgunj, and on to the Birgunj ICD (Sirsiya) for Nepal Customs clearance. Congestion and dwell at Kolkata can be significant, which is a large part of why Visakhapatnam has grown as an alternative east-coast gateway.

How cargo reaches Nepal

Ocean vessel → Kolkata Port → Broad-gauge rail → Raxaul (border) → Birgunj ICD → Nepal market

Inland leg to Birgunj ICD: ~720 km · ~3–6 days (after port release). Mode: Broad-gauge rail + road.
