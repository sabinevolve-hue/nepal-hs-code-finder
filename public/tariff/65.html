Chapter 65 legal notes Notes. 1.- This Chapter does not cover : (a) Worn headgear of heading 63.09; (b) Asbestos headgear (heading 68.12); or (c) Dolls' hats, other toy hats or carnival articles of Chapter 95. 2.- Heading 65.02 does not cover hat-shapes made by sewing other than those obtained simply by sewing strips in spirals.
