Chapter 37 legal notes Notes. 1.- This Chapter does not cover waste or scrap. 2.- In this Chapter the word “photographic” relates to the process by which visible images are formed, directly or indirectly, by the action of light or other forms of radiation on photosensitive, including thermosensitive, surfaces.
