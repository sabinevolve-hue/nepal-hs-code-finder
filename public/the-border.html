Quick answers

Which is the busiest customs point in Nepal?
Birgunj. It cleared imports worth Rs 9.86 kharba in FY 2082/83, 47% of everything Nepal imported.
How much of Nepal’s imports come across the India border?
About 84% of imports by value were cleared at gates on the India border in FY 2082/83, 5.1% at the China border gates and 10% at airports and the inland dry port.
How many customs offices handle trade in Nepal?
28 customs offices reported trade in FY 2082/83: 22 on the India border, 3 on the China border and 3 airports or inland ports.
