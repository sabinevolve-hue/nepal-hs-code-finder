What this means if you trade

  • The lower rate applies only with a valid certificate of origin, and only to goods that meet the origin rules. How SAFTA works.
  • The saving is larger than the duty gap, because VAT is charged on the value after duty.

How this was worked out

Every figure is calculated from the Department of Customs, Nepal, Foreign Trade Statistics, FY 2082/83, and the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. Values are in Nepali rupees (1 crore = 10 million, 1 arba = 1 billion, 1 kharba = 100 billion). Rates are from the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. India import values are from the top-six partner list for each line, so small amounts may be missing. The article reports what the data shows and does not explain causes it cannot show.
