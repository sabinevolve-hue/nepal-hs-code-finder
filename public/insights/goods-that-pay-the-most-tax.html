What this means if you trade

  • Where revenue as a share of value is well above the customs duty rate, the difference is excise, VAT and other border charges.
  • Where it is below the duty rate, part of the imports entered under an exemption, a SAFTA rate or a concession.

How this was worked out

Every figure is calculated from the Department of Customs, Nepal, Foreign Trade Statistics, FY 2082/83, and the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. Values are in Nepali rupees (1 crore = 10 million, 1 arba = 1 billion, 1 kharba = 100 billion). “Revenue” is the Department of Customs figure for all taxes collected at the border on that line, not customs duty alone. The article reports what the data shows and does not explain causes it cannot show.
