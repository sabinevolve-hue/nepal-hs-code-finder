What this means if you trade

  • Most import value sits in the low bands, while the high bands collect far more duty per rupee imported.
  • The duty-free page lists the 0% and 5% lines. Each product page shows the other lines in its family and their rates.

How this was worked out

Every figure is calculated from the Department of Customs, Nepal, Foreign Trade Statistics, FY 2082/83, and the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. Values are in Nepali rupees (1 crore = 10 million, 1 arba = 1 billion, 1 kharba = 100 billion). “Others” covers lines charged per unit (per litre, per stick, per tonne) and partial exemptions, as grouped by the Department of Customs. The article reports what the data shows and does not explain causes it cannot show.
