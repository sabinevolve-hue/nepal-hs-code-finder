Nepal’s food import bill

Nepal imported Rs 4.41 kharba of food and farm goods in FY 2082/83, 21.0% of all imports.

Food and farm goods: imports over seven years

Imports of food and farm goods were Rs 2.43 kharba in FY 2076/77 and Rs 4.41 kharba in FY 2082/83 (+81%). Their share of the total import bill moved from 20.3% to 21.0%.

How this was worked out

Every figure is calculated from the Department of Customs, Nepal, Foreign Trade Statistics, FY 2082/83, and the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. Values are in Nepali rupees (1 crore = 10 million, 1 arba = 1 billion, 1 kharba = 100 billion). “Food and farm” here means HS chapters 1 to 24: live animals, food, drink, animal feed and tobacco. Edible oil imported for refining and re-export is included. The article reports what the data shows and does not explain causes it cannot show.
