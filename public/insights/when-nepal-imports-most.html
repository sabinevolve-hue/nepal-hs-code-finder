The months when Nepal imports most

An even year would put 8.3% in every month. On average over FY 2080/81, 2081/82, 2082/83, Jestha takes 9.5% of imports and Kartik 6.7%.

What this means if you trade

  • Busy months mean busier customs yards and tighter truck supply at the main gates. Book transport earlier in those months.
  • The pattern is an average of 3 years. Any one year can differ.

How this was worked out

Every figure is calculated from the Department of Customs, Nepal, Foreign Trade Statistics, FY 2082/83, and the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. Values are in Nepali rupees (1 crore = 10 million, 1 arba = 1 billion, 1 kharba = 100 billion). Monthly values are the Department of Customs’ month-by-month figures for FY 2080/81, 2081/82, 2082/83. Nepali months run mid-month to mid-month (Shrawan starts in mid-July). The article reports what the data shows and does not explain causes it cannot show.
