China supplies the most in Electrical & electronics, Fertilisers, Fruit & nuts, Woven garments. In Edible oils & fats, Gems, gold & jewellery, Oil seeds & medicinal plants, Optical, medical & precision, all other countries combined supply more than either.

What this means if you trade

  • Where India leads, check the SAFTA rate. Where China leads, the general rate applies and transit is longer.
  • Compare the two countries line by line.

How this was worked out

Every figure is calculated from the Department of Customs, Nepal, Foreign Trade Statistics, FY 2082/83, and the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. Values are in Nepali rupees (1 crore = 10 million, 1 arba = 1 billion, 1 kharba = 100 billion). The article reports what the data shows and does not explain causes it cannot show.
