What this means if you trade

  • Groups with a high revenue-to-value figure are where the landed cost sits furthest above the invoice price. Run those in the calculator before quoting a price.
  • See the effective rate for every large group.

How this was worked out

Every figure is calculated from the Department of Customs, Nepal, Foreign Trade Statistics, FY 2082/83, and the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. Values are in Nepali rupees (1 crore = 10 million, 1 arba = 1 billion, 1 kharba = 100 billion). “Revenue” is the figure the Department of Customs reports against imports: customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border. The article reports what the data shows and does not explain causes it cannot show.
