Is Nepal taxing imports more or less?

8.0% of Nepal’s imports by value entered duty free in FY 2082/83, against 7.0% in FY 2076/77.

In FY 2076/77, 28.2% of imports by value entered at 5% duty or less; in FY 2082/83 it was 29.1%. The share entering at 30% or more went from 8.9% to 8.2%.

What this means if you trade

  • The mix shifts when the tariff changes and when the goods people buy change. This chart shows the result, not which of the two caused it.

How this was worked out

Every figure is calculated from the Department of Customs, Nepal, Foreign Trade Statistics, FY 2082/83, and the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. Values are in Nepali rupees (1 crore = 10 million, 1 arba = 1 billion, 1 kharba = 100 billion). The article reports what the data shows and does not explain causes it cannot show.
