What each product group really pays at the border

Across all imports, customs collected 24.2% of value in FY 2082/83. By product group it ranged from 0.1% to 71.7%.

Customs Nepal data desk. Figures for FY 2082/83. Updated 2026-11-12.

The figure is revenue collected at customs divided by import value, for every product group with imports over Rs 10 arba in FY 2082/83. It is what was actually paid, after exemptions and SAFTA rates, not the rate printed in the tariff.

What this means if you trade

  • Use this as a first estimate of how far landed cost sits above invoice value in a sector. The exact figure for a line is on its product page.
  • Light-taxed groups are mostly inputs: fertiliser, raw materials, machinery. Heavy-taxed groups are mostly finished consumer goods and fuel.

How this was worked out

Every figure is calculated from the Department of Customs, Nepal, Foreign Trade Statistics, FY 2082/83, and the Nepal Customs Tariff 2083/84. Values are in Nepali rupees (1 crore = 10 million, 1 arba = 1 billion, 1 kharba = 100 billion). The article reports what the data shows and does not explain causes it cannot show.
