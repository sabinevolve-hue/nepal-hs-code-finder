Quick answers

How much trade passes through Biratnagar customs?
Biratnagar Customs Office cleared imports worth Rs 2.42 kharba and exports worth Rs 88.73 arba in FY 2082/83, according to the Department of Customs.
What share of Nepal’s imports comes through Biratnagar?
11.5% of Nepal’s imports and 28.1% of its exports by value were cleared at Biratnagar in FY 2082/83. It ranks #3 of 28 customs offices by total trade.
Where is Biratnagar Customs Office?
Biratnagar Customs Office is in Rani, Biratnagar, Koshi Province — India (Jogbani, Bihar).
