Quick answers

How much trade passes through Jaleshwor customs?
Jaleshwor Customs Office cleared imports worth Rs 8.24 arba and exports worth Rs 3.95 arba in FY 2082/83, according to the Department of Customs.
What share of Nepal’s imports comes through Jaleshwor?
0.4% of Nepal’s imports and 1.3% of its exports by value were cleared at Jaleshwor in FY 2082/83. It ranks #13 of 28 customs offices by total trade.
Where is Jaleshwor Customs Office?
Jaleshwor Customs Office is in Mahottari, Madhesh Province — India (Bihar).
