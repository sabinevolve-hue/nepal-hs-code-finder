🪢 Exporting jute sacks & bags from Nepal

Jute goods from the eastern Terai mills — a traditional industry, sold mainly to India.

About the product

Jute sacks and bags are produced by Nepal's jute mills, concentrated in the eastern Terai (Biratnagar). Jute is a long-standing industrial export using domestic and imported raw fibre.

Nepal’s position

India is overwhelmingly the main buyer. Demand tracks Indian packaging needs and the duty treatment under the India-Nepal trade regime.

How to export from Nepal

  1. Register for an EXIM code — One-time exporter registration with the Department of Customs.
  2. Certificate of origin — Chamber/TEPC; a SAFTA certificate for India.
  3. Quality to buyer spec — Weave, weight and sizing to the packaging buyer's standard.
  4. File the export declaration — Clear at the border.
