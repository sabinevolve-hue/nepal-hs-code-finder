🌿 Exporting large cardamom (alaichi) from Nepal

Nepal is the world's largest producer of large (black) cardamom. The trade picture and how to ship it.

About the product

Large cardamom (Amomum subulatum), known locally as alaichi, is grown in the eastern hill districts — Taplejung, Panchthar, Ilam and Sankhuwasabha. Nepal is the world's top producer.

Nepal’s position

Almost all of Nepal's crop goes to India, which consumes it and re-exports onward. Because the market is so India-concentrated, prices swing with Indian demand and with how the India-Nepal trade treaty treats the product in a given year.

How to export from Nepal

  1. Register for an EXIM code — One-time exporter registration with the Department of Customs.
  2. Phytosanitary certificate — From the National Plant Quarantine Program — required for agricultural produce.
  3. Certificate of origin — From a chamber or TEPC (a SAARC/SAFTA certificate for shipments to India).
  4. Grading & quality — To the buyer's specification — moisture, size, cleanliness.
  5. File the export declaration — Clear at the border / dry port.
