🍵 Exporting tea from Nepal

Himalayan orthodox tea from the eastern hills — bulk to India, premium leaf to Europe and Japan.

About the product

Nepali tea is grown mainly in Ilam and the eastern hills, bordering the Darjeeling belt. It splits into high-volume CTC tea and higher-value orthodox (whole-leaf) tea.

Nepal’s position

Most volume goes to India, often re-blended and re-exported. The real value opportunity is premium orthodox tea sold direct to specialty buyers in Germany, the EU, Japan and the US — where origin and quality command far higher prices.

How to export from Nepal

  1. Register for an EXIM code — One-time exporter registration with the Department of Customs.
  2. Phytosanitary certificate — From the National Plant Quarantine Program.
  3. Certificate of origin & quality — Chamber/TEPC certificate; orthodox grading and, increasingly, the Nepal Tea collective-trademark / origin story for premium buyers.
  4. File the export declaration — Clear through customs.
