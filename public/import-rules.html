Do I need a permit to import this into Nepal?

Customs duty is one question. Whether another authority has to approve the goods first is a separate one. Enter an HS code to see which authority to ask, and where that rule comes from.

Don’t know the code? Search by product name first.

Every commercial import

EXIM codeThe registration required for anyone importing or exporting goods. Issued by the Department of Customs; apply through the Nepal National Single Window.
Permits go through one portalLicences, permits and certificates from government agencies are applied for on the Nepal National Single Window. The system does not allow more goods than the agency approved.
Customs declarationDuty and 13% VAT are paid on declaration. See the rate for your product in import duty by product or work it out in the calculator.

Source for the first two: NNSW frequently asked questions, read 16 November 2026.

Who controls what

The rules this page knows, each with where it was read. “Reported” means a news report or a foreign government guide, not the authority’s own text.

Plants, seeds, fruit, vegetables, grain, spicesNational Center for Plant Quarantine and BiosecurityPlant quarantine offices issue an entry permit for imports of plants and plant products. Ask the office before shipping.Official page Plant Quarantine and Protection Act 2007 and Plant Protection Rules 2010, as described on the centre’s own site. National Center for Plant Quarantine and Biosecurity
Live animals, meat, fish, milk, eggs, other animal productsDepartment of Livestock Services, Animal QuarantineImports must enter through an Animal Quarantine Check Post and are held there for inspection at the importer’s cost. Animal feed, chicks, fingerlings, veterinary biological productsDepartment of Livestock ServicesA recommendation letter is needed before importing biological products, chicks, fingerlings or animal feed.Law Animal Health and Livestock Services Act 2055 (1998), section 18. Act text on FAOLEX
Food and drinkDepartment of Food Technology and Quality Control (DFTQC)Imported food can be sampled and tested by DFTQC at the border. Medicines and vaccinesDepartment of Drug Administration (DDA)A drug must be registered before it is imported, and the importer needs a recommendation letter for the import.Law Drugs Registration Rules 2038 (1981), rules 4B and 5, as summarised by FAOLEX. FAOLEX summary

What this page does not cover

These goods have rules of their own that this page has not tied to a source yet: vehicles, pesticides, fertiliser, cosmetics, medical devices, tobacco and alcohol, arms and explosives, radio and broadcasting equipment, chemicals and ozone-depleting gases, wildlife and forest products, gold and silver, used goods. Some goods are also banned or limited by government notice, and that list changes. If the checker shows nothing for your code, that is not a statement that no permit is needed. Ask the Department of Customs or your customs agent before you pay a supplier.
