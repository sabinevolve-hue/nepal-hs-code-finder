🇨🇷 Nepal–Costa Rica trade

Nepal and Costa Rica traded about Rs 11.0 crore in imports and Rs 9 lakh in exports in FY 2081/82. Here is what actually crosses the border, the duty it carries and how it moves.

What Nepal imports from Costa Rica

No SAFTA / preferential rate. Costa Rica is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

How goods move between Nepal and Costa Rica

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Costa Rica arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

This is a sourcing relationship, not an export one — Nepal buys far more than it sells. The practical moves: check whether a SAARC source carries a lower SAFTA rate, compare the route and landed cost before committing, and watch budget-year duty changes on electronics and vehicles. Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.
