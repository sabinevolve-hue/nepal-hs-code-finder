What Nepal imports from Finland

No SAFTA / preferential rate. Finland is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Finland — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 1.5 crore at customs on goods from Finland — 0.0% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 4.8 for every Rs 100 of goods, below the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Finland

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Finland arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.
