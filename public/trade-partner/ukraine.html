The Rs 100 test

What Nepal imports from Ukraine

No SAFTA / preferential rate. Ukraine is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Ukraine — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 1.56 arba at customs on goods from Ukraine — 0.3% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 17.4 for every Rs 100 of goods, below the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Ukraine

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Ukraine arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

This is a sourcing relationship, not an export one — Nepal buys far more than it sells. The practical moves: check whether a SAARC source carries a lower SAFTA rate, compare the route and landed cost before committing, and watch budget-year duty changes on electronics and vehicles. Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from Ukraine?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 8.98 arba from Ukraine in FY 2082/83, 0.4% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to Ukraine?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 29 lakh to Ukraine in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with Ukraine?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 8.98 arba with Ukraine in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from Ukraine?
The largest imports from Ukraine in FY 2082/83 were Crude sunflower oil (Rs 4.44 arba); Low erucic acid rape or colza seeds (Rs 2.46 arba); Crude soya-bean oil (Rs 1.26 arba).
