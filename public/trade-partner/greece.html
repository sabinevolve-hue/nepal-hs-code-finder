🇬🇷 Nepal–Greece trade

Nepal and Greece traded about Rs 9.3 crore in imports and Rs 2.3 crore in exports in FY 2081/82. Here is what actually crosses the border, the duty it carries and how it moves.

What Nepal imports from Greece

No SAFTA / preferential rate. Greece is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

How goods move between Nepal and Greece

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Greece arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.
