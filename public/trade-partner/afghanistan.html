“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Afghanistan — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 28.9 crore at customs on goods from Afghanistan — 0.1% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 25.4 for every Rs 100 of goods, above the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Afghanistan

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Afghanistan arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where Afghanistan already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from Afghanistan?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 1.14 arba from Afghanistan in FY 2082/83, 0.1% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to Afghanistan?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 25.7 crore to Afghanistan in FY 2082/83, 0.1% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with Afghanistan?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 88.1 crore with Afghanistan in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from Afghanistan?
The largest imports from Afghanistan in FY 2082/83 were Melon seeds (Rs 1.10 arba); Other Figs, (Anjir) (Rs 2.7 crore); Dried apricots (Rs 1.4 crore).
What does Nepal export most to Afghanistan?
The largest exports to Afghanistan in FY 2082/83 were Self-propelled bulldozers, excavators, nes (Rs 25.2 crore); Bridges and bridge-sections of iron or ste l (Rs 43 lakh); Stranded wire, cables of iron or steel, no electically insu… (Rs 7 lakh).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from Afghanistan?
Nepal collected Rs 28.9 crore at customs on imports from Afghanistan in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
