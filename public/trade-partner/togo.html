What Nepal imports from Togo

No SAFTA / preferential rate. Togo is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

What Nepal exports to Togo

Nepal’s exports to Togo are negligible this year.

How goods move between Nepal and Togo

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Togo arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.
