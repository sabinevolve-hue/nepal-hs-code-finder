What Nepal imports from United Kingdom

How goods move between Nepal and United Kingdom

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from United Kingdom arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.
