7 years of trade

What Nepal imports from United Kingdom

No SAFTA / preferential rate. United Kingdom is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from United Kingdom — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 3.45 arba at customs on goods from United Kingdom — 0.7% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 71.2 for every Rs 100 of goods, above the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and United Kingdom

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from United Kingdom arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where United Kingdom already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from United Kingdom?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 4.85 arba from United Kingdom in FY 2082/83, 0.2% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to United Kingdom?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 3.23 arba to United Kingdom in FY 2082/83, 1.0% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with United Kingdom?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 1.62 arba with United Kingdom in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from United Kingdom?
The largest imports from United Kingdom in FY 2082/83 were Ferrous waste and scrap, nes (Rs 82.5 crore); Whiskies of an alcoholic strength 30UP (39.94%) (Rs 77.6 crore); Raw Material of Whiskey (alcoholic fluids including spirits… (Rs 71.2 crore).
What does Nepal export most to United Kingdom?
The largest exports to United Kingdom in FY 2082/83 were other Felt, whether or not impregnated, coated, covered or… (Rs 62.1 crore); Carpets and other textile floor coverings, of wool or fine… (Rs 49.3 crore); Women's or girls' trousers, breeches, etc, of cotton (Rs 43.5 crore).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from United Kingdom?
Nepal collected Rs 3.45 arba at customs on imports from United Kingdom in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
