7 years of trade

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Indonesia — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 5.74 arba at customs on goods from Indonesia — 1.1% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 25.9 for every Rs 100 of goods, above the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Indonesia

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Indonesia arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

This is a sourcing relationship, not an export one — Nepal buys far more than it sells. The practical moves: check whether a SAARC source carries a lower SAFTA rate, compare the route and landed cost before committing, and watch budget-year duty changes on electronics and vehicles. Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from Indonesia?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 22.19 arba from Indonesia in FY 2082/83, 1.1% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to Indonesia?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 26.1 crore to Indonesia in FY 2082/83, 0.1% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with Indonesia?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 21.93 arba with Indonesia in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from Indonesia?
The largest imports from Indonesia in FY 2082/83 were Crude palm oil (Rs 9.01 arba); Fertilizer Grade (Rs 2.49 arba); Areca nuts, fresh or dried (Rs 2.38 arba).
What does Nepal export most to Indonesia?
The largest exports to Indonesia in FY 2082/83 were Other, including hides (leather) nes (Rs 18.2 crore); Other, leather of bovine or equine animals in the dry state… (Rs 3.2 crore); Percussion musical instruments (eg drums, xylophones, cymba… (Rs 1.2 crore).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from Indonesia?
Nepal collected Rs 5.74 arba at customs on imports from Indonesia in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
