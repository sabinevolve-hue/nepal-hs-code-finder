7 years of trade

Since FY 2076/77, Nepal’s imports from Canada moved from Rs 16.74 arba to Rs 8.02 arba (−52%) and exports from Rs 68.7 crore to Rs 1.55 arba (+126%).

FY 2083/84 so far (Shrawan–Bhadra (first 2 months)): imports Rs 1.96 arba · exports Rs 28.0 crore. Provisional, from the latest monthly release; it will be replaced by the full-year figure.

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Canada — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 1.17 arba at customs on goods from Canada — 0.2% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 14.6 for every Rs 100 of goods, below the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Canada

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Canada arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

This is a sourcing relationship, not an export one — Nepal buys far more than it sells. The practical moves: check whether a SAARC source carries a lower SAFTA rate, compare the route and landed cost before committing, and watch budget-year duty changes on electronics and vehicles. Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from Canada?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 8.02 arba from Canada in FY 2082/83, 0.4% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to Canada?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 1.55 arba to Canada in FY 2082/83, 0.5% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with Canada?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 6.47 arba with Canada in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from Canada?
The largest imports from Canada in FY 2082/83 were Aircraft spark-ignition piston engines (Rs 4.34 arba); Turbo-propellers, of a power >1100kw (Rs 1.27 arba); Low erucic acid rape or colza seeds (Rs 1.08 arba).
What does Nepal export most to Canada?
The largest exports to Canada in FY 2082/83 were Dog or cat food, put up for retail sale (Rs 31.6 crore); Carpets and other textile floor coverings, of wool or fine… (Rs 22.6 crore); other Felt, whether or not impregnated, coated, covered or… (Rs 17.4 crore).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from Canada?
Nepal collected Rs 1.17 arba at customs on imports from Canada in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
