“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Japan — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 4.63 arba at customs on goods from Japan — 0.9% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 49.6 for every Rs 100 of goods, above the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Japan

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Japan arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where Japan already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from Japan?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 9.35 arba from Japan in FY 2082/83, 0.4% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to Japan?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 2.31 arba to Japan in FY 2082/83, 0.7% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with Japan?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 7.04 arba with Japan in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from Japan?
The largest imports from Japan in FY 2082/83 were Flat/hot-rolled iron/steel, in coils, width >=600mm, not pi… (Rs 3.33 arba); Other vehicle (Diesel engine) capacity >2500CC (Rs 72.9 crore); Computed tomography apparatus, based on the use of X-rays (Rs 43.8 crore).
What does Nepal export most to Japan?
The largest exports to Japan in FY 2082/83 were Other pasta, nes (Rs 22.9 crore); Women's or girls' trousers, breeches, etc, of cotton (Rs 17.1 crore); Dalmoth, Namkeen, Bhujiya and Chamena (titbits) (Rs 16.0 crore).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from Japan?
Nepal collected Rs 4.63 arba at customs on imports from Japan in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
