“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Sri Lanka — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 6.5 crore at customs on goods from Sri Lanka — 0.0% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 18.7 for every Rs 100 of goods, below the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Sri Lanka

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Sri Lanka arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

This is a sourcing relationship, not an export one — Nepal buys far more than it sells. The practical moves: check whether a SAARC source carries a lower SAFTA rate, compare the route and landed cost before committing, and watch budget-year duty changes on electronics and vehicles. Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from Sri Lanka?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 34.9 crore from Sri Lanka in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to Sri Lanka?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 5.4 crore to Sri Lanka in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with Sri Lanka?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 29.6 crore with Sri Lanka in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from Sri Lanka?
The largest imports from Sri Lanka in FY 2082/83 were Other preparations of a kind used in animal feeding, nes (Rs 12.5 crore); Carboys, bottles, flasks, jar, pot, phials etc of glass, nes (Rs 5.6 crore); Computer Software's Printed Manual (Rs 5.2 crore).
What does Nepal export most to Sri Lanka?
The largest exports to Sri Lanka in FY 2082/83 were Other Electro-diagnostic apparatus (Rs 1.7 crore); Intraocular contact lenses (Rs 1.1 crore); Inner tubes of a kind used on Agricultural Tractors (Rs 71 lakh).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from Sri Lanka?
Nepal collected Rs 6.5 crore at customs on imports from Sri Lanka in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
