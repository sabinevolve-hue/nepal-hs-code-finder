🇱🇰 Nepal–Sri Lanka trade

Nepal and Sri Lanka traded about Rs 35.8 crore in imports and Rs 7.6 crore in exports in FY 2081/82. Here is what actually crosses the border, the duty it carries and how it moves.

What Nepal imports from Sri Lanka

SAFTA may apply. Sri Lanka is a SAARC member, so many goods qualify for the lower SAARC/SAFTA preferential duty with a valid Certificate of Origin — see the SAFTA guide. The rates below are the general (MFN) rates; the SAFTA rate is shown where it is lower.

How goods move between Nepal and Sri Lanka

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Sri Lanka arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where Sri Lanka already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.
