How goods move between Nepal and Taiwan, Province of China

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Taiwan, Province of China arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.
