7 years of trade

Since FY 2076/77, Nepal’s imports from India moved from Rs 7.35 kharba to Rs 12.11 kharba (+65%) and exports from Rs 70.11 arba to Rs 2.59 kharba (+269%).

FY 2083/84 so far (Shrawan–Asoj (first 3 months)): imports Rs 3.67 kharba · exports Rs 95.59 arba. Provisional, from the latest monthly release; it will be replaced by the full-year figure.

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from India — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 3.36 kharba at customs on goods from India — 66.3% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 27.7 for every Rs 100 of goods, above the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and India

Overland, across the open Nepal–India border. Goods move by road and rail directly across the border — Birgunj–Raxaul (the rail-linked main gate), Bhairahawa–Sunauli, Biratnagar–Jogbani and Kakarbhitta. No sea leg and no third-country transit.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where India already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from India?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 12.11 kharba from India in FY 2082/83, 57.7% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to India?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 2.59 kharba to India in FY 2082/83, 82.0% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with India?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 9.52 kharba with India in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from India?
The largest imports from India in FY 2082/83 were Diesel (Rs 1.72 kharba); Petrol (Rs 77.27 arba); Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) (Rs 60.28 arba).
What does Nepal export most to India?
The largest exports to India in FY 2082/83 were Soya-bean oil (excl. crude) and fractions (Rs 1.29 kharba); Big Cardamon (Alaichi) neither crushed nor ground (Rs 12.64 arba); Sunflower-seed and safflower oil (excl. crude) and fraction… (Rs 8.94 arba).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from India?
Nepal collected Rs 3.36 kharba at customs on imports from India in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).

Source: Department of Customs, Nepal — Foreign Trade Statistics FY 2082/83.
