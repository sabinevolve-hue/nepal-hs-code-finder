4 years of trade

Since FY 2079/80, Nepal’s imports from China moved from Rs 2.23 kharba to Rs 4.25 kharba (+91%) and exports from Rs 1.77 arba to Rs 1.89 arba (+7%).

FY 2083/84 so far (Shrawan–Bhadra (first 2 months)): imports Rs 76.40 arba · exports Rs 29.3 crore. Provisional, from the latest monthly release; it will be replaced by the full-year figure.

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from China — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 1.02 kharba at customs on goods from China — 20.2% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 24.0 for every Rs 100 of goods, close to the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and China

Overland, over the Himalaya — not by sea. China trade runs by road across the mountains: Rasuwagadhi–Kerung (Gyirong), Nepal's main China gate, and the historic Tatopani–Khasa (Zhangmu) crossing. A Kerung–Kathmandu rail link is proposed, not built.

The opportunity, honestly

This is a sourcing relationship, not an export one — Nepal buys far more than it sells. The practical moves: check whether a SAARC source carries a lower SAFTA rate, compare the route and landed cost before committing, and watch budget-year duty changes on electronics and vehicles. Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from China?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 4.25 kharba from China in FY 2082/83, 20.3% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to China?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 1.89 arba to China in FY 2082/83, 0.6% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with China?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 4.23 kharba with China in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from China?
The largest imports from China in FY 2082/83 were Smartphones (Rs 36.79 arba); Diammonium hydrogenorthophosphate (diammonium phosphate) (Rs 20.98 arba); Electric car, jeep & van 51KW to <=100KW (Rs 12.03 arba).
What does Nepal export most to China?
The largest exports to China in FY 2082/83 were Table, kitchen or other household articles nd parts thereof… (Rs 25.1 crore); Carpets and other textile floor coverings, of wool or fine… (Rs 24.3 crore); Other vegetable saps and extracts, nes (Rs 20.2 crore).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from China?
Nepal collected Rs 1.02 kharba at customs on imports from China in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
