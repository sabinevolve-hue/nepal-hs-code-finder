🇸🇳 Nepal–Senegal trade

Nepal and Senegal traded about Rs 30.7 crore in imports and Rs 0 in exports in FY 2081/82. Here is what actually crosses the border, the duty it carries and how it moves.

What Nepal imports from Senegal

No SAFTA / preferential rate. Senegal is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

What Nepal exports to Senegal

Nepal’s exports to Senegal are negligible this year.

How goods move between Nepal and Senegal

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Senegal arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where Senegal already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.
