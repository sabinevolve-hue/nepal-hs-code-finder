What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 5 lakh at customs on goods from Senegal — 0.0% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 31.5 for every Rs 100 of goods, above the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Senegal

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Senegal arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.
