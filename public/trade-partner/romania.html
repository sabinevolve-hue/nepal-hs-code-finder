🇷🇴 Nepal–Romania trade

Nepal and Romania traded about Rs 9.4 crore in imports and Rs 15.9 crore in exports in FY 2081/82. Here is what actually crosses the border, the duty it carries and how it moves.

What Nepal imports from Romania

No SAFTA / preferential rate. Romania is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

How goods move between Nepal and Romania

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Romania arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where Romania already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.
