“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Pakistan — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 7.6 crore at customs on goods from Pakistan — 0.0% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 25.2 for every Rs 100 of goods, close to the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Pakistan

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Pakistan arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where Pakistan already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from Pakistan?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 30.3 crore from Pakistan in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to Pakistan?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 21.5 crore to Pakistan in FY 2082/83, 0.1% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with Pakistan?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 8.8 crore with Pakistan in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).

What does Nepal export most to Pakistan?

How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from Pakistan?
