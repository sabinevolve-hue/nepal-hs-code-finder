7 years of trade

Since FY 2076/77, Nepal’s imports from Chile moved from Rs 16.8 crore to Rs 59.5 crore (+255%) and exports from Rs 73 lakh to Rs 53 lakh (−27%).

FY 2083/84 so far (Shrawan–Bhadra (first 2 months)): imports Rs 5.4 crore · exports Rs 51 lakh. Provisional, from the latest monthly release; it will be replaced by the full-year figure.

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Chile — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 14.7 crore at customs on goods from Chile — 0.0% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 24.6 for every Rs 100 of goods, close to the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Chile

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Chile arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

This is a sourcing relationship, not an export one — Nepal buys far more than it sells. The practical moves: check whether a SAARC source carries a lower SAFTA rate, compare the route and landed cost before committing, and watch budget-year duty changes on electronics and vehicles. Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

How much does Nepal import from Chile?

How much does Nepal export to Chile?

What is Nepal’s trade balance with Chile?
