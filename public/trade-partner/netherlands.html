What Nepal imports from Netherlands

No SAFTA / preferential rate. Netherlands is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Netherlands — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 35.1 crore at customs on goods from Netherlands — 0.1% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 15.0 for every Rs 100 of goods, below the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Netherlands

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Netherlands arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where Netherlands already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from Netherlands?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 2.34 arba from Netherlands in FY 2082/83, 0.1% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to Netherlands?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 1.46 arba to Netherlands in FY 2082/83, 0.5% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with Netherlands?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 88.0 crore with Netherlands in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from Netherlands?
The largest imports from Netherlands in FY 2082/83 were Magnetic Resonance imaging (MRI) Machine (Rs 45.1 crore); Vaccines for human medicine (Rs 28.6 crore); Malt not roasted (Rs 24.2 crore).
What does Nepal export most to Netherlands?
The largest exports to Netherlands in FY 2082/83 were other Felt, whether or not impregnated, coated, covered or… (Rs 51.7 crore); Imitation jewellery of base metal (incl p l ted), nes (Rs 7.7 crore); Shawls, scarves, mufflers, mantillas, veil, etc, knitted or… (Rs 7.7 crore).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from Netherlands?
Nepal collected Rs 35.1 crore at customs on imports from Netherlands in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
