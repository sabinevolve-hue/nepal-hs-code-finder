🇧🇪 Nepal–Belgium trade

Nepal and Belgium traded about Rs 95.1 crore in imports and Rs 55.4 crore in exports in FY 2081/82. Here is what actually crosses the border, the duty it carries and how it moves.

What Nepal imports from Belgium

No SAFTA / preferential rate. Belgium is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

How goods move between Nepal and Belgium

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Belgium arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

Nepal has a real two-way relationship here. On the export side, look at where Belgium already buys heavily and where Nepal has genuine product (herbs, carpets, felt, pashmina, specialty agro). Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.
