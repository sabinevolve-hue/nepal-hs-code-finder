“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from Cambodia — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 3.6 crore at customs on goods from Cambodia — 0.0% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 31.4 for every Rs 100 of goods, above the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and Cambodia

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from Cambodia arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

This is a sourcing relationship, not an export one — Nepal buys far more than it sells. The practical moves: check whether a SAARC source carries a lower SAFTA rate, compare the route and landed cost before committing, and watch budget-year duty changes on electronics and vehicles. Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from Cambodia?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 11.5 crore from Cambodia in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to Cambodia?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 1.6 crore to Cambodia in FY 2082/83, 0.0% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with Cambodia?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 9.9 crore with Cambodia in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from Cambodia?
The largest imports from Cambodia in FY 2082/83 were Smartphones (Rs 4.4 crore); New pneumatic tyres, of rubber of a kind used on motor cars (Rs 1.4 crore); Sport footwear Having the upper parts of other fabrics (Rs 49 lakh).
What does Nepal export most to Cambodia?
The largest exports to Cambodia in FY 2082/83 were Other Dried pepper neither crushed nor ground (Rs 58 lakh); Other, including hides (leather) nes (Rs 43 lakh); Rum and other spirits of an alcoholic strength 25UP (42.8%) (Rs 13 lakh).
How much customs revenue does Nepal collect on goods from Cambodia?
Nepal collected Rs 3.6 crore at customs on imports from Cambodia in FY 2082/83 (customs duty plus other taxes charged at the border).
