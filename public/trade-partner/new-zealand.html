7 years of trade

Since FY 2076/77, Nepal’s imports from New Zealand moved from Rs 1.08 arba to Rs 1.77 arba (+64%) and exports from Rs 6.9 crore to Rs 26.6 crore (+282%).

FY 2083/84 so far (Shrawan–Asoj (first 3 months)): imports Rs 58.0 crore · exports Rs 8.1 crore. Provisional, from the latest monthly release; it will be replaced by the full-year figure.

What Nepal imports from New Zealand

No SAFTA / preferential rate. New Zealand is not a SAARC member, so its goods pay Nepal’s full general (MFN) duty — the rates shown below apply in full.

“Collected at customs” is the revenue the Department of Customs reports against each product from New Zealand — customs duty plus the other taxes charged at the border (such as VAT and excise) — so it can be well above the duty rate alone, and is not zero just because the duty rate is.

What Nepal collects at the border

Nepal collected Rs 22.9 crore at customs on goods from New Zealand — 0.0% of everything customs collected on imports that year. That is about Rs 13.0 for every Rs 100 of goods, below the Rs 24.1 average across all imports. The mix explains it: fuel, vehicles and consumer goods are taxed heavily; raw materials, machinery and many electronics lightly.

How goods move between Nepal and New Zealand

By sea to an Indian gateway port, then overland. As a landlocked country Nepal has no sea port, so goods from New Zealand arrive by ocean freight at an Indian gateway — Kolkata/Haldia or Visakhapatnam — then move by rail/road to the Birgunj (Sirsiya) ICD for customs clearance.

The opportunity, honestly

This is a sourcing relationship, not an export one — Nepal buys far more than it sells. The practical moves: check whether a SAARC source carries a lower SAFTA rate, compare the route and landed cost before committing, and watch budget-year duty changes on electronics and vehicles. Import demand is not the same as profit — treat these figures as a starting point for research.

Quick answers

How much does Nepal import from New Zealand?
Nepal imported goods worth Rs 1.77 arba from New Zealand in FY 2082/83, 0.1% of Nepal’s total imports, according to the Department of Customs.
How much does Nepal export to New Zealand?
Nepal exported goods worth Rs 26.6 crore to New Zealand in FY 2082/83, 0.1% of Nepal’s total exports.
What is Nepal’s trade balance with New Zealand?
Nepal ran a trade deficit of Rs 1.50 arba with New Zealand in FY 2082/83 (exports minus imports).
What does Nepal import most from New Zealand?
