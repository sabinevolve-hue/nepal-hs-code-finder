About Customs Nepal

Nepal's customs tariff, HS codes and trade data — in one simple place.

Customs Nepal is a free portal for individuals and organizations to find the correct Nepal HS code, customs duty, VAT and landed cost for any product — and to understand how import and export classification works in Nepal.

It brings the official Customs Tariff 2026/27 (FY 2083/84) together with trade data and simple tools, so importers, exporters, customs agents, banks and businesses get accurate answers in seconds instead of digging through PDFs.

What you can do

  • Search HS codes — the full Customs Tariff 2026/27 (6,340 lines), by product name, material or code, in English or Nepali.
  • Classify with AI — describe a product in plain words and get ranked HS code suggestions with the reasoning.
  • Calculate landed cost — customs duty, VAT and total landed cost in rupees, plus a proforma or commercial invoice.
  • Explore trade data — Nepal's imports and exports by product category and partner country, with multi-year trends.

Who it's for

Importers and exporters, customs and clearing agents, freight forwarders, banks and trade-finance teams, students, and any business that needs to know what a product costs to bring into or send out of Nepal.

Official data sources

The customs tariff, HS codes, duty rates and trade statistics in Customs Nepal are compiled from official Government of Nepal publications:

  • Department of Customs, Government of Nepal — Customs Tariff 2026/27 (FY 2083/84), HS classification and foreign trade statistics: customs.gov.np
  • Ministry of Finance, Government of Nepal — the annual Finance Act / budget that sets customs duty, excise and VAT rates: mof.gov.np

Not a government website

Customs Nepal is an independent, privately operated reference tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing the Government of Nepal, the Department of Customs, the Ministry of Finance, or any government entity. For official rulings and the authoritative text, always refer to the sources above.

A note on accuracy

Customs Nepal is a reference and estimation tool based on the official Customs Tariff 2026/27. It does not replace an official customs classification or assessment — always confirm the final HS code and taxes with the customs office or your clearing agent.

Privacy Policy

How Customs Nepal handles your information.

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

Customs Nepal ("we", "our") operates the website and Android app at customsnepal.com. This policy explains what information we handle when you use the HS code search, duty and landed-cost calculator, invoice tools, AI suggestions and trade data. It applies to both the website and the Android app, which shows the same site.

The short version

Most of what you do stays on your own device. What you type into the search, calculator and invoice tools lives in your browser and is cleared when you close the tab — it reaches our server only if you sign in and choose "Save to my account". We do not sell your data.

What we handle

  • Things you type or upload (searches, product descriptions, invoice details) stay in your browser. They are sent to our server only when you are signed in and tap "Save to my account".
  • Account information. Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google or an email link, our authentication provider (Supabase) stores your email address, and for Google sign-in your basic Google profile (name and email), so we can keep your saved items against your account.
  • AI features. When you use "Ask AI", "Suggest HS codes" or the invoice reader, the text you submit is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a response. Send only what you need classified.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics cookies to count visits and understand how the site is used. These collect standard, aggregated usage data (such as pages viewed and approximate region). No personal data you type into the tools is sent to them.

How we use it

To provide and operate the tools, keep you signed in and hold the items you choose to save, understand overall usage so we can improve the app, and keep the service secure.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers needed to run Customs Nepal: Supabase (sign-in and saved data), Anthropic (AI suggestions), and Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics and Vercel hosting (operating and measuring the site). Each processes data only to provide its service to us. We may disclose information if required by law.

Keeping and deleting data

Data in your browser is cleared when you close the tab or clear your browser. Saved account data is kept until you delete it or ask us to. To delete your account and the data linked to it, email info@customsnepal.com and we will remove it.

Security

All traffic to and from the site is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Customs Nepal is a business and reference tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the date above.
