Electric generating sets and rotary converters: where imports grew, 2022 to 2024

HS heading 85.02. Which markets bought more, who supplies them now, and which exporters gained. Largest exporters and importers of electric generating sets and rotary converters

World trade in electric generating sets and rotary converters went from $24B in 2022 to $26B in 2024 (+6.3%). Imports rose most in 🇬🇧 United Kingdom, by $369M, followed by 🇹🇷 Turkiye and 🇧🇷 Brazil.

How to read this page

These are recorded trade values, not forecasts. A rise in imports shows that a market bought more in dollar terms; it can reflect higher prices as well as larger volumes, and it says nothing about tariffs, standards, licences or demand next year. Check the importing country's rules and current prices before acting on any figure here.
