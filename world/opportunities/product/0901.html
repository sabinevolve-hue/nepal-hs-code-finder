Coffee: where imports grew, 2022 to 2024

HS heading 09.01. Which markets bought more, who supplies them now, and which exporters gained. Largest exporters and importers of coffee

World trade in coffee went from $46B in 2022 to $50B in 2024 (+8.9%). Imports rose most in 🇪🇸 Spain, by $422M, followed by 🇧🇪 Belgium and 🇮🇹 Italy.

How to read this page

These are recorded trade values, not forecasts. A rise in imports shows that a market bought more in dollar terms; it can reflect higher prices as well as larger volumes, and it says nothing about tariffs, standards, licences or demand next year. Check the importing country's rules and current prices before acting on any figure here.
