About the product

Hand-knotted Tibetan–Nepali woollen carpets are made around the Kathmandu Valley (Jawalakhel, Boudha, Sankhu). They are a flagship manufactured export and one of the country's larger formal-sector employers.

Nepal's position

The United States is Nepal's largest carpet market, followed by Germany, the UK and other EU countries. Western buyers increasingly require child-labour-free certification (GoodWeave), so that certification is effectively part of market access.

How to export from Nepal

  1. Register for an EXIM code with the Department of Customs.
  2. Certificate of origin from a chamber or TEPC; a GSP Form A where the destination grants Nepal preferential access (eligibility varies by country — confirm the current status before quoting duty-free).
  3. GoodWeave / quality certification, valued or required by most Western importers.
  4. File the export declaration and clear through customs (usually air or sea freight via Kolkata/Haldia).

Manufactured exports may qualify for the government's cash export-incentive scheme — check the current rate with TEPC, as it is reset annually.
