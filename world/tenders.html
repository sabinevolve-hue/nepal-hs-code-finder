How to read this page

Notices are loaded live from the World Bank each time you search, so the list is as current as its own. Each title opens the full notice on the World Bank site, which has the documents, the contact and the rules for bidding. World Trade Data does not run these tenders and cannot answer questions about them. Always confirm the deadline on the notice itself.

Where the figures come from. Partners and products for 2024: BACI by CEPII, reconciled from UN Comtrade. The latest year and the current year: the International Monetary Fund's trade-by-partner database, which carries each economy's own customs figures. Long-run totals: World Bank. Latest product detail: Eurostat for EU members, HM Revenue & Customs for the United Kingdom, and Brazil's trade ministry. Product names and codes: the Harmonized System, © World Customs Organization.
