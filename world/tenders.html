Open tenders: World Bank-financed projects

Invitations to bid and requests for expressions of interest are published by the World Bank for the projects it finances. Pick a country to open its notices on the World Bank site, newest first.

Each link opens the World Bank's procurement search for that name in a new tab. There you can narrow by notice type, category (goods, works, consulting services) and date, and open the full notice with its documents and deadline.

How to read this page

World Trade Data does not run these tenders, does not copy the notices and cannot answer questions about them. The search is by name, so a few results may mention a country without being for it; check the country shown on each notice. Only projects financed by the World Bank are covered; other lenders and national tenders are not. Always confirm the deadline on the notice itself.
