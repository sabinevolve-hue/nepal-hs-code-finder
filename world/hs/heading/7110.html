HS heading 71.10: Platinum; unwrought or in semi-manufactured forms, or in powder form

Where imports of platinum grew, 2022 to 2024

World trade in this heading was $47B in 2024, exported by 98 economies and imported by 131. The largest exporter was 🇿🇦 South Africa with 32.4% of the total, and the largest importer was 🇺🇸 United States with 15.9%.
