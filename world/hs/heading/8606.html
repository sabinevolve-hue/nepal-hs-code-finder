HS heading 86.06: Railway or tramway goods vans and wagons; not self-propelled

World trade in this heading was $7.3B in 2024, exported by 83 economies and imported by 132. The largest exporter was 🇲🇽 Mexico with 43.1% of the total, and the largest importer was 🇺🇸 United States with 46.2%.
