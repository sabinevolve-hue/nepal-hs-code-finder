HS heading 28.50: Hydrides, nitrides, azides, silicides and borides, whether or not chemically defined, other than compounds which are also carbides of heading no. 2849

World trade in this heading was $723M in 2024, exported by 67 economies and imported by 140. The largest exporter was 🇺🇸 United States with 30.9% of the total, and the largest importer was 🇰🇷 South Korea with 14.9%.
