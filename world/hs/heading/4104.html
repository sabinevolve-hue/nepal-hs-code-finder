Tanned or crust hides and skins of bovine: largest exporters and importers, 2024

HS heading 41.04: Tanned or crust hides and skins of bovine (including buffalo) or equine animals, without hair on, whether or not split, but not further prepared

World trade in this heading was $3.4B in 2024, exported by 116 economies and imported by 139. The largest exporter was 🇧🇷 Brazil with 20.8% of the total, and the largest importer was 🇨🇳 China with 24.0%.
