Cocoa: largest exporters and importers, 2024

HS heading 18.02: Cocoa; shells, husks, skins and other cocoa waste

World trade in this heading was $615M in 2024, exported by 78 economies and imported by 92. The largest exporter was 🇨🇮 Cote d'Ivoire with 93.2% of the total, and the largest importer was 🇺🇸 United States with 33.8%.
