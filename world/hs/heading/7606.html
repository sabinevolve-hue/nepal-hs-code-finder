HS heading 76.06: Aluminium; plates, sheets and strip, thickness exceeding 0.2mm

Where imports of aluminium grew, 2022 to 2024

World trade in this heading was $41B in 2024, exported by 151 economies and imported by 218. The largest exporter was 🇨🇳 China with 25.4% of the total, and the largest importer was 🇺🇸 United States with 9.0%.
