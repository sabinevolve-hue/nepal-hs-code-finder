HS heading 68.09: Plaster or compositions based on plaster; articles thereof

World trade in this heading was $2.8B in 2024, exported by 152 economies and imported by 222. The largest exporter was 🇲🇽 Mexico with 14.3% of the total, and the largest importer was 🇺🇸 United States with 13.0%.
