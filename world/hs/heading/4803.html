Chapter 48

Tissue: largest exporters and importers, 2024

HS heading 48.03: Tissue, towel, napkin stock or similar; for household or sanitary uses, cellulose wadding, webs of cellulose fibres, in rolls over 36cm in width or rectangular sheets with one side exceeding 36cm when unfolded

World trade in this heading was $5.5B in 2024, exported by 145 economies and imported by 222. The largest exporter was 🇮🇩 Indonesia with 14.2% of the total, and the largest importer was 🇺🇸 United States with 11.7%.
