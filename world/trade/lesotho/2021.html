Lesotho exported $1.1B of goods and imported $1.8B in 2021, a trade deficit of $762M. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (40.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (77.9% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +18.1% and imports by +4.6%.
