Netherlands exported $912B of goods and imported $841B in 2023, a trade surplus of $71B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (24.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (15.2% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −3.0% and imports by −5.8%.
