🇳🇱 Netherlands: trade in 2020

Europe & Central Asia · World rank #4 exporter, #5 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Netherlands exported $667B of goods and imported $591B in 2020, a trade surplus of $76B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (23.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −4.5% and imports by −6.3%.
