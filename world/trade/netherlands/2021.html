Netherlands exported $828B of goods and imported $752B in 2021, a trade surplus of $76B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (24.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.5% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +24.1% and imports by +27.2%.
