Sri Lanka exported $12B of goods and imported $18B in 2024, a trade deficit of $6.3B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (23.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.3% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +4.2% and imports by +14.0%.

† Sri Lanka had not reported some or all of 2024 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
