🇪🇷 Eritrea: trade in 2020

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #145 exporter, #189 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Eritrea exported $1.3B of goods and imported $300M in 2020, a trade surplus of $952M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (93.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +269.4% and imports by +9.9%.

† Eritrea had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
