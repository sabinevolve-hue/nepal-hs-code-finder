🇪🇷 Eritrea: trade in 2021

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #163 exporter, #191 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019
›

Eritrea exported $496M of goods and imported $312M in 2021, a trade surplus of $184M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (75.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.9% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −60.3% and imports by +3.9%.

† Eritrea had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
