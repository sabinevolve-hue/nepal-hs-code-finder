Tajikistan exported $1.7B of goods and imported $4.0B in 2024, a trade deficit of $2.3B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (23.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan (22.6% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +27.6% and imports by −5.7%.
