Kazakhstan exported $79B of goods and imported $65B in 2025, a trade surplus of $14B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇹 Italy (19.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇷🇺 Russia (29.9% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +10.2% and imports by +61.9%.

What stands out: Kazakhstan

  • Its three largest markets (🇮🇹 Italy, 🇨🇳 China, 🇷🇺 Russia) took 49% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 121 other partners.
  • One product chapter, mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation (HS 27), made up 47% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It was the world's largest exporter of 7 of its main products in 2024, among them Uranium; natural uranium and its compounds… 2844.10.
  • Exports in 2025 were 8 times their 2000 level ($9.9B then, $79B now), in current dollars.
