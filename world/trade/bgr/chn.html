🇧🇬 Bulgaria – 🇨🇳 China trade, 2025 and 2026

Bulgaria exported $822M of goods to China in 2025, and China exported $4.4B to Bulgaria. Trade between the two totalled $5.2B, with a $3.5B surplus for China. Against 2024, Bulgaria's exports to China changed by +0.4% and China's to Bulgaria by +53.5%.
