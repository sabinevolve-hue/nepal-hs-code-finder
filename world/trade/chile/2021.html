Chile exported $95B of goods and imported $92B in 2021, a trade surplus of $2.2B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (38.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (29.8% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +28.7% and imports by +56.0%.
