Chile exported $73B of goods and imported $59B in 2020, a trade surplus of $14B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (38.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (27.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +6.9% and imports by −15.3%.
