Benin exported $841M of goods and imported $2.7B in 2020, a trade deficit of $1.8B. Its largest export market was 🇧🇩 Bangladesh (38.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇳 India (11.6% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −1.4% and imports by −9.2%.
