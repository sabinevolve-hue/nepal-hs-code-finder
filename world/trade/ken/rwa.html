🇰🇪 Kenya – 🇷🇼 Rwanda trade, 2025 and 2026

Kenya exported $335M of goods to Rwanda in 2025, and Rwanda exported $10M to Kenya. Trade between the two totalled $346M, with a $325M surplus for Kenya. Against 2024, Kenya's exports to Rwanda changed by +6.5% and Rwanda's to Kenya by +131.0%.
