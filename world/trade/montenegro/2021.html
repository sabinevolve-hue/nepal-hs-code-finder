Montenegro exported $506M of goods and imported $2.9B in 2021, a trade deficit of $2.4B. Its largest export market was 🇷🇸 Serbia (25.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇷🇸 Serbia (20.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +22.3% and imports by +24.0%.
