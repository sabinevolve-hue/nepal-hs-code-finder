Nepal exported $1.2B of goods and imported $12B in 2023, a trade deficit of $11B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (68.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇳 India (61.0% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −7.2% and imports by −12.1%.
