🇩🇿 Algeria – 🇪🇸 Spain trade, 2025 and 2026

Algeria exported $4.0B of goods to Spain in 2025, and Spain exported $2.4B to Algeria. Trade between the two totalled $6.5B, with a $1.6B surplus for Algeria. Against 2024, Algeria's exports to Spain changed by −37.0% and Spain's to Algeria by +171.6%.
