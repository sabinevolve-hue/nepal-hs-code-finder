🇩🇿 Algeria – 🇹🇷 Turkiye trade, 2025 and 2026

Algeria exported $2.4B of goods to Turkiye in 2025, and Turkiye exported $2.5B to Algeria. Trade between the two totalled $4.8B, with a $92M surplus for Turkiye. Against 2024, Algeria's exports to Turkiye changed by −31.3% and Turkiye's to Algeria by −14.5%.
