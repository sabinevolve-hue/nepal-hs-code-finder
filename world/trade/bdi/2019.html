Burundi exported $155M of goods and imported $791M in 2019, a trade deficit of $635M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (45.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia (17.3% of imports).
