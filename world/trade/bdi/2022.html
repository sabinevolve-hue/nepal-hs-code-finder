Burundi exported $155M of goods and imported $1.1B in 2022, a trade deficit of $966M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (37.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia (16.5% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +33.2% and imports by +24.8%.
