Burundi exported $123M of goods and imported $776M in 2020, a trade deficit of $653M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (42.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.3% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −20.8% and imports by −1.9%.

† Burundi had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
