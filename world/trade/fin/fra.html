🇫🇮 Finland – 🇫🇷 France trade, 2025 and 2026

Finland exported $2.6B of goods to France in 2025, and France exported $2.1B to Finland. Trade between the two totalled $4.8B, with a $493M surplus for Finland. Against 2024, Finland's exports to France changed by +5.1% and France's to Finland by −4.2%.
