Israel exported $59B of goods and imported $90B in 2021, a trade deficit of $31B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (27.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (11.9% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +20.6% and imports by +28.8%.
