Israel exported $64B of goods and imported $92B in 2023, a trade deficit of $27B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (27.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (12.2% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −11.3% and imports by −14.9%.
