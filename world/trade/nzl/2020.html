New Zealand exported $39B of goods and imported $37B in 2020, a trade surplus of $1.6B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (27.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (22.6% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −3.1% and imports by −11.9%.
