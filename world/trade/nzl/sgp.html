🇳🇿 New Zealand – 🇸🇬 Singapore trade, 2025 and 2026

New Zealand exported $1.7B of goods to Singapore in 2025, and Singapore exported $3.3B to New Zealand. Trade between the two totalled $5.1B, with a $1.6B surplus for Singapore. Against 2024, New Zealand's exports to Singapore changed by +64.8% and Singapore's to New Zealand by +4.6%.
