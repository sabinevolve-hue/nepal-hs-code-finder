Turkmenistan exported $12B of goods and imported $5.6B in 2025, a trade surplus of $6.7B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (64.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (30.4% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −18.5% and imports by +26.1%.

What stands out: Turkmenistan

  • Its three largest markets (🇨🇳 China, 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan, 🇹🇷 Turkiye) took 85% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 74 other partners.
  • One product chapter, mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation (HS 27), made up 92% of what it exported in 2024.
  • Exports in 2025 were 4 times their 2000 level ($2.9B then, $12B now), in current dollars.
  • It exported 2.2 times as much as it imported in 2025.
