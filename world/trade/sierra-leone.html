What stands out: Sierra Leone

  • Its three largest markets (🇨🇳 China, 🇳🇱 Netherlands, 🇰🇷 South Korea) took 81% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 92 other partners.
  • One product chapter, ores, slag and ash (HS 26), made up 72% of what it exported in 2024.
  • Exports in 2025 were 112 times their 2000 level ($4M then, $466M now), in current dollars.
  • It imported 4.1 times as much as it exported in 2025.
