Uganda exported $6.2B of goods and imported $12B in 2023, a trade deficit of $5.8B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (22.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.9% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +75.4% and imports by +20.5%.
