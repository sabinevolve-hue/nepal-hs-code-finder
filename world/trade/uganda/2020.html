Uganda exported $4.2B of goods and imported $8.8B in 2020, a trade deficit of $4.6B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (44.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (15.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +16.4% and imports by +9.7%.
