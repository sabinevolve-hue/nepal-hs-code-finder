Uganda exported $3.5B of goods and imported $10.0B in 2022, a trade deficit of $6.4B. Its largest export market was 🇸🇸 South Sudan (17.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (19.3% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −11.1% and imports by +4.9%.
