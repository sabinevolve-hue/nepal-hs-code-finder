Bermuda exported $42M of goods and imported $1.3B in 2020, a trade deficit of $1.3B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (42.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (47.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −25.3% and imports by +38.3%.
