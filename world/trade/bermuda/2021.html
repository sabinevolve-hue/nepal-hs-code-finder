🇧🇲 Bermuda: trade in 2021

North America · World rank #188 exporter, #167 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Year‹

Bermuda exported $49M of goods and imported $1.3B in 2021, a trade deficit of $1.2B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (52.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (59.3% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +17.5% and imports by −3.8%.

† Bermuda had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
