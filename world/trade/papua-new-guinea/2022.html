Papua New Guinea exported $11B of goods and imported $6.6B in 2022, a trade surplus of $4.4B. Its largest export market was 🇯🇵 Japan (24.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇺 Australia (27.2% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +13.2% and imports by +28.5%.

† Papua New Guinea had not reported some or all of 2022 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
