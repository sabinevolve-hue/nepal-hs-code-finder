Papua New Guinea exported $9.9B of goods and imported $6.3B in 2024, a trade surplus of $3.6B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇺 Australia (24.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇺 Australia (29.9% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by −0.5% and imports by −0.3%.
