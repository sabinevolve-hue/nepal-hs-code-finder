🇧🇹 Bhutan: trade in 2022

South Asia · World rank #167 exporter, #169 importer of 202 economies (2022).

Bhutan exported $445M of goods and imported $1.6B in 2022, a trade deficit of $1.1B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (76.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇳 India (71.4% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −8.0% and imports by +20.3%.

† Bhutan had not reported some or all of 2022 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
