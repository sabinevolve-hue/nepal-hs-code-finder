🇧🇹 Bhutan: trade in 2020

South Asia · World rank #172 exporter, #174 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019

Bhutan exported $251M of goods and imported $924M in 2020, a trade deficit of $673M. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (71.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇳 India (73.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −43.5% and imports by −5.7%.

† Bhutan had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
