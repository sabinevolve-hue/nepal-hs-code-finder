Bhutan exported $323M of goods and imported $1.8B in 2024, a trade deficit of $1.4B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (68.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇳 India (64.6% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +2.5% and imports by +17.0%.

† Bhutan had not reported some or all of 2024 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
