Bhutan exported $315M of goods and imported $1.5B in 2023, a trade deficit of $1.2B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (69.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇳 India (62.2% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −29.2% and imports by −3.8%.
