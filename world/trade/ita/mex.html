🇮🇹 Italy – 🇲🇽 Mexico trade, 2025 and 2026

Italy exported $6.8B of goods to Mexico in 2025, and Mexico exported $1.4B to Italy. Trade between the two totalled $8.2B, with a $5.4B surplus for Italy. Against 2024, Italy's exports to Mexico changed by −5.8% and Mexico's to Italy by +5.4%.
