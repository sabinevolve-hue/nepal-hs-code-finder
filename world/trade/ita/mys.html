🇮🇹 Italy – 🇲🇾 Malaysia trade, 2025 and 2026

Italy exported $1.9B of goods to Malaysia in 2025, and Malaysia exported $1.8B to Italy. Trade between the two totalled $3.7B, with a $59M surplus for Italy. Against 2024, Italy's exports to Malaysia changed by +4.1% and Malaysia's to Italy by +26.3%.
