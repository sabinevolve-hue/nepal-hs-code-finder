🇿🇦 South Africa: trade in 2022

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #38 exporter, #37 importer of 202 economies (2022).

South Africa exported $123B of goods and imported $117B in 2022, a trade surplus of $6.1B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (9.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (20.3% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +0.8% and imports by +19.1%.
