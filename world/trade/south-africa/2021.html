South Africa exported $122B of goods and imported $98B in 2021, a trade surplus of $24B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (11.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (20.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +42.5% and imports by +35.4%.
