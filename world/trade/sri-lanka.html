Sri Lanka exported $13B of goods and imported $21B in 2025, a trade deficit of $8.0B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (22.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.2% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +7.4% and imports by +14.0%.
