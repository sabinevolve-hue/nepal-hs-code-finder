Cameroon exported $5.6B of goods and imported $7.9B in 2022, a trade deficit of $2.3B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (26.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (15.8% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +30.9% and imports by +14.0%.
