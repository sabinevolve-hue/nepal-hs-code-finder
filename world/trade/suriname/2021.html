🇸🇷 Suriname: trade in 2021

Latin America & Caribbean · World rank #136 exporter, #165 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019
›

Suriname exported $2.3B of goods and imported $1.4B in 2021, a trade surplus of $903M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (49.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (24.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +21.1% and imports by −7.6%.

† Suriname had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
