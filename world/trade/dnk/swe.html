🇩🇰 Denmark – 🇸🇪 Sweden trade, 2025 and 2026

Denmark exported $13B of goods to Sweden in 2025, and Sweden exported $15B to Denmark. Trade between the two totalled $28B, with a $1.4B surplus for Sweden. Against 2024, Denmark's exports to Sweden changed by +11.7% and Sweden's to Denmark by +10.2%.
