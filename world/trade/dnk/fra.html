🇩🇰 Denmark – 🇫🇷 France trade, 2025 and 2026

Denmark exported $5.4B of goods to France in 2025, and France exported $5.2B to Denmark. Trade between the two totalled $11B, with a $264M surplus for Denmark. Against 2024, Denmark's exports to France changed by +1.9% and France's to Denmark by +13.7%.
