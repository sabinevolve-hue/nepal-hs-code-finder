Bahrain exported $12B of goods and imported $15B in 2023, a trade deficit of $3.1B. Its largest export market was 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia (23.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (14.0% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −18.5% and imports by −1.1%.

† Bahrain had not reported some or all of 2023 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
