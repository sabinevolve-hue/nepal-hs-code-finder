Bahrain exported $15B of goods and imported $16B in 2022, a trade deficit of $472M. Its largest export market was 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia (20.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (14.5% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +21.7% and imports by +9.9%.
