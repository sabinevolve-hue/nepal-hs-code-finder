Bahrain exported $8.2B of goods and imported $19B in 2019, a trade deficit of $10B. Its largest export market was 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia (24.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia (33.8% of imports).
