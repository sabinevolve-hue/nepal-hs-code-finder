Greece exported $47B of goods and imported $76B in 2021, a trade deficit of $29B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇹 Italy (10.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (11.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +34.0% and imports by +35.8%.
