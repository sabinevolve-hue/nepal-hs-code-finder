Greece exported $55B of goods and imported $88B in 2023, a trade deficit of $33B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇹 Italy (11.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (10.7% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −5.8% and imports by −9.8%.
