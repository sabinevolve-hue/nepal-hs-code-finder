Spain exported $304B of goods and imported $326B in 2020, a trade deficit of $22B. Its largest export market was 🇫🇷 France (16.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (13.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −6.9% and imports by −12.5%.
