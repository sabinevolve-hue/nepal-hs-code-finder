Barbados exported $276M of goods and imported $1.7B in 2021, a trade deficit of $1.4B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (24.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (47.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +5.0% and imports by +11.5%.
