🇨🇲 Cameroon: trade in 2019

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #113 exporter, #117 importer of 202 economies (2019).

Year‹

Cameroon exported $4.1B of goods and imported $6.5B in 2019, a trade deficit of $2.5B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (18.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (16.2% of imports).

† Cameroon had not reported some or all of 2019 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
