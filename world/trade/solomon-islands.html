Solomon Islands exported $731M of goods and imported $791M in 2025, a trade deficit of $60M. Its largest export market was 🇲🇾 Malaysia (30.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (37.3% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +58.7% and imports by +18.1%.
