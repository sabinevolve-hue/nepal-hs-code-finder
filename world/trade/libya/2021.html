Libya exported $31B of goods and imported $15B in 2021, a trade surplus of $16B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇹 Italy (21.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇹🇷 Turkiye (18.5% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +266.8% and imports by +34.7%.
