Libya exported $8.4B of goods and imported $11B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.7B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇹 Italy (23.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (16.9% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −71.1% and imports by −26.5%.
