Denmark exported $125B of goods and imported $120B in 2021, a trade surplus of $4.9B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (14.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (20.9% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +16.1% and imports by +22.4%.
