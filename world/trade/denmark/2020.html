Denmark exported $108B of goods and imported $98B in 2020, a trade surplus of $9.5B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (14.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (21.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −1.9% and imports by +0.1%.
