🇵🇭 Philippines: trade in 2022

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #47 exporter, #34 importer of 202 economies (2022).

Year‹

Philippines exported $80B of goods and imported $143B in 2022, a trade deficit of $63B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (16.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (20.2% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +7.2% and imports by +15.0%.

† Philippines had not reported some or all of 2022 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
