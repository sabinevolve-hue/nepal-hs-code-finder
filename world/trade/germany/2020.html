Germany exported $1.38T of goods and imported $1.17T in 2020, a trade surplus of $208B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (8.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (13.7% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −7.0% and imports by −5.0%.
