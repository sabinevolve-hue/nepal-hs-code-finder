Germany exported $1.69T of goods and imported $1.48T in 2023, a trade surplus of $216B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (10.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (14.3% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +1.7% and imports by −6.6%.
