🇩🇪 Germany: trade in 2021

Germany exported $1.62T of goods and imported $1.41T in 2021, a trade surplus of $213B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (8.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (14.0% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +17.7% and imports by +20.5%.
