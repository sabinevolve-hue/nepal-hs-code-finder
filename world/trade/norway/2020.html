Norway exported $85B of goods and imported $81B in 2020, a trade surplus of $3.7B. Its largest export market was 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (17.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (12.1% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −17.4% and imports by −5.4%.
