Colombia exported $30B of goods and imported $43B in 2020, a trade deficit of $12B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (31.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (24.9% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −22.1% and imports by −17.0%.
