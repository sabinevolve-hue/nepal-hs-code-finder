Colombia exported $41B of goods and imported $60B in 2021, a trade deficit of $19B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (28.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.7% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +34.0% and imports by +40.3%.
