Mauritius exported $1.6B of goods and imported $4.8B in 2022, a trade deficit of $3.1B. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (15.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇳 India (12.2% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −1.5% and imports by −7.1%.
