Mauritius exported $1.7B of goods and imported $4.5B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.9B. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (11.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (16.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −11.5% and imports by −19.3%.
