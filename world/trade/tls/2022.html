Timor-Leste exported $472M of goods and imported $933M in 2022, a trade deficit of $461M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (26.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇩 Indonesia (28.8% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +2.9% and imports by +72.1%.
