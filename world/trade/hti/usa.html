🇭🇹 Haiti – 🇺🇸 United States trade, 2025 and 2026

Haiti exported $90M of goods to United States in 2025, and United States exported $1.3B to Haiti. Trade between the two totalled $1.4B, with a $1.2B surplus for United States. Against 2024, Haiti's exports to United States changed by −12.8% and United States's to Haiti by +8.1%.
