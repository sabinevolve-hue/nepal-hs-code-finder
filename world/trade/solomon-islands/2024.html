Solomon Islands exported $460M of goods and imported $670M in 2024, a trade deficit of $209M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (52.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (35.0% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +15.2% and imports by −1.5%.
