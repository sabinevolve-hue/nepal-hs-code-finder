🇸🇧 Solomon Islands: trade in 2021

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #169 exporter, #181 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Year‹

Solomon Islands exported $366M of goods and imported $553M in 2021, a trade deficit of $187M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (54.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (34.0% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −1.5% and imports by +29.6%.

† Solomon Islands had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
