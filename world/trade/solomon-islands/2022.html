🇸🇧 Solomon Islands: trade in 2022

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #170 exporter, #184 importer of 202 economies (2022).

Solomon Islands exported $395M of goods and imported $626M in 2022, a trade deficit of $231M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (47.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (35.9% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +7.9% and imports by +13.2%.

† Solomon Islands had not reported some or all of 2022 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
