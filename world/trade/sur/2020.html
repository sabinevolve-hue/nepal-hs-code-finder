Suriname exported $1.9B of goods and imported $1.5B in 2020, a trade surplus of $392M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (42.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (25.1% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +19.5% and imports by +2.3%.
