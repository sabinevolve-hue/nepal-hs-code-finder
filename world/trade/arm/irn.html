🇦🇲 Armenia – 🇮🇷 Iran trade, 2025 and 2026

Armenia exported $229M of goods to Iran in 2025, and Iran exported $1.7B to Armenia. Trade between the two totalled $1.9B, with a $1.4B surplus for Iran. Against 2024, Armenia's exports to Iran changed by +111.6% and Iran's to Armenia by +179.8%.
