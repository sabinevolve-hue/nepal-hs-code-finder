🇦🇲 Armenia – 🇷🇺 Russia trade, 2025 and 2026

Armenia exported $7.5B of goods to Russia in 2025, and Russia exported $13B to Armenia. Trade between the two totalled $20B, with a $5.3B surplus for Russia. Against 2024, Armenia's exports to Russia changed by +138.7% and Russia's to Armenia by +31.5%.
