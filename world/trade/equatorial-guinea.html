Equatorial Guinea exported $3.3B of goods and imported $1.5B in 2025, a trade surplus of $1.8B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (22.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇬 Nigeria (28.9% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −19.8% and imports by +22.8%.
