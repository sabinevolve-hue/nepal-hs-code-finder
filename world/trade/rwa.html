Rwanda exported $2.2B of goods and imported $5.4B in 2025, a trade deficit of $3.1B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇩 DR Congo (39.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.4% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −27.0% and imports by −16.2%.

Latest product detail: Eurostat for EU members, HM Revenue & Customs for the United Kingdom, and Brazil's trade ministry. Product names and codes: the Harmonized System, © World Customs Organization.
