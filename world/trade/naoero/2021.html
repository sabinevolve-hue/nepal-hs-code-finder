Naoero exported $205M of goods and imported $110M in 2021, a trade surplus of $95M. Its largest export market was 🇹🇭 Thailand (46.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇺 Australia (48.3% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +75.4% and imports by −7.2%.
