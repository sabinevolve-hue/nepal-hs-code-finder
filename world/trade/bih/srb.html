🇧🇦 Bosnia and Herzegovina – 🇷🇸 Serbia trade, 2025 and 2026

Bosnia and Herzegovina exported $672M of goods to Serbia in 2025, and Serbia exported $2.3B to Bosnia and Herzegovina. Trade between the two totalled $3.0B, with a $1.6B surplus for Serbia. Against 2024, Bosnia and Herzegovina's exports to Serbia changed by −34.0% and Serbia's to Bosnia and Herzegovina by +10.3%.
