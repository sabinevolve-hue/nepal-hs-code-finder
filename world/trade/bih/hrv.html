🇧🇦 Bosnia and Herzegovina – 🇭🇷 Croatia trade, 2025 and 2026

Bosnia and Herzegovina exported $1.0B of goods to Croatia in 2025, and Croatia exported $3.1B to Bosnia and Herzegovina. Trade between the two totalled $4.1B, with a $2.0B surplus for Croatia. Against 2024, Bosnia and Herzegovina's exports to Croatia changed by −28.5% and Croatia's to Bosnia and Herzegovina by +5.5%.
