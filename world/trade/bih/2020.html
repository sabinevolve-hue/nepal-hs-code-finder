Bosnia and Herzegovina exported $6.2B of goods and imported $9.9B in 2020, a trade deficit of $3.7B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (15.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (12.3% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −6.5% and imports by −11.6%.
