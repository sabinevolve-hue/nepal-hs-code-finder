🇧🇦 Bosnia and Herzegovina – 🇲🇪 Montenegro trade, 2025 and 2026

Bosnia and Herzegovina exported $235M of goods to Montenegro in 2025, and Montenegro exported $73M to Bosnia and Herzegovina. Trade between the two totalled $307M, with a $162M surplus for Bosnia and Herzegovina. Against 2024, Bosnia and Herzegovina's exports to Montenegro changed by −28.9% and Montenegro's to Bosnia and Herzegovina by +49.5%.
