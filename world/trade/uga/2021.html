Uganda exported $4.0B of goods and imported $9.5B in 2021, a trade deficit of $5.5B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (26.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.4% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −4.4% and imports by +8.3%.
