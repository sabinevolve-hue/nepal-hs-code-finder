Trinidad and Tobago exported $5.5B of goods and imported $4.9B in 2020, a trade surplus of $642M. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (39.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (37.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −23.2% and imports by −21.7%.
