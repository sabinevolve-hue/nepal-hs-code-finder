Trinidad and Tobago exported $7.8B of goods and imported $8.9B in 2023, a trade deficit of $1.2B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (34.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (43.4% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −41.6% and imports by +43.1%.
