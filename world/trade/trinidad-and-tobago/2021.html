Trinidad and Tobago exported $8.4B of goods and imported $5.7B in 2021, a trade surplus of $2.6B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (42.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (34.7% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +51.8% and imports by +17.9%.
