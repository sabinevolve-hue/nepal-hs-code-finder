Laos exported $8.4B of goods and imported $4.7B in 2022, a trade surplus of $3.7B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (39.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇹🇭 Thailand (47.0% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +28.8% and imports by −25.1%.
