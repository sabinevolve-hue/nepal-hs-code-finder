Brazil exported $211B of goods and imported $168B in 2020, a trade surplus of $43B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (32.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (21.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −4.9% and imports by −10.4%.
