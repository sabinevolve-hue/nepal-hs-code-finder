🇧🇷 Brazil: trade in 2021

Brazil exported $282B of goods and imported $233B in 2021, a trade surplus of $49B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (31.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (21.7% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +33.3% and imports by +38.1%.
