🇧🇷 Brazil: trade in 2023

Brazil exported $340B of goods and imported $255B in 2023, a trade surplus of $84B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (30.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (22.1% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +1.6% and imports by −11.7%.
