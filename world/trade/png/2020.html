Papua New Guinea exported $10B of goods and imported $4.4B in 2020, a trade surplus of $5.9B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇺 Australia (21.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇺 Australia (34.9% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +1.7% and imports by −14.4%.
