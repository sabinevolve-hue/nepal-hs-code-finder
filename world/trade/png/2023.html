Papua New Guinea exported $9.9B of goods and imported $6.3B in 2023, a trade surplus of $3.6B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇺 Australia (22.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇺 Australia (27.0% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −9.1% and imports by −3.5%.
