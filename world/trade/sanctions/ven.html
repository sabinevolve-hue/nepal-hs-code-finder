🇻🇪 Venezuela: trade under sanctions, 2019 to 2025

Where Venezuela's goods went and came from each year, as its trading partners recorded it.

Partners recorded $8.3B of goods bought from Venezuela in 2025 and $17B sold to it. In 2019 the figures were $15B and $8.1B. The largest recorded market in 2025 was 🇺🇸 United States, with 40.6% of exports.

How to read these figures. Venezuela does not report complete trade figures to the IMF, so everything here is what its trading partners report, as compiled by the IMF. That leaves gaps. The first two mean the recorded totals understate the real trade.
  • Partners that do not report either. Trade between two economies that both withhold data is mostly missing.
  • Goods declared under another origin. Cargo that is relabelled or transshipped is recorded against the other country, not this one.
  • Valuation. Exports here are partners' import records, which include freight and insurance.
This page reports the recorded figures only. It does not estimate unrecorded trade.
