🇲🇲 Myanmar: trade under sanctions, 2010 to 2025

Where Myanmar's goods went and came from each year, as its trading partners recorded it.

Partners recorded $22B of goods bought from Myanmar in 2025 and $25B sold to it. In 2010 the figures were $6.6B and $8.9B. The largest recorded market in 2025 was 🇨🇳 China, with 36.6% of the total.

Of the 2025 figures, 82% of the exports and 77% of the imports were reported directly by the partner; the IMF estimated the rest for partners that had not reported.

How to read these figures. Myanmar does not report complete trade figures, so every number here, for every year, is what its trading partners record: their imports from it and their exports to it, as collected by the IMF. That keeps the years comparable, and it leaves gaps. The first two mean the recorded totals understate the real trade.
  • Partners that do not report either. Trade between two economies that both withhold data is missing, or is an IMF estimate.
  • Goods declared under another origin. Cargo that is relabelled or transshipped is recorded against the other country, not this one.
  • Valuation. Exports here are partners' import records, which include freight and insurance, so they run a little above the figures on the country profile.
This page reports the recorded figures only. It does not estimate unrecorded trade.
