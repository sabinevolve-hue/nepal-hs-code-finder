🇰🇿 Kazakhstan – 🇹🇷 Turkiye trade, 2025 and 2026

Kazakhstan exported $3.9B of goods to Turkiye in 2025, and Turkiye exported $3.2B to Kazakhstan. Trade between the two totalled $7.1B, with a $708M surplus for Kazakhstan. Against 2024, Kazakhstan's exports to Turkiye changed by +16.7% and Turkiye's to Kazakhstan by −3.8%.
