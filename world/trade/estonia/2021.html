Estonia exported $21B of goods and imported $23B in 2021, a trade deficit of $2.2B. Its largest export market was 🇫🇮 Finland (14.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇫🇮 Finland (14.0% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +32.2% and imports by +36.0%.
