Cabo Verde exported $1.3B of goods and imported $1.3B in 2021, a trade surplus of $83M. Its largest export market was 🇪🇸 Spain (5.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇵🇹 Portugal (36.7% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +211.4% and imports by +12.9%.
