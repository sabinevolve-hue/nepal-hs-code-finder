South Africa exported $86B of goods and imported $73B in 2020, a trade surplus of $13B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (11.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (20.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −4.5% and imports by −22.2%.
