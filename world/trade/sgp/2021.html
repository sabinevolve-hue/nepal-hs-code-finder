Singapore exported $457B of goods and imported $406B in 2021, a trade surplus of $51B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (14.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (13.4% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +58.9% and imports by +55.5%.
