American Samoa exported $18M of goods and imported $126M in 2020, a trade deficit of $109M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇺 Australia (42.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇿 New Zealand (24.2% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −28.7% and imports by −26.4%.

† American Samoa had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
