🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

United Arab Emirates exported $63B of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $39B to United Arab Emirates. Trade between the two totalled $102B, with a $25B surplus for United Arab Emirates. Against 2024, United Arab Emirates's exports to India changed by +10.1% and India's to United Arab Emirates by +2.2%.
