🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates – 🇰🇪 Kenya trade, 2025 and 2026

United Arab Emirates exported $2.4B of goods to Kenya in 2025, and Kenya exported $601M to United Arab Emirates. Trade between the two totalled $3.0B, with a $1.8B surplus for United Arab Emirates. Against 2024, United Arab Emirates's exports to Kenya changed by −0.4% and Kenya's to United Arab Emirates by −20.2%.
