🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates – 🇺🇬 Uganda trade, 2025 and 2026

United Arab Emirates exported $1.1B of goods to Uganda in 2025, and Uganda exported $5.2B to United Arab Emirates. Trade between the two totalled $6.2B, with a $4.1B surplus for Uganda. Against 2024, United Arab Emirates's exports to Uganda changed by −12.5% and Uganda's to United Arab Emirates by +98.5%.
