🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates – 🇵🇰 Pakistan trade, 2025 and 2026

United Arab Emirates exported $6.6B of goods to Pakistan in 2025, and Pakistan exported $1.9B to United Arab Emirates. Trade between the two totalled $8.5B, with a $4.6B surplus for United Arab Emirates. Against 2024, United Arab Emirates's exports to Pakistan changed by +14.2% and Pakistan's to United Arab Emirates by +4.4%.
