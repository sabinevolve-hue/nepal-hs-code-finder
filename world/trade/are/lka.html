🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates – 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka trade, 2025 and 2026

United Arab Emirates exported $1.3B of goods to Sri Lanka in 2025, and Sri Lanka exported $353M to United Arab Emirates. Trade between the two totalled $1.7B, with a $983M surplus for United Arab Emirates. Against 2024, United Arab Emirates's exports to Sri Lanka changed by +4.6% and Sri Lanka's to United Arab Emirates by +3.5%.
