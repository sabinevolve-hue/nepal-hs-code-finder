Colombia exported $56B of goods and imported $76B in 2022, a trade deficit of $19B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (27.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (25.0% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +38.3% and imports by +26.6%.
