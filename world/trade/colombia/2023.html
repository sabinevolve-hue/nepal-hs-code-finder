Colombia exported $49B of goods and imported $62B in 2023, a trade deficit of $12B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (28.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (26.3% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −12.8% and imports by −18.9%.
