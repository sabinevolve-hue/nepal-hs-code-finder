Hong Kong exported $576B of goods and imported $656B in 2023, a trade deficit of $79B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (57.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (40.0% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −5.8% and imports by −2.0%.
