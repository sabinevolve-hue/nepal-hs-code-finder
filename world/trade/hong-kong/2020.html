Hong Kong exported $553B of goods and imported $574B in 2020, a trade deficit of $21B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (55.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (43.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +3.2% and imports by −0.9%.
