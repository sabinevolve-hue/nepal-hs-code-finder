Botswana exported $3.7B of goods and imported $6.3B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.5B. Its largest export market was 🇧🇪 Belgium (22.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (59.2% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −26.1% and imports by −2.6%.
