🇧🇼 Botswana: trade in 2021

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #100 exporter, #115 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019
›

Botswana exported $7.0B of goods and imported $7.5B in 2021, a trade deficit of $553M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (25.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (56.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +87.2% and imports by +20.6%.

† Botswana had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
