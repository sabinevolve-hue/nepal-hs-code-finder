🇧🇴 Bolivia – 🇨🇱 Chile trade, 2025 and 2026

Bolivia exported $120M of goods to Chile in 2025, and Chile exported $1.0B to Bolivia. Trade between the two totalled $1.1B, with a $906M surplus for Chile. Against 2024, Bolivia's exports to Chile changed by −8.4% and Chile's to Bolivia by −1.4%.
