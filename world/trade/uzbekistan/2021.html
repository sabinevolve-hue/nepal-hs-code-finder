Uzbekistan exported $14B of goods and imported $24B in 2021, a trade deficit of $9.7B. Its largest export market was 🇹🇷 Turkiye (14.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (21.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +7.3% and imports by +19.0%.
