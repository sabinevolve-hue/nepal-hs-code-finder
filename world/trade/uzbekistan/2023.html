Uzbekistan exported $72B of goods and imported $119B in 2023, a trade deficit of $47B. Its largest export market was 🇷🇺 Russia (12.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (28.6% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +366.5% and imports by +321.8%.
