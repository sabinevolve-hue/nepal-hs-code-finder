What stands out: Naoero

  • Its three largest markets (🇹🇭 Thailand, 🇰🇷 South Korea, 🇵🇭 Philippines) took 91% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 68 other partners.
  • One product chapter, fish and crustaceans, molluscs and other aquatic invertebrates (HS 03), made up 76% of what it exported in 2024.
  • Exports in 2025 were 5 times their 2000 level ($33M then, $170M now), in current dollars.
