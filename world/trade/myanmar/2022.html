Myanmar exported $17B of goods and imported $17B in 2022, a trade deficit of $341M. Its largest export market was 🇹🇭 Thailand (22.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (32.1% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +12.7% and imports by +21.5%.
