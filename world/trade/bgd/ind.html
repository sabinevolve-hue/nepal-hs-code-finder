🇧🇩 Bangladesh – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

Bangladesh exported $1.7B of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $11B to Bangladesh. Trade between the two totalled $13B, with a $9.6B surplus for India. Against 2024, Bangladesh's exports to India changed by +0.5% and India's to Bangladesh by −2.1%.
