🇧🇩 Bangladesh – 🇨🇦 Canada trade, 2025 and 2026

Bangladesh exported $1.5B of goods to Canada in 2025, and Canada exported $949M to Bangladesh. Trade between the two totalled $2.4B, with a $502M surplus for Bangladesh. Against 2024, Bangladesh's exports to Canada changed by +13.1% and Canada's to Bangladesh by +23.5%.
