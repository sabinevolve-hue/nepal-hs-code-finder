Bangladesh exported $38B of goods and imported $67B in 2021, a trade deficit of $29B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (19.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (25.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +26.5% and imports by +46.7%.
