Sri Lanka exported $9.9B of goods and imported $16B in 2020, a trade deficit of $5.7B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (25.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.0% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −15.5% and imports by −20.0%.
