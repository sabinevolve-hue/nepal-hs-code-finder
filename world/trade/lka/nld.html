🇱🇰 Sri Lanka – 🇳🇱 Netherlands trade, 2025 and 2026

Sri Lanka exported $469M of goods to Netherlands in 2025, and Netherlands exported $114M to Sri Lanka. Trade between the two totalled $582M, with a $355M surplus for Sri Lanka. Against 2024, Sri Lanka's exports to Netherlands changed by +20.5% and Netherlands's to Sri Lanka by +11.8%.
