🇱🇰 Sri Lanka – 🇲🇾 Malaysia trade, 2025 and 2026

Sri Lanka exported $76M of goods to Malaysia in 2025, and Malaysia exported $1.2B to Sri Lanka. Trade between the two totalled $1.3B, with a $1.1B surplus for Malaysia. Against 2024, Sri Lanka's exports to Malaysia changed by −15.9% and Malaysia's to Sri Lanka by +66.5%.
