Chad exported $1.7B of goods and imported $1.1B in 2020, a trade surplus of $622M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (26.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (31.2% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +32.7% and imports by +2.5%.
