Chad exported $4.2B of goods and imported $1.5B in 2023, a trade surplus of $2.8B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (24.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (27.6% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −7.5% and imports by +23.3%.
