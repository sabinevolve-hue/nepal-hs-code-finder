Chad exported $4.6B of goods and imported $1.2B in 2022, a trade surplus of $3.4B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (25.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (25.3% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +59.5% and imports by +6.8%.
