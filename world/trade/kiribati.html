Kiribati exported $8M of goods and imported $310M in 2025, a trade deficit of $302M. Its largest export market was 🇯🇵 Japan (53.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (35.9% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +43.9% and imports by +51.5%.
