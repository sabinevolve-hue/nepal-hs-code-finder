🇦🇴 Angola: trade in 2021

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #63 exporter, #96 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Angola exported $35B of goods and imported $12B in 2021, a trade surplus of $24B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (58.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (14.9% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +66.9% and imports by +23.4%.
