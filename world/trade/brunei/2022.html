🇧🇳 Brunei: trade in 2022

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #77 exporter, #107 importer of 202 economies (2022).

Brunei exported $18B of goods and imported $12B in 2022, a trade surplus of $6.6B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇺 Australia (27.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇲🇾 Malaysia (18.6% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +67.6% and imports by +39.0%.
