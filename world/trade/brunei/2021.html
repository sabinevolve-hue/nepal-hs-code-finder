Brunei exported $11B of goods and imported $8.5B in 2021, a trade surplus of $2.5B. Its largest export market was 🇸🇬 Singapore (21.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇲🇾 Malaysia (22.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +66.7% and imports by +59.1%.
