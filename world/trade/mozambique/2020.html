🇲🇿 Mozambique: trade in 2020

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #118 exporter, #112 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Year‹

Mozambique exported $3.7B of goods and imported $6.5B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.8B. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (23.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (30.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −22.3% and imports by −15.4%.
