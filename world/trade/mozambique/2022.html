Mozambique exported $8.3B of goods and imported $15B in 2022, a trade deficit of $6.4B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (21.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇰🇷 South Korea (32.0% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +45.5% and imports by +67.5%.
