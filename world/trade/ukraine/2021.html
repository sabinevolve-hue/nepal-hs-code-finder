Ukraine exported $66B of goods and imported $70B in 2021, a trade deficit of $4.1B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (12.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (15.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +33.8% and imports by +30.3%.
