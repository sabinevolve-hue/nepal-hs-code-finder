Ukraine exported $36B of goods and imported $64B in 2023, a trade deficit of $27B. Its largest export market was 🇵🇱 Poland (13.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (16.4% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −18.9% and imports by +15.0%.
