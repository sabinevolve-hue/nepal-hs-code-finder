Malaysia exported $299B of goods and imported $239B in 2021, a trade surplus of $60B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (15.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +27.8% and imports by +25.7%.
