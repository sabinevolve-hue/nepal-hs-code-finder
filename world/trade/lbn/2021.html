Lebanon exported $4.1B of goods and imported $14B in 2021, a trade deficit of $9.7B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (20.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇹🇷 Turkiye (11.0% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +1.4% and imports by +23.6%.
