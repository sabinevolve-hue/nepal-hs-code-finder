🇬🇳 Guinea: trade in 2023

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #118 exporter, #132 importer of 202 economies (2023).

Guinea exported $5.1B of goods and imported $5.4B in 2023, a trade deficit of $307M. Its largest export market was 🇬🇭 Ghana (26.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.7% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −17.2% and imports by +7.1%.

† Guinea had not reported some or all of 2023 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
