Equatorial Guinea exported $4.2B of goods and imported $1.2B in 2024, a trade surplus of $2.9B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (22.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (14.7% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by −10.4% and imports by +10.8%.
