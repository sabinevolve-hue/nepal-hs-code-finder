Equatorial Guinea exported $4.6B of goods and imported $1.1B in 2023, a trade surplus of $3.5B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (27.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (20.1% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −38.5% and imports by +8.9%.
