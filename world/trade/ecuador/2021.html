Ecuador exported $27B of goods and imported $26B in 2021, a trade surplus of $1.0B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (24.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.5% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +31.3% and imports by +43.4%.
