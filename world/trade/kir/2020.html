Kiribati exported $9M of goods and imported $109M in 2020, a trade deficit of $100M. Its largest export market was 🇯🇵 Japan (31.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇫🇯 Fiji (18.0% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −46.7% and imports by −1.6%.
