🇧🇼 Botswana – 🇿🇲 Zambia trade, 2025 and 2026

Botswana exported $39M of goods to Zambia in 2025, and Zambia exported $188M to Botswana. Trade between the two totalled $227M, with a $149M surplus for Zambia. Against 2024, Botswana's exports to Zambia changed by +248.5% and Zambia's to Botswana by +46.7%.
