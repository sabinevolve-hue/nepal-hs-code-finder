🇧🇼 Botswana – 🇿🇦 South Africa trade, 2025 and 2026

Botswana exported $438M of goods to South Africa in 2025, and South Africa exported $4.1B to Botswana. Trade between the two totalled $4.6B, with a $3.7B surplus for South Africa. Against 2024, Botswana's exports to South Africa changed by −20.8% and South Africa's to Botswana by −4.6%.
