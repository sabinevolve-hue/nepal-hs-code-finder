Luxembourg exported $17B of goods and imported $26B in 2023, a trade deficit of $9.3B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (26.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇧🇪 Belgium (34.5% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −2.0% and imports by −2.9%.
