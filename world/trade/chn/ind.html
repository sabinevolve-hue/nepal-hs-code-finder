🇨🇳 China – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $136B of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $18B to China. Trade between the two totalled $154B, with a $118B surplus for China. Against 2024, China's exports to India changed by +12.7% and India's to China by +19.4%.
