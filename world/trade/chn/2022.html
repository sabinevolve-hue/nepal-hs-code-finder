China exported $3.60T of goods and imported $2.72T in 2022, a trade surplus of $889B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (16.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was Taiwan (8.8% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +7.0% and imports by +1.4%.
