🇨🇳 China – 🇩🇿 Algeria trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $14B of goods to Algeria in 2025, and Algeria exported $3.3B to China. Trade between the two totalled $17B, with a $11B surplus for China. Against 2024, China's exports to Algeria changed by +19.0% and Algeria's to China by +64.5%.
