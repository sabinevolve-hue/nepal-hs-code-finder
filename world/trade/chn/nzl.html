🇨🇳 China – 🇳🇿 New Zealand trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $8.1B of goods to New Zealand in 2025, and New Zealand exported $20B to China. Trade between the two totalled $28B, with a $12B surplus for New Zealand. Against 2024, China's exports to New Zealand changed by +4.1% and New Zealand's to China by +86.8%.
