🇨🇳 China – 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $5.8B of goods to Sri Lanka in 2025, and Sri Lanka exported $282M to China. Trade between the two totalled $6.0B, with a $5.5B surplus for China. Against 2024, China's exports to Sri Lanka changed by +15.2% and Sri Lanka's to China by +8.0%.
