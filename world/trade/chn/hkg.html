🇨🇳 China – 🇭🇰 Hong Kong trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $337B of goods to Hong Kong in 2025, and Hong Kong exported $454B to China. Trade between the two totalled $791B, with a $117B surplus for Hong Kong. Against 2024, China's exports to Hong Kong changed by +15.2% and Hong Kong's to China by +20.0%.
