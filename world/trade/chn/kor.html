🇨🇳 China – 🇰🇷 South Korea trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $145B of goods to South Korea in 2025, and South Korea exported $131B to China. Trade between the two totalled $275B, with a $14B surplus for China. Against 2024, China's exports to South Korea changed by −1.9% and South Korea's to China by −1.7%.
