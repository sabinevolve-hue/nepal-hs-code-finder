🇨🇳 China – 🇷🇺 Russia trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $103B of goods to Russia in 2025, and Russia exported $108B to China. Trade between the two totalled $212B, with a $4.8B surplus for Russia. Against 2024, China's exports to Russia changed by −10.5% and Russia's to China by −3.4%.
