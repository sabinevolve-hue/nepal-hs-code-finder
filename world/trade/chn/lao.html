🇨🇳 China – 🇱🇦 Laos trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $4.3B of goods to Laos in 2025, and Laos exported $7.2B to China. Trade between the two totalled $12B, with a $2.9B surplus for Laos. Against 2024, China's exports to Laos changed by +17.6% and Laos's to China by +68.6%.
