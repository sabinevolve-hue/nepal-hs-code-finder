🇨🇳 China – 🇻🇪 Venezuela trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $5.3B of goods to Venezuela in 2025, and Venezuela exported $868M to China. Trade between the two totalled $6.2B, with a $4.5B surplus for China. Against 2024, China's exports to Venezuela changed by +10.9% and Venezuela's to China by −21.5%.
