🇨🇳 China – 🇮🇷 Iran trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $6.9B of goods to Iran in 2025, and Iran exported $2.9B to China. Trade between the two totalled $9.8B, with a $4.1B surplus for China. Against 2024, China's exports to Iran changed by −22.4% and Iran's to China by −31.7%.
