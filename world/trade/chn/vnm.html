🇨🇳 China – 🇻🇳 Vietnam trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $199B of goods to Vietnam in 2025, and Vietnam exported $72B to China. Trade between the two totalled $270B, with a $127B surplus for China. Against 2024, China's exports to Vietnam changed by +22.4% and Vietnam's to China by +17.5%.
