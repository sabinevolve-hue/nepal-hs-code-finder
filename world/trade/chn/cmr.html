🇨🇳 China – 🇨🇲 Cameroon trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $4.3B of goods to Cameroon in 2025, and Cameroon exported $240M to China. Trade between the two totalled $4.6B, with a $4.1B surplus for China. Against 2024, China's exports to Cameroon changed by +19.0% and Cameroon's to China by −69.3%.
