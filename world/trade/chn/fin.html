🇨🇳 China – 🇫🇮 Finland trade, 2025 and 2026

China exported $3.7B of goods to Finland in 2025, and Finland exported $3.8B to China. Trade between the two totalled $7.5B, with a $109M surplus for Finland. Against 2024, China's exports to Finland changed by −0.8% and Finland's to China by −0.1%.
