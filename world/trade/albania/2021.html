🇦🇱 Albania: trade in 2021

Albania exported $3.7B of goods and imported $8.1B in 2021, a trade deficit of $4.4B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇹 Italy (42.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇹 Italy (24.3% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +35.5% and imports by +34.5%.
