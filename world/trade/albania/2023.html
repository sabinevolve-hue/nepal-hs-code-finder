Albania exported $4.4B of goods and imported $8.7B in 2023, a trade deficit of $4.3B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇹 Italy (42.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇹 Italy (22.2% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +1.5% and imports by +3.5%.
