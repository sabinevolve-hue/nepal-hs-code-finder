Sierra Leone exported $342M of goods and imported $1.7B in 2023, a trade deficit of $1.4B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (62.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.6% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −28.9% and imports by −4.8%.
