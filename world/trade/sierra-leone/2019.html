🇸🇱 Sierra Leone: trade in 2019

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #170 exporter, #168 importer of 202 economies (2019).

Sierra Leone exported $381M of goods and imported $1.2B in 2019, a trade deficit of $780M. Its largest export market was 🇲🇰 North Macedonia (42.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (19.4% of imports).

† Sierra Leone had not reported some or all of 2019 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
