🇲🇾 Malaysia – 🇸🇬 Singapore trade, 2025 and 2026

Malaysia exported $81B of goods to Singapore in 2025, and Singapore exported $57B to Malaysia. Trade between the two totalled $138B, with a $24B surplus for Malaysia. Against 2024, Malaysia's exports to Singapore changed by +60.6% and Singapore's to Malaysia by +8.9%.
