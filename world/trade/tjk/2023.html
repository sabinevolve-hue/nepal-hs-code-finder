Tajikistan exported $1.3B of goods and imported $4.3B in 2023, a trade deficit of $2.9B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (23.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇷🇺 Russia (21.2% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −21.4% and imports by −18.0%.
