Chad exported $4.0B of goods and imported $1.8B in 2025, a trade surplus of $2.3B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (33.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (32.3% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −10.5% and imports by +11.4%.
