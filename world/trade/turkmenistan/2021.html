🇹🇲 Turkmenistan: trade in 2021

Europe & Central Asia · World rank #93 exporter, #138 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Turkmenistan exported $9.4B of goods and imported $4.1B in 2021, a trade surplus of $5.3B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (68.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇹🇷 Turkiye (25.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +45.8% and imports by +28.7%.

† Turkmenistan had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
