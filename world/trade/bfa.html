Burkina Faso exported $10B of goods and imported $7.0B in 2025, a trade surplus of $3.4B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (45.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (20.5% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +95.9% and imports by +8.5%.
