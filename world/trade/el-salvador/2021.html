El Salvador exported $6.4B of goods and imported $15B in 2021, a trade deficit of $8.2B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (40.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (27.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +33.1% and imports by +47.9%.
