Afghanistan exported $469M of goods and imported $3.0B in 2023, a trade deficit of $2.6B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (45.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇷 Iran (26.2% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +2.8% and imports by −5.4%.
