🇲🇺 Mauritius – 🇿🇦 South Africa trade, 2025 and 2026

Mauritius exported $206M of goods to South Africa in 2025, and South Africa exported $453M to Mauritius. Trade between the two totalled $660M, with a $247M surplus for South Africa. Against 2024, Mauritius's exports to South Africa changed by +2.5% and South Africa's to Mauritius by −9.5%.
