Mauritius exported $1.7B of goods and imported $5.1B in 2021, a trade deficit of $3.5B. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (13.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.8% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +0.0% and imports by +13.7%.
