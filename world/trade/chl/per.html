🇨🇱 Chile – 🇵🇪 Peru trade, 2025 and 2026

Chile exported $1.6B of goods to Peru in 2025, and Peru exported $2.3B to Chile. Trade between the two totalled $3.9B, with a $750M surplus for Peru. Against 2024, Chile's exports to Peru changed by +6.3% and Peru's to Chile by +10.8%.
