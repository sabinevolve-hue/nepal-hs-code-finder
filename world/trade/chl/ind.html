🇨🇱 Chile – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

Chile exported $4.2B of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $1.2B to Chile. Trade between the two totalled $5.5B, with a $3.0B surplus for Chile. Against 2024, Chile's exports to India changed by +64.8% and India's to Chile by +6.5%.
