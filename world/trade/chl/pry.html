🇨🇱 Chile – 🇵🇾 Paraguay trade, 2025 and 2026

Chile exported $706M of goods to Paraguay in 2025, and Paraguay exported $979M to Chile. Trade between the two totalled $1.7B, with a $273M surplus for Paraguay. Against 2024, Chile's exports to Paraguay changed by +12.5% and Paraguay's to Chile by +10.5%.
