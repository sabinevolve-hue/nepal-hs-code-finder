Rwanda exported $435M of goods and imported $2.9B in 2023, a trade deficit of $2.5B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇩 DR Congo (43.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (28.0% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −72.2% and imports by −23.8%.
