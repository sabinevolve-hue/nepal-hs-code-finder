Rwanda exported $1.6B of goods and imported $4.2B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.6B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (39.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.1% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +41.4% and imports by +31.9%.
