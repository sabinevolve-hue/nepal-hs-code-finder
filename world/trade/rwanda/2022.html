Rwanda exported $1.6B of goods and imported $3.9B in 2022, a trade deficit of $2.3B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇩 DR Congo (37.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.5% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +30.8% and imports by +15.9%.
