Chile exported $110B of goods and imported $93B in 2025, a trade surplus of $17B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (36.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (28.5% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +11.6% and imports by +10.4%.

What stands out: Chile

  • Its three largest markets (🇨🇳 China, 🇺🇸 United States, 🇯🇵 Japan) took 61% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 159 other partners.
  • One product chapter, ores, slag and ash (HS 26), made up 34% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It was the world's largest exporter of 8 of its main products in 2024, among them Copper ores and concentrates 2603.00.
  • Exports in 2025 were 6 times their 2000 level ($19B then, $110B now), in current dollars.
