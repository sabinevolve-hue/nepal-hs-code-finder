🇬🇷 Greece – 🇱🇾 Libya trade, 2025 and 2026

Greece exported $894M of goods to Libya in 2025, and Libya exported $2.0B to Greece. Trade between the two totalled $2.9B, with a $1.1B surplus for Libya. Against 2024, Greece's exports to Libya changed by −24.6% and Libya's to Greece by −23.1%.
