🇩🇪 Germany – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

Germany exported $18B of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $11B to Germany. Trade between the two totalled $30B, with a $7.1B surplus for Germany. Against 2024, Germany's exports to India changed by +0.6% and India's to Germany by +8.1%.
