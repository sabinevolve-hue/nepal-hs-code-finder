🇩🇪 Germany – 🇮🇶 Iraq trade, 2025 and 2026

Germany exported $1.6B of goods to Iraq in 2025, and Iraq exported $1.1B to Germany. Trade between the two totalled $2.7B, with a $500M surplus for Germany. Against 2024, Germany's exports to Iraq changed by +12.0% and Iraq's to Germany by −12.0%.
