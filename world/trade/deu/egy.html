🇩🇪 Germany – 🇪🇬 Egypt trade, 2025 and 2026

Germany exported $5.0B of goods to Egypt in 2025, and Egypt exported $1.2B to Germany. Trade between the two totalled $6.2B, with a $3.7B surplus for Germany. Against 2024, Germany's exports to Egypt changed by +17.3% and Egypt's to Germany by +24.3%.
