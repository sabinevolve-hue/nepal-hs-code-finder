🇩🇪 Germany – 🇪🇪 Estonia trade, 2025 and 2026

Germany exported $2.8B of goods to Estonia in 2025, and Estonia exported $1.6B to Germany. Trade between the two totalled $4.4B, with a $1.2B surplus for Germany. Against 2024, Germany's exports to Estonia changed by +4.5% and Estonia's to Germany by +15.7%.
