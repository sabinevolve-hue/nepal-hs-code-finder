🇩🇪 Germany – 🇳🇿 New Zealand trade, 2025 and 2026

Germany exported $1.6B of goods to New Zealand in 2025, and New Zealand exported $1.2B to Germany. Trade between the two totalled $2.8B, with a $369M surplus for Germany. Against 2024, Germany's exports to New Zealand changed by −3.3% and New Zealand's to Germany by +153.6%.
