🇩🇪 Germany – 🇬🇧 United Kingdom trade, 2025 and 2026

Germany exported $90B of goods to United Kingdom in 2025, and United Kingdom exported $44B to Germany. Trade between the two totalled $135B, with a $46B surplus for Germany. Against 2024, Germany's exports to United Kingdom changed by +3.8% and United Kingdom's to Germany by +8.6%.
