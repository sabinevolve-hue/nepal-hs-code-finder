🇩🇪 Germany – 🇺🇸 United States trade, 2025 and 2026

Germany exported $165B of goods to United States in 2025, and United States exported $83B to Germany. Trade between the two totalled $248B, with a $82B surplus for Germany. Against 2024, Germany's exports to United States changed by −5.2% and United States's to Germany by +9.9%.
