🇩🇪 Germany – 🇱🇷 Liberia trade, 2025 and 2026

Germany exported $883M of goods to Liberia in 2025, and Liberia exported $11M to Germany. Trade between the two totalled $894M, with a $873M surplus for Germany. Against 2024, Germany's exports to Liberia changed by +91.8% and Liberia's to Germany by +17769.5%.
