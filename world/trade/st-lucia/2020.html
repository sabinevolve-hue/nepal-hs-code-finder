St. Lucia exported $65M of goods and imported $566M in 2020, a trade deficit of $501M. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (29.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (37.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −31.3% and imports by −8.6%.
