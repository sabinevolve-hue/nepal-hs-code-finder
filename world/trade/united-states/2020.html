United States exported $1.43T of goods and imported $2.33T in 2020, a trade deficit of $901B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇦 Canada (17.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.6% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −13.1% and imports by −6.4%.
