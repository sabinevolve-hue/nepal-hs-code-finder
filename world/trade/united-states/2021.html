United States exported $1.76T of goods and imported $2.83T in 2021, a trade deficit of $1.07T. Its largest export market was 🇨🇦 Canada (17.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.8% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +22.9% and imports by +21.3%.
