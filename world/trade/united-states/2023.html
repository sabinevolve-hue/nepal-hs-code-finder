United States exported $2.02T of goods and imported $3.08T in 2023, a trade deficit of $1.06T. Its largest export market was 🇨🇦 Canada (17.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇲🇽 Mexico (15.4% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −2.2% and imports by −4.9%.
