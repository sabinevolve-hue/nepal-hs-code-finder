🇳🇴 Norway – 🇵🇱 Poland trade, 2025 and 2026

Norway exported $10B of goods to Poland in 2025, and Poland exported $4.0B to Norway. Trade between the two totalled $14B, with a $6.1B surplus for Norway. Against 2024, Norway's exports to Poland changed by +2.8% and Poland's to Norway by +24.8%.
