🇯🇵 Japan – 🇿🇦 South Africa trade, 2025 and 2026

Japan exported $2.3B of goods to South Africa in 2025, and South Africa exported $5.5B to Japan. Trade between the two totalled $7.8B, with a $3.2B surplus for South Africa. Against 2024, Japan's exports to South Africa changed by +10.0% and South Africa's to Japan by +10.9%.
