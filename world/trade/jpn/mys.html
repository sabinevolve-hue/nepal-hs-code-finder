🇯🇵 Japan – 🇲🇾 Malaysia trade, 2025 and 2026

Japan exported $14B of goods to Malaysia in 2025, and Malaysia exported $20B to Japan. Trade between the two totalled $34B, with a $5.7B surplus for Malaysia. Against 2024, Japan's exports to Malaysia changed by +1.6% and Malaysia's to Japan by +9.6%.
