🇯🇵 Japan – 🇳🇿 New Zealand trade, 2025 and 2026

Japan exported $2.2B of goods to New Zealand in 2025, and New Zealand exported $4.0B to Japan. Trade between the two totalled $6.2B, with a $1.8B surplus for New Zealand. Against 2024, Japan's exports to New Zealand changed by −5.7% and New Zealand's to Japan by +72.9%.
