🇹🇿 Tanzania – 🇿🇦 South Africa trade, 2025 and 2026

Tanzania exported $2.9B of goods to South Africa in 2025, and South Africa exported $605M to Tanzania. Trade between the two totalled $3.5B, with a $2.3B surplus for Tanzania. Against 2024, Tanzania's exports to South Africa changed by +26.8% and South Africa's to Tanzania by +11.1%.
