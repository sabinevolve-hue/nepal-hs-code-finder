Zimbabwe exported $9.5B of goods and imported $11B in 2025, a trade deficit of $1.7B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (33.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (37.9% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +27.5% and imports by +10.2%.
