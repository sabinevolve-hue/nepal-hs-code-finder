🇪🇹 Ethiopia – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

Ethiopia exported $347M of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $471M to Ethiopia. Trade between the two totalled $817M, with a $124M surplus for India. Against 2024, Ethiopia's exports to India changed by +169.9% and India's to Ethiopia by +4.0%.
