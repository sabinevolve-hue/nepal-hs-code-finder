Ethiopia exported $2.5B of goods and imported $14B in 2020, a trade deficit of $12B. Its largest export market was 🇸🇴 Somalia, Fed. Rep. (11.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (29.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −5.6% and imports by −9.3%.

† Ethiopia had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
