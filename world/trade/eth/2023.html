Ethiopia exported $2.9B of goods and imported $17B in 2023, a trade deficit of $14B. Its largest export market was 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia (10.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (32.7% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −7.3% and imports by +3.1%.
