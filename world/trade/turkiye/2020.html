Turkiye exported $170B of goods and imported $220B in 2020, a trade deficit of $50B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (9.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (10.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −6.2% and imports by +4.4%.
