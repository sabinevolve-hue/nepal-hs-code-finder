🇰🇮 Kiribati: trade in 2022

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #201 exporter, #200 importer of 202 economies (2022).

Kiribati exported $11M of goods and imported $179M in 2022, a trade deficit of $168M. Its largest export market was 🇯🇵 Japan (36.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (16.8% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +29.5% and imports by +2.9%.

† Kiribati had not reported some or all of 2022 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
