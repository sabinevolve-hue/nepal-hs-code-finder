🇰🇮 Kiribati: trade in 2019

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #197 exporter, #199 importer of 202 economies (2019).

Year‹

Kiribati exported $17M of goods and imported $111M in 2019, a trade deficit of $94M. Its largest export market was 🇯🇵 Japan (28.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇬 Singapore (24.3% of imports).

† Kiribati had not reported some or all of 2019 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
