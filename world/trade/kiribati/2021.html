Kiribati exported $8M of goods and imported $174M in 2021, a trade deficit of $165M. Its largest export market was 🇳🇨 New Caledonia (28.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇫🇯 Fiji (16.3% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −9.0% and imports by +59.1%.

† Kiribati had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
