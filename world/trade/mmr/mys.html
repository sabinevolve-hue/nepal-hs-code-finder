🇲🇲 Myanmar – 🇲🇾 Malaysia trade, 2025 and 2026

Myanmar exported $175M of goods to Malaysia in 2025, and Malaysia exported $1.1B to Myanmar. Trade between the two totalled $1.3B, with a $949M surplus for Malaysia. Against 2024, Myanmar's exports to Malaysia changed by −26.4% and Malaysia's to Myanmar by +63.9%.
