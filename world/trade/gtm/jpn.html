🇬🇹 Guatemala – 🇯🇵 Japan trade, 2025 and 2026

Guatemala exported $186M of goods to Japan in 2025, and Japan exported $521M to Guatemala. Trade between the two totalled $706M, with a $335M surplus for Japan. Against 2024, Guatemala's exports to Japan changed by +50.7% and Japan's to Guatemala by +6.5%.
