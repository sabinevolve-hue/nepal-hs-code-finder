Burkina Faso exported $4.5B of goods and imported $5.5B in 2022, a trade deficit of $994M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (69.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (11.9% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −9.9% and imports by +20.1%.
