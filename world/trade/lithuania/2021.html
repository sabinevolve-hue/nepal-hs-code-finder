Lithuania exported $41B of goods and imported $44B in 2021, a trade deficit of $3.9B. Its largest export market was 🇷🇺 Russia (10.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (12.8% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +24.1% and imports by +33.5%.
