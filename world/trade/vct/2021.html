St. Vincent and the Grenadines exported $53M of goods and imported $372M in 2021, a trade deficit of $319M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇴 Angola (34.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (46.8% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −58.9% and imports by +16.0%.
