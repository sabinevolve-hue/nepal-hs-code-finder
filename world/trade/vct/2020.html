St. Vincent and the Grenadines exported $130M of goods and imported $321M in 2020, a trade deficit of $191M. Its largest export market was 🇯🇴 Jordan (56.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (43.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +12.6% and imports by −4.2%.
