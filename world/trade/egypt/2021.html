🇪🇬 Egypt: trade in 2021

Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan · World rank #55 exporter, #45 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Egypt exported $41B of goods and imported $74B in 2021, a trade deficit of $33B. Its largest export market was 🇹🇷 Turkiye (7.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (15.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +51.7% and imports by +22.4%.
