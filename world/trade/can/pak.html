🇨🇦 Canada – 🇵🇰 Pakistan trade, 2025 and 2026

Canada exported $359M of goods to Pakistan in 2025, and Pakistan exported $389M to Canada. Trade between the two totalled $748M, with a $31M surplus for Pakistan. Against 2024, Canada's exports to Pakistan changed by +61.9% and Pakistan's to Canada by −3.3%.
