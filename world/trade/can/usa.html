🇨🇦 Canada – 🇺🇸 United States trade, 2025 and 2026

Canada exported $404B of goods to United States in 2025, and United States exported $337B to Canada. Trade between the two totalled $740B, with a $67B surplus for Canada. Against 2024, Canada's exports to United States changed by −7.2% and United States's to Canada by −3.7%.
