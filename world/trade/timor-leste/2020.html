🇹🇱 Timor-Leste: trade in 2020

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #171 exporter, #182 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019

Timor-Leste exported $252M of goods and imported $497M in 2020, a trade deficit of $245M. Its largest export market was 🇯🇵 Japan (28.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇩 Indonesia (37.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +69.2% and imports by −7.2%.

† Timor-Leste had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
