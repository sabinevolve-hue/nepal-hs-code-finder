🇹🇱 Timor-Leste: trade in 2021

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #167 exporter, #182 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019

Timor-Leste exported $459M of goods and imported $542M in 2021, a trade deficit of $83M. Its largest export market was 🇸🇬 Singapore (28.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇩 Indonesia (35.4% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +82.1% and imports by +9.1%.

† Timor-Leste had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
