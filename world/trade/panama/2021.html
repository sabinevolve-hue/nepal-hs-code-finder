Panama exported $3.7B of goods and imported $10B in 2021, a trade deficit of $6.5B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (32.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (33.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +115.4% and imports by +57.9%.
