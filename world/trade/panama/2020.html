Panama exported $1.7B of goods and imported $6.5B in 2020, a trade deficit of $4.8B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (21.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (32.0% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +142.1% and imports by −34.0%.
