Togo exported $1.3B of goods and imported $2.8B in 2022, a trade deficit of $1.4B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (16.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (19.7% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +27.0% and imports by +9.4%.
