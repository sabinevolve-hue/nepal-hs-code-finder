Ireland exported $179B of goods and imported $96B in 2020, a trade surplus of $83B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (30.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (28.2% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +6.1% and imports by −2.0%.
