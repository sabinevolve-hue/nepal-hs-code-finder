Ireland exported $190B of goods and imported $119B in 2021, a trade surplus of $71B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (31.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (19.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +5.8% and imports by +23.0%.
