Ireland exported $213B of goods and imported $146B in 2022, a trade surplus of $67B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (29.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (21.1% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +12.2% and imports by +23.2%.
