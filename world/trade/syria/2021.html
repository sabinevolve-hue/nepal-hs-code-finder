Syria exported $3.2B of goods and imported $4.4B in 2021, a trade deficit of $1.2B. Its largest export market was 🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia (22.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (8.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +15.6% and imports by −22.7%.

† Syria had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
