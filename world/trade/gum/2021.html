Guam exported $153M of goods and imported $904M in 2021, a trade deficit of $750M. Its largest export market was 🇫🇲 Micronesia, Fed. Sts. (81.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇬 Singapore (50.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −9.9% and imports by −59.9%.
