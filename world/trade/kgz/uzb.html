🇰🇬 Kyrgyzstan – 🇺🇿 Uzbekistan trade, 2025 and 2026

Kyrgyzstan exported $405M of goods to Uzbekistan in 2025, and Uzbekistan exported $1.7B to Kyrgyzstan. Trade between the two totalled $2.1B, with a $1.3B surplus for Uzbekistan. Against 2024, Kyrgyzstan's exports to Uzbekistan changed by +23.9% and Uzbekistan's to Kyrgyzstan by +3.0%.
