Kyrgyzstan exported $1.7B of goods and imported $5.6B in 2021, a trade deficit of $3.9B. Its largest export market was 🇷🇺 Russia (24.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇷🇺 Russia (33.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −15.6% and imports by +51.2%.
