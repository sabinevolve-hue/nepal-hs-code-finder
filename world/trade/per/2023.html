Peru exported $65B of goods and imported $59B in 2023, a trade surplus of $5.7B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (35.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (25.9% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +11.1% and imports by −11.9%.
