Peru exported $39B of goods and imported $40B in 2020, a trade deficit of $1.1B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (28.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.7% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −13.3% and imports by −10.3%.
