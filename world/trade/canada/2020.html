Canada exported $390B of goods and imported $428B in 2020, a trade deficit of $38B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (73.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (48.9% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −12.5% and imports by −10.6%.
