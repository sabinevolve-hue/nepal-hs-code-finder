Canada exported $597B of goods and imported $601B in 2022, a trade deficit of $4.5B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (77.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (49.1% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +19.0% and imports by +16.2%.
