Madagascar exported $1.9B of goods and imported $3.2B in 2020, a trade deficit of $1.3B. Its largest export market was 🇫🇷 France (23.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (25.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −23.7% and imports by −14.2%.
