🇲🇬 Madagascar – 🇿🇦 South Africa trade, 2025 and 2026

Madagascar exported $95M of goods to South Africa in 2025, and South Africa exported $192M to Madagascar. Trade between the two totalled $287M, with a $97M surplus for South Africa. Against 2024, Madagascar's exports to South Africa changed by +0.4% and South Africa's to Madagascar by −19.5%.
