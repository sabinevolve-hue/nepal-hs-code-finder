Finland exported $66B of goods and imported $68B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.0B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (13.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (17.6% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −8.5% and imports by −7.4%.
