Finland exported $82B of goods and imported $86B in 2021, a trade deficit of $3.7B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (13.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇪 Sweden (16.7% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +23.9% and imports by +25.8%.
