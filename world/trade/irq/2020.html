Iraq exported $60B of goods and imported $37B in 2020, a trade surplus of $23B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (28.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (31.2% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −31.4% and imports by −6.9%.
