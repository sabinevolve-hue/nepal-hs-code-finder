Argentina exported $55B of goods and imported $42B in 2020, a trade surplus of $12B. Its largest export market was 🇧🇷 Brazil (14.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇧🇷 Brazil (20.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −15.3% and imports by −13.8%.
