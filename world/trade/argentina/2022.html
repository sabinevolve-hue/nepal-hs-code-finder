Argentina exported $88B of goods and imported $82B in 2022, a trade surplus of $6.6B. Its largest export market was 🇧🇷 Brazil (14.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (21.4% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +13.6% and imports by +29.5%.
