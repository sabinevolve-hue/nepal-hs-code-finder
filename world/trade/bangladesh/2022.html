Bangladesh exported $47B of goods and imported $80B in 2022, a trade deficit of $33B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (20.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.4% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +24.4% and imports by +19.1%.
