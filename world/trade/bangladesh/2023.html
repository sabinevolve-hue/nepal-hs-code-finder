Bangladesh exported $39B of goods and imported $62B in 2023, a trade deficit of $23B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (17.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (26.4% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −17.3% and imports by −22.6%.
