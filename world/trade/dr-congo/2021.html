🇨🇩 DR Congo: trade in 2021

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #69 exporter, #117 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Year‹

DR Congo exported $23B of goods and imported $7.2B in 2021, a trade surplus of $16B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (49.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (26.9% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +69.2% and imports by −39.2%.

† DR Congo had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
