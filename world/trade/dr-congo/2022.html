DR Congo exported $25B of goods and imported $11B in 2022, a trade surplus of $14B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (67.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (26.5% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +8.7% and imports by +58.5%.
