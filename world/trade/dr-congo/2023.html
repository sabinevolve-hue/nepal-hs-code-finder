DR Congo exported $26B of goods and imported $26B in 2023, a trade surplus of $601M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (55.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (26.5% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +3.0% and imports by +123.2%.
