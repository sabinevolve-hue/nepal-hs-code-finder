🇨🇷 Costa Rica – 🇳🇮 Nicaragua trade, 2025 and 2026

Costa Rica exported $818M of goods to Nicaragua in 2025, and Nicaragua exported $126M to Costa Rica. Trade between the two totalled $944M, with a $692M surplus for Costa Rica. Against 2024, Costa Rica's exports to Nicaragua changed by +11.0% and Nicaragua's to Costa Rica by +6.7%.
