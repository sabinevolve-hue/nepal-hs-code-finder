🇨🇷 Costa Rica – 🇯🇵 Japan trade, 2025 and 2026

Costa Rica exported $414M of goods to Japan in 2025, and Japan exported $567M to Costa Rica. Trade between the two totalled $981M, with a $152M surplus for Japan. Against 2024, Costa Rica's exports to Japan changed by +72.5% and Japan's to Costa Rica by −3.1%.
