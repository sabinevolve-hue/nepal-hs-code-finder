China exported $3.78T of goods and imported $2.58T in 2025, a trade surplus of $1.19T. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (11.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was Taiwan (8.9% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +5.5% and imports by −0.2%.

What stands out: China

  • Its three largest markets (🇺🇸 United States, 🇭🇰 Hong Kong, 🇻🇳 Vietnam) took 25% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 205 other partners.
  • One product chapter, electrical machinery and equipment and parts thereof (HS 85), made up 29% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It was the world's largest exporter of 11 of its main products in 2024, among them Telephone sets; smartphones for cellular or… 8517.13.
  • Exports in 2025 were 15 times their 2000 level ($249B then, $3.78T now), in current dollars.
