Switzerland exported $379B of goods and imported $320B in 2021, a trade surplus of $59B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (16.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (19.4% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +20.8% and imports by +10.4%.
