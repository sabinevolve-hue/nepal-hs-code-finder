🇰🇷 South Korea – 🇲🇭 Marshall Islands trade, 2025 and 2026

South Korea exported $5.3B of goods to Marshall Islands in 2025, and Marshall Islands exported $87M to South Korea. Trade between the two totalled $5.4B, with a $5.2B surplus for South Korea. Against 2024, South Korea's exports to Marshall Islands changed by +103.0% and Marshall Islands's to South Korea by +55.8%.
