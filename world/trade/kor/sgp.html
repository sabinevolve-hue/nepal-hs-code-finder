🇰🇷 South Korea – 🇸🇬 Singapore trade, 2025 and 2026

South Korea exported $20B of goods to Singapore in 2025, and Singapore exported $23B to South Korea. Trade between the two totalled $43B, with a $3.4B surplus for Singapore. Against 2024, South Korea's exports to Singapore changed by +7.3% and Singapore's to South Korea by +7.8%.
