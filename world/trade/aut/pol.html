🇦🇹 Austria – 🇵🇱 Poland trade, 2025 and 2026

Austria exported $8.6B of goods to Poland in 2025, and Poland exported $8.3B to Austria. Trade between the two totalled $17B, with a $284M surplus for Austria. Against 2024, Austria's exports to Poland changed by +2.6% and Poland's to Austria by +6.3%.
