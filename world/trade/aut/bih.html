🇦🇹 Austria – 🇧🇦 Bosnia and Herzegovina trade, 2025 and 2026

Austria exported $741M of goods to Bosnia and Herzegovina in 2025, and Bosnia and Herzegovina exported $518M to Austria. Trade between the two totalled $1.3B, with a $223M surplus for Austria. Against 2024, Austria's exports to Bosnia and Herzegovina changed by +7.0% and Bosnia and Herzegovina's to Austria by −40.6%.
