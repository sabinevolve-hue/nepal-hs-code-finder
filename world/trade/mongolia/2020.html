Mongolia exported $7.6B of goods and imported $5.3B in 2020, a trade surplus of $2.3B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (72.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (35.7% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −0.6% and imports by −13.5%.
