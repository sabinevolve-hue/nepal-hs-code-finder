Australia exported $413B of goods and imported $307B in 2022, a trade surplus of $106B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (29.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (26.7% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +19.9% and imports by +16.4%.
