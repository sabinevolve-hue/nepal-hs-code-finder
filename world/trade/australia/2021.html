Australia exported $344B of goods and imported $263B in 2021, a trade surplus of $81B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (37.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (27.5% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +36.6% and imports by +22.6%.
