Niger exported $707M of goods and imported $2.9B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.2B. Its largest export market was 🇫🇷 France (20.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇫🇷 France (19.3% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +1.5% and imports by +5.5%.
