Bahamas, The exported $620M of goods and imported $2.2B in 2020, a trade deficit of $1.6B. Its largest export market was 🇿🇼 Zimbabwe (49.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (78.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −23.6% and imports by −34.0%.
