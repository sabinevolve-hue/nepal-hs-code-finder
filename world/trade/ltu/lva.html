🇱🇹 Lithuania – 🇱🇻 Latvia trade, 2025 and 2026

Lithuania exported $5.2B of goods to Latvia in 2025, and Latvia exported $4.1B to Lithuania. Trade between the two totalled $9.3B, with a $1.1B surplus for Lithuania. Against 2024, Lithuania's exports to Latvia changed by +9.7% and Latvia's to Lithuania by +10.8%.
