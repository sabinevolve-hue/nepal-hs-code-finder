🇱🇹 Lithuania – 🇸🇪 Sweden trade, 2025 and 2026

Lithuania exported $1.8B of goods to Sweden in 2025, and Sweden exported $1.7B to Lithuania. Trade between the two totalled $3.6B, with a $62M surplus for Lithuania. Against 2024, Lithuania's exports to Sweden changed by +6.3% and Sweden's to Lithuania by +17.8%.
