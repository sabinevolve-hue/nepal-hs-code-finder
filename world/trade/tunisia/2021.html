Tunisia exported $17B of goods and imported $22B in 2021, a trade deficit of $5.8B. Its largest export market was 🇫🇷 France (24.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇹 Italy (13.5% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +20.9% and imports by +22.4%.
