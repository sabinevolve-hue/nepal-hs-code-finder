Tunisia exported $14B of goods and imported $18B in 2020, a trade deficit of $4.6B. Its largest export market was 🇫🇷 France (26.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇹 Italy (14.2% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −7.6% and imports by −14.9%.
