Guinea-Bissau exported $507M of goods and imported $247M in 2022, a trade surplus of $260M. Its largest export market was 🇸🇬 Singapore (53.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇵🇹 Portugal (33.5% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +4.9% and imports by −0.1%.
