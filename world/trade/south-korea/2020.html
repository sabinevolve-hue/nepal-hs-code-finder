🇰🇷 South Korea: trade in 2020

South Korea exported $513B of goods and imported $468B in 2020, a trade surplus of $45B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (25.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.3% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −5.5% and imports by −7.0%.
