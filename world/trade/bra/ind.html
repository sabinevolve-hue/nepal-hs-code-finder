🇧🇷 Brazil – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

Brazil exported $6.9B of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $6.9B to Brazil. Trade between the two totalled $14B, with a $62M surplus for India. Against 2024, Brazil's exports to India changed by +30.5% and India's to Brazil by +5.8%.
