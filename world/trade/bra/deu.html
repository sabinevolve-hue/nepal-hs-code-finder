🇧🇷 Brazil – 🇩🇪 Germany trade, 2025 and 2026

Brazil exported $6.5B of goods to Germany in 2025, and Germany exported $15B to Brazil. Trade between the two totalled $21B, with a $8.0B surplus for Germany. Against 2024, Brazil's exports to Germany changed by +11.5% and Germany's to Brazil by +2.0%.
