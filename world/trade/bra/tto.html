🇧🇷 Brazil – 🇹🇹 Trinidad and Tobago trade, 2025 and 2026

Brazil exported $310M of goods to Trinidad and Tobago in 2025, and Trinidad and Tobago exported $229M to Brazil. Trade between the two totalled $539M, with a $81M surplus for Brazil. Against 2024, Brazil's exports to Trinidad and Tobago changed by −11.8% and Trinidad and Tobago's to Brazil by +3.5%.
