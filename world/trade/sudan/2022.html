Sudan exported $4.4B of goods and imported $11B in 2022, a trade deficit of $6.7B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (47.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (20.8% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −13.3% and imports by +12.1%.
