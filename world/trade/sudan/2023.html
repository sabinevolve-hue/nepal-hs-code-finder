Sudan exported $9.7B of goods and imported $8.3B in 2023, a trade surplus of $1.3B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (66.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.4% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +121.5% and imports by −24.8%.
