Congo exported $2.4B of goods and imported $2.4B in 2021, a trade surplus of $21M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (45.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (19.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −51.6% and imports by +23.2%.
