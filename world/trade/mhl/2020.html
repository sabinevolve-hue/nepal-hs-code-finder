Marshall Islands exported $709M of goods and imported $14B in 2020, a trade deficit of $13B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (26.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇰🇷 South Korea (37.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −23.6% and imports by +10.0%.
