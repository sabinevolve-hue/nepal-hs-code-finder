Timor-Leste exported $90M of goods and imported $914M in 2025, a trade deficit of $824M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (28.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇩 Indonesia (39.0% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +185.1% and imports by −2.1%.

What stands out: Timor-Leste

  • Its three largest markets (🇨🇳 China, 🇮🇩 Indonesia, 🇯🇵 Japan) took 65% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 48 other partners.
  • One product chapter, mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation (HS 27), made up 72% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It imported 10.2 times as much as it exported in 2025.
