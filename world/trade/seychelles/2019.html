Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #157 exporter, #162 importer of 202 economies (2019).

Seychelles exported $815M of goods and imported $1.4B in 2019, a trade deficit of $621M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (37.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (22.2% of imports).

† Seychelles had not reported some or all of 2019 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
