Seychelles exported $550M of goods and imported $1.5B in 2023, a trade deficit of $915M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (35.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (34.5% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −8.7% and imports by −30.9%.
