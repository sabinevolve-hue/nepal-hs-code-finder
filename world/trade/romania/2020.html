Romania exported $71B of goods and imported $92B in 2020, a trade deficit of $21B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (22.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (20.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −7.9% and imports by −4.6%.
