Iceland exported $4.5B of goods and imported $4.3B in 2020, a trade surplus of $255M. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (20.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (9.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −14.2% and imports by −34.9%.
