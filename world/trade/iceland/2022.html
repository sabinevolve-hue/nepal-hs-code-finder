🇮🇸 Iceland: trade in 2022

Europe & Central Asia · World rank #106 exporter, #113 importer of 202 economies (2022).

Iceland exported $7.4B of goods and imported $9.7B in 2022, a trade deficit of $2.3B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (37.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇴 Norway (12.1% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +22.3% and imports by +23.7%.
