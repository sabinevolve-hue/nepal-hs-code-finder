Iceland exported $6.6B of goods and imported $9.5B in 2023, a trade deficit of $2.9B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (36.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇴 Norway (11.5% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −10.5% and imports by −2.3%.
