Iceland exported $6.1B of goods and imported $7.8B in 2021, a trade deficit of $1.8B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (26.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇴 Norway (9.7% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +33.9% and imports by +83.8%.
