Eritrea exported $595M of goods and imported $423M in 2022, a trade surplus of $172M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (77.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (36.8% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +19.9% and imports by +35.6%.
