Eritrea exported $1.3B of goods and imported $300M in 2020, a trade surplus of $952M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (93.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +269.4% and imports by +9.9%.
