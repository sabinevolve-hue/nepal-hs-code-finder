Iraq exported $111B of goods and imported $59B in 2024, a trade surplus of $52B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (32.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (28.8% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +3.1% and imports by +6.4%.
