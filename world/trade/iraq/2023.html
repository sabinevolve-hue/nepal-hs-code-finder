Iraq exported $107B of goods and imported $55B in 2023, a trade surplus of $52B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (31.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (27.8% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −14.7% and imports by +7.4%.
