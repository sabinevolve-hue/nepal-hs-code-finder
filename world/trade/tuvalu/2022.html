Tuvalu exported $22M of goods and imported $83M in 2022, a trade deficit of $61M. Its largest export market was 🇭🇷 Croatia (63.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇬 Singapore (65.3% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −30.6% and imports by +11.8%.
