🇪🇪 Estonia – 🇫🇮 Finland trade, 2025 and 2026

Estonia exported $3.2B of goods to Finland in 2025, and Finland exported $2.7B to Estonia. Trade between the two totalled $5.9B, with a $426M surplus for Estonia. Against 2024, Estonia's exports to Finland changed by +6.8% and Finland's to Estonia by +0.3%.
