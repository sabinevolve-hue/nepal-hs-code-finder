🇪🇪 Estonia – 🇳🇱 Netherlands trade, 2025 and 2026

Estonia exported $783M of goods to Netherlands in 2025, and Netherlands exported $1.5B to Estonia. Trade between the two totalled $2.3B, with a $707M surplus for Netherlands. Against 2024, Estonia's exports to Netherlands changed by +6.2% and Netherlands's to Estonia by +0.2%.
