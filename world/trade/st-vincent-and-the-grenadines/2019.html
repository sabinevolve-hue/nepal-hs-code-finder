St. Vincent and the Grenadines exported $115M of goods and imported $335M in 2019, a trade deficit of $220M. Its largest export market was 🇯🇴 Jordan (64.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (40.7% of imports).

† St. Vincent and the Grenadines had not reported some or all of 2019 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
