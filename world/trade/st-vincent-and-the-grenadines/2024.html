St. Vincent and the Grenadines exported $65M of goods and imported $517M in 2024, a trade deficit of $452M. Its largest export market was 🇹🇹 Trinidad and Tobago (49.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (33.9% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +22.7% and imports by +13.3%.
