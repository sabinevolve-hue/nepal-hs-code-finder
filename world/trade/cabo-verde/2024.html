Cabo Verde exported $301M of goods and imported $898M in 2024, a trade deficit of $597M. Its largest export market was 🇪🇸 Spain (15.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇵🇹 Portugal (48.4% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by −45.2% and imports by −48.4%.
