Cabo Verde exported $551M of goods and imported $1.7B in 2023, a trade deficit of $1.2B. Its largest export market was 🇪🇸 Spain (9.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇵🇹 Portugal (30.7% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +7.7% and imports by −0.9%.
