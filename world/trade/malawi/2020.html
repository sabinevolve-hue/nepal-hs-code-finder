Malawi exported $729M of goods and imported $2.9B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.1B. Its largest export market was 🇧🇪 Belgium (21.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (20.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −15.8% and imports by −3.1%.
