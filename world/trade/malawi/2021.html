Malawi exported $1.0B of goods and imported $3.1B in 2021, a trade deficit of $2.1B. Its largest export market was 🇧🇪 Belgium (13.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +38.1% and imports by +7.9%.
