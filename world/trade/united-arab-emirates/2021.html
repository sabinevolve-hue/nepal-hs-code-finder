🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates: trade in 2021

Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan · World rank #25 exporter, #22 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Year‹

United Arab Emirates exported $276B of goods and imported $270B in 2021, a trade surplus of $6.2B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (14.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (19.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +48.2% and imports by +26.3%.

† United Arab Emirates had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
