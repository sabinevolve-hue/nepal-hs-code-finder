🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates: trade in 2023

United Arab Emirates exported $361B of goods and imported $392B in 2023, a trade deficit of $31B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (11.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (19.8% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −5.3% and imports by +18.4%.

† United Arab Emirates had not reported some or all of 2023 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
