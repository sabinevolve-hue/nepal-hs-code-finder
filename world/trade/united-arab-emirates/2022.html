United Arab Emirates exported $381B of goods and imported $331B in 2022, a trade surplus of $50B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (13.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (20.3% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +37.8% and imports by +22.6%.
