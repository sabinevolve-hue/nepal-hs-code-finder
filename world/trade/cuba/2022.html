Cuba exported $886M of goods and imported $6.3B in 2022, a trade deficit of $5.4B. Its largest export market was 🇪🇸 Spain (39.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.9% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −55.0% and imports by −24.8%.
