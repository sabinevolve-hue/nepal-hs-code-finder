Iran exported $14B of goods and imported $66B in 2023, a trade deficit of $51B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (29.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (31.4% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −16.0% and imports by +11.7%.
