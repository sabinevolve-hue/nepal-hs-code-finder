Iran exported $15B of goods and imported $65B in 2024, a trade deficit of $50B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (28.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (33.5% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +1.3% and imports by −1.0%.
