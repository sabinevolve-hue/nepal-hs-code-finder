🇮🇷 Iran: trade in 2021

Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan · World rank #78 exporter, #54 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Iran exported $16B of goods and imported $53B in 2021, a trade deficit of $37B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (37.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (31.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +21.3% and imports by +36.1%.

† Iran had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
