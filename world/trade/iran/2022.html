Iran exported $17B of goods and imported $59B in 2022, a trade deficit of $41B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (35.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (30.7% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +5.0% and imports by +10.7%.

† Iran had not reported some or all of 2022 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
