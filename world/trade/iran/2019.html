🇮🇷 Iran: trade in 2019

Iran exported $29B of goods and imported $44B in 2019, a trade deficit of $15B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (43.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (25.4% of imports).

† Iran had not reported some or all of 2019 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
