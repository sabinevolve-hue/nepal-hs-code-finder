Arab League: trade in 2025

League of Arab States. 21 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 21 economies counted here exported $1.20T of goods and imported $1.33T in 2025, with exports +2.5% on 2024. 16.3% of those exports, $196B, went to other members; the remaining $1.01T went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 4.8% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
