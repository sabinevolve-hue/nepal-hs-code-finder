OPEC: trade in 2025

Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries. 12 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 12 economies counted here exported $1.01T of goods and imported $1.03T in 2025, with exports +2.4% on 2024. 8.7% of those exports, $88B, went to other members; the remaining $922B went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 4.0% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
