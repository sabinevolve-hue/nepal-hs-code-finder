G20: trade in 2025

Group of Twenty. 19 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 19 economies counted here exported $14.91T of goods and imported $15.15T in 2025, with exports +4.8% on 2024. 55.9% of those exports, $8.33T, went to other members; the remaining $6.58T went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 59.3% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
