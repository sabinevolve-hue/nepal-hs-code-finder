USMCA: trade in 2025

United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement. 3 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 3 economies counted here exported $3.41T of goods and imported $4.72T in 2025, with exports +4.8% on 2024. 48.7% of those exports, $1.66T, went to other members; the remaining $1.75T went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 13.5% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
