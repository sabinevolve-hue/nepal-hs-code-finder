GCC: trade in 2025

Gulf Cooperation Council. 6 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 6 economies counted here exported $846B of goods and imported $872B in 2025, with exports +4.3% on 2024. 12.9% of those exports, $109B, went to other members; the remaining $736B went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 3.4% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
