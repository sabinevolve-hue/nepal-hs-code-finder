Middle East: trade in 2025

Geographic area. 13 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 13 economies counted here exported $1.04T of goods and imported $1.14T in 2025, with exports +2.5% on 2024. 15.5% of those exports, $162B, went to other members; the remaining $881B went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 4.1% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
