OECD: trade in 2025

Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development. 38 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 38 economies counted here exported $14.41T of goods and imported $15.73T in 2025, with exports +6.6% on 2024. 75.0% of those exports, $10.80T, went to other members; the remaining $3.60T went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 57.3% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
