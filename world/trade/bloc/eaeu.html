Eurasian Economic Union: trade in 2025

Customs union. 5 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 5 economies counted here exported $554B of goods and imported $375B in 2025, with exports +0.9% on 2024. 15.4% of those exports, $85B, went to other members; the remaining $469B went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 2.2% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
