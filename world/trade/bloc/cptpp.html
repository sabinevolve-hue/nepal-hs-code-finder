CPTPP: trade in 2025

Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership. 12 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 12 economies counted here exported $4.58T of goods and imported $4.72T in 2025, with exports +10.2% on 2024. 16.5% of those exports, $755B, went to other members; the remaining $3.83T went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 18.2% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
