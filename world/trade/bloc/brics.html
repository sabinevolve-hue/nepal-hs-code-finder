BRICS: trade in 2025

Group of emerging economies. 10 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 10 economies counted here exported $5.90T of goods and imported $4.88T in 2025, with exports +5.1% on 2024. 20.2% of those exports, $1.19T, went to other members; the remaining $4.71T went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 23.5% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.

In Jan–Jul 2026, the members with figures for those months exported $3.83T, +14.7% on the same months a year earlier.

Who is counted

Ten members: the original five, the four that joined in 2024 and Indonesia, which joined in 2025; all are included in every year shown. Saudi Arabia was invited in 2023 and some sources count it as a member; it is not included here. Membership as of 15 November 2026; the same list is used for every year, so earlier years show today's members, not the membership of the time.
