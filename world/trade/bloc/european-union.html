European Union: trade in 2025

Customs union and single market. 27 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 27 economies counted here exported $7.57T of goods and imported $7.40T in 2025, with exports +6.8% on 2024. 61.5% of those exports, $4.65T, went to other members; the remaining $2.92T went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 30.1% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
