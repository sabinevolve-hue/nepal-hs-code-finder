African Union: trade in 2025

Continental union. 54 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 54 economies counted here exported $711B of goods and imported $771B in 2025, with exports +9.5% on 2024. 17.2% of those exports, $122B, went to other members; the remaining $589B went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 2.8% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
