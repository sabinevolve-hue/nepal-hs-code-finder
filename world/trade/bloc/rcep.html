RCEP: trade in 2025

Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership. 15 economies with figures, ranked by exports.

The 15 economies counted here exported $7.92T of goods and imported $6.42T in 2025, with exports +8.8% on 2024. 38.0% of those exports, $3.01T, went to other members; the remaining $4.92T went to the rest of the world. Members' exports add up to 31.5% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site.
