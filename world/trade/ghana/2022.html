Ghana exported $18B of goods and imported $18B in 2022, a trade deficit of $761M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (17.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.9% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +18.5% and imports by +2.7%.
