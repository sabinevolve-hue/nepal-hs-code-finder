Ghana exported $21B of goods and imported $18B in 2024, a trade surplus of $3.1B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (20.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (22.6% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +22.5% and imports by +7.0%.
