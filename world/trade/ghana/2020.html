Ghana exported $15B of goods and imported $14B in 2020, a trade surplus of $577M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (16.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (20.7% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −11.1% and imports by +36.2%.
