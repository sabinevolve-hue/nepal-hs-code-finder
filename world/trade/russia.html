Russia exported $437B of goods and imported $232B in 2025, a trade surplus of $205B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (24.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (47.9% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −0.4% and imports by −3.3%.
