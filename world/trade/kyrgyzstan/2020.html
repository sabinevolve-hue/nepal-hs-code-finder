🇰🇬 Kyrgyzstan: trade in 2020

Europe & Central Asia · World rank #133 exporter, #136 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Kyrgyzstan exported $2.0B of goods and imported $3.7B in 2020, a trade deficit of $1.7B. Its largest export market was 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (50.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇷🇺 Russia (35.7% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −0.1% and imports by −24.9%.
