Kyrgyzstan exported $2.2B of goods and imported $9.6B in 2022, a trade deficit of $7.4B. Its largest export market was 🇷🇺 Russia (44.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (42.3% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +31.8% and imports by +72.9%.
