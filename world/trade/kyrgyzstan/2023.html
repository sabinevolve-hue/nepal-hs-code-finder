Kyrgyzstan exported $3.4B of goods and imported $13B in 2023, a trade deficit of $9.1B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (32.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (42.8% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +54.8% and imports by +30.0%.
