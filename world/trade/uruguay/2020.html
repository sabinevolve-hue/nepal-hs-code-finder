Uruguay exported $6.9B of goods and imported $7.6B in 2020, a trade deficit of $699M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (20.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇧🇷 Brazil (21.1% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −10.6% and imports by −1.5%.
