🇮🇩 Indonesia – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

Indonesia exported $18B of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $4.3B to Indonesia. Trade between the two totalled $23B, with a $14B surplus for Indonesia. Against 2024, Indonesia's exports to India changed by −10.1% and India's to Indonesia by −21.3%.
