Armenia exported $3.0B of goods and imported $5.4B in 2021, a trade deficit of $2.3B. Its largest export market was 🇷🇺 Russia (27.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇷🇺 Russia (33.3% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +18.9% and imports by +17.0%.
