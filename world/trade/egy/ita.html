🇪🇬 Egypt – 🇮🇹 Italy trade, 2025 and 2026

Egypt exported $4.0B of goods to Italy in 2025, and Italy exported $3.3B to Egypt. Trade between the two totalled $7.3B, with a $779M surplus for Egypt. Against 2024, Egypt's exports to Italy changed by +18.0% and Italy's to Egypt by +6.9%.
