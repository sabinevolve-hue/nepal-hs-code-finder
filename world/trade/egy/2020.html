Egypt exported $27B of goods and imported $60B in 2020, a trade deficit of $33B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (10.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (15.0% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −12.4% and imports by −23.3%.
