🇪🇬 Egypt – 🇷🇺 Russia trade, 2025 and 2026

Egypt exported $762M of goods to Russia in 2025, and Russia exported $7.9B to Egypt. Trade between the two totalled $8.6B, with a $7.1B surplus for Russia. Against 2024, Egypt's exports to Russia changed by +26.4% and Russia's to Egypt by −10.3%.
