🇭🇷 Croatia – 🇭🇺 Hungary trade, 2025 and 2026

Croatia exported $1.3B of goods to Hungary in 2025, and Hungary exported $3.4B to Croatia. Trade between the two totalled $4.7B, with a $2.0B surplus for Hungary. Against 2024, Croatia's exports to Hungary changed by −17.7% and Hungary's to Croatia by +14.9%.
