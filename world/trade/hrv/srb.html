🇭🇷 Croatia – 🇷🇸 Serbia trade, 2025 and 2026

Croatia exported $1.6B of goods to Serbia in 2025, and Serbia exported $1.1B to Croatia. Trade between the two totalled $2.7B, with a $432M surplus for Croatia. Against 2024, Croatia's exports to Serbia changed by +12.3% and Serbia's to Croatia by +9.1%.
