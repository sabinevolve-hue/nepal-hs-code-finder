Iraq exported $101B of goods and imported $61B in 2025, a trade surplus of $40B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (31.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (30.0% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −8.8% and imports by +3.1%.

What stands out: Iraq

  • Its three largest markets (🇨🇳 China, 🇮🇳 India, 🇰🇷 South Korea) took 65% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 107 other partners.
  • One product chapter, mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation (HS 27), made up 99% of what it exported in 2024.
  • Exports in 2025 were 6 times their 2000 level ($16B then, $101B now), in current dollars.
