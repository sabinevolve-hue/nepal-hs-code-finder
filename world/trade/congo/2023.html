🇨🇬 Congo: trade in 2023

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #97 exporter, #137 importer of 202 economies (2023).

Year‹

Congo exported $9.5B of goods and imported $4.7B in 2023, a trade surplus of $4.8B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (54.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (27.6% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +6.6% and imports by +29.4%.

† Congo had not reported some or all of 2023 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
