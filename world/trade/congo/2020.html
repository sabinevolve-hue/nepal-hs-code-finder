🇨🇬 Congo: trade in 2020

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #104 exporter, #156 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019
›

Congo exported $4.9B of goods and imported $1.9B in 2020, a trade surplus of $3.0B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (63.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.7% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −12.1% and imports by −14.9%.

† Congo had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
