Croatia exported $22B of goods and imported $34B in 2021, a trade deficit of $13B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇹 Italy (13.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (14.4% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +27.2% and imports by +28.4%.
