Dominica exported $15M of goods and imported $192M in 2021, a trade deficit of $176M. Its largest export market was 🇹🇹 Trinidad and Tobago (16.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (40.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −7.6% and imports by +5.5%.

† Dominica had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
