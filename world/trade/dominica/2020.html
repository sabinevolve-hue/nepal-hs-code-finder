Dominica exported $17M of goods and imported $182M in 2020, a trade deficit of $165M. Its largest export market was 🇹🇹 Trinidad and Tobago (14.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (38.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +16.0% and imports by −37.9%.
