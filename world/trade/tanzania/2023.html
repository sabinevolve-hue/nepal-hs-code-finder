Tanzania exported $7.3B of goods and imported $15B in 2023, a trade deficit of $7.8B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (21.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (27.5% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +6.6% and imports by −3.4%.
