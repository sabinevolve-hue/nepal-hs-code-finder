Tanzania exported $6.8B of goods and imported $16B in 2022, a trade deficit of $8.8B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (17.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (25.2% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +6.8% and imports by +44.5%.
