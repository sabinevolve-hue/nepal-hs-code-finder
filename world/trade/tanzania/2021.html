🇹🇿 Tanzania: trade in 2021

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #104 exporter, #98 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Tanzania exported $6.4B of goods and imported $11B in 2021, a trade deficit of $4.4B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (16.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.9% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +5.4% and imports by +27.2%.
