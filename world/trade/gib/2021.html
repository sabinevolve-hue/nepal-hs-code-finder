Gibraltar exported $120M of goods and imported $9.3B in 2021, a trade deficit of $9.1B. Its largest export market was 🇵🇱 Poland (20.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇪🇸 Spain (13.7% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −73.8% and imports by +11.1%.
