Mali exported $2.4B of goods and imported $6.5B in 2022, a trade deficit of $4.1B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (47.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇳 Senegal (23.8% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −7.1% and imports by +11.9%.
