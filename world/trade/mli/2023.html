Mali exported $2.2B of goods and imported $6.4B in 2023, a trade deficit of $4.2B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (38.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇳 Senegal (21.7% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −8.3% and imports by −0.5%.
