Russia exported $471B of goods and imported $252B in 2023, a trade surplus of $219B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (23.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (47.6% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −20.7% and imports by +9.3%.
