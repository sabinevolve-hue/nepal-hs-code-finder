🇷🇺 Russia: trade in 2020

Russia exported $338B of goods and imported $231B in 2020, a trade surplus of $106B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (14.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.7% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −19.5% and imports by −4.6%.
