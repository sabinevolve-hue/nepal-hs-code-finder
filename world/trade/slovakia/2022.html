Slovakia exported $108B of goods and imported $112B in 2022, a trade deficit of $4.1B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (20.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (17.8% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +4.3% and imports by +8.0%.
