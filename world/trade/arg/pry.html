🇦🇷 Argentina – 🇵🇾 Paraguay trade, 2025 and 2026

Argentina exported $1.4B of goods to Paraguay in 2025, and Paraguay exported $3.3B to Argentina. Trade between the two totalled $4.7B, with a $1.9B surplus for Paraguay. Against 2024, Argentina's exports to Paraguay changed by −5.0% and Paraguay's to Argentina by −8.9%.
