🇦🇷 Argentina – 🇪🇸 Spain trade, 2025 and 2026

Argentina exported $1.2B of goods to Spain in 2025, and Spain exported $1.2B to Argentina. Trade between the two totalled $2.5B, with a $24M surplus for Spain. Against 2024, Argentina's exports to Spain changed by −15.7% and Spain's to Argentina by +18.3%.
