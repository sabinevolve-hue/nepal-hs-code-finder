🇧🇪 Belgium – 🇱🇺 Luxembourg trade, 2025 and 2026

Belgium exported $8.3B of goods to Luxembourg in 2025, and Luxembourg exported $2.4B to Belgium. Trade between the two totalled $11B, with a $5.8B surplus for Belgium. Against 2024, Belgium's exports to Luxembourg changed by +1.3% and Luxembourg's to Belgium by +17.3%.
