🇳🇬 Nigeria: trade in 2021

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #53 exporter, #55 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Nigeria exported $47B of goods and imported $52B in 2021, a trade deficit of $5.0B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (16.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.7% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +31.7% and imports by −6.3%.
