🇳🇬 Nigeria: trade in 2020

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #53 exporter, #46 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Nigeria exported $36B of goods and imported $55B in 2020, a trade deficit of $20B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (15.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (28.6% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −43.8% and imports by −15.6%.
