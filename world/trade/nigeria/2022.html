🇳🇬 Nigeria: trade in 2022

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #45 exporter, #53 importer of 202 economies (2022).

Year‹

Nigeria exported $84B of goods and imported $60B in 2022, a trade surplus of $24B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇮 Cote d'Ivoire (25.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (23.8% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +79.8% and imports by +16.4%.

† Nigeria had not reported some or all of 2022 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
