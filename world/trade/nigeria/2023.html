Nigeria exported $63B of goods and imported $64B in 2023, a trade deficit of $1.1B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (12.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.1% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −25.0% and imports by +6.4%.
