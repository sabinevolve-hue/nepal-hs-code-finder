Guinea-Bissau exported $512M of goods and imported $320M in 2024, a trade surplus of $192M. Its largest export market was 🇸🇬 Singapore (53.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇳 Senegal (29.1% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +19.8% and imports by −0.4%.
