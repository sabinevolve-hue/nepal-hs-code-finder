🇬🇼 Guinea-Bissau: trade in 2020

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #163 exporter, #194 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019

Guinea-Bissau exported $491M of goods and imported $191M in 2020, a trade surplus of $300M. Its largest export market was 🇸🇬 Singapore (55.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇵🇹 Portugal (25.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −12.0% and imports by +7.8%.

† Guinea-Bissau had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
