🇻🇪 Venezuela: trade in 2021

Latin America & Caribbean · World rank #132 exporter, #101 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019

Venezuela exported $2.7B of goods and imported $10B in 2021, a trade deficit of $7.4B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (24.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.9% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −40.9% and imports by +12.4%.

† Venezuela had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
