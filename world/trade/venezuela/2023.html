Venezuela exported $7.5B of goods and imported $14B in 2023, a trade deficit of $6.3B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (43.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (29.5% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +83.0% and imports by +4.8%.
