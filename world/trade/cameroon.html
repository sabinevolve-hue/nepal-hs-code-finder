Cameroon exported $5.3B of goods and imported $8.5B in 2025, a trade deficit of $3.2B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (30.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (21.2% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −2.9% and imports by +3.3%.
