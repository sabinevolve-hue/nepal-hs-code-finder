Nigeria exported $50B of goods and imported $22B in 2025, a trade surplus of $27B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (11.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.0% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −6.8% and imports by −45.5%.
