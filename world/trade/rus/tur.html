🇷🇺 Russia – 🇹🇷 Turkiye trade, 2025 and 2026

Russia exported $39B of goods to Turkiye in 2025, and Turkiye exported $6.7B to Russia. Trade between the two totalled $46B, with a $32B surplus for Russia. Against 2024, Russia's exports to Turkiye changed by −3.6% and Turkiye's to Russia by −21.5%.
