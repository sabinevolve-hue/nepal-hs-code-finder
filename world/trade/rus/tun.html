🇷🇺 Russia – 🇹🇳 Tunisia trade, 2025 and 2026

Russia exported $1.3B of goods to Tunisia in 2025, and Tunisia exported $19M to Russia. Trade between the two totalled $1.3B, with a $1.3B surplus for Russia. Against 2024, Russia's exports to Tunisia changed by +18.4% and Tunisia's to Russia by −3.1%.
