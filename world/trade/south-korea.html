What stands out: South Korea

  • Its three largest markets (🇨🇳 China, 🇺🇸 United States, 🇻🇳 Vietnam) took 45% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 203 other partners.
  • One product chapter, electrical machinery and equipment and parts thereof (HS 85), made up 32% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It was the world's largest exporter of 6 of its main products in 2024, among them Electronic integrated circuits; memories 8542.32.
  • Exports in 2025 were 4 times their 2000 level ($172B then, $709B now), in current dollars.
