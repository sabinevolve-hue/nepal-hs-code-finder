🇭🇳 Honduras – 🇲🇽 Mexico trade, 2025 and 2026

Honduras exported $257M of goods to Mexico in 2025, and Mexico exported $1.1B to Honduras. Trade between the two totalled $1.4B, with a $854M surplus for Mexico. Against 2024, Honduras's exports to Mexico changed by −8.9% and Mexico's to Honduras by −4.7%.
