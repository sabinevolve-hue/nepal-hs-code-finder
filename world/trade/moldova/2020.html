Moldova exported $2.5B of goods and imported $5.4B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.9B. Its largest export market was 🇷🇴 Romania (28.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (11.9% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −10.6% and imports by −7.5%.
