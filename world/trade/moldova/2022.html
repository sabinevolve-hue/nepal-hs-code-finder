Moldova exported $4.3B of goods and imported $9.2B in 2022, a trade deficit of $4.9B. Its largest export market was 🇷🇴 Romania (28.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇷🇴 Romania (17.9% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +37.8% and imports by +28.5%.
