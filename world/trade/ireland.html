Ireland exported $288B of goods and imported $159B in 2025, a trade surplus of $129B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (42.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (16.0% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +20.1% and imports by +11.9%.

What stands out: Ireland

  • Its three largest markets (🇺🇸 United States, 🇳🇱 Netherlands, 🇬🇧 United Kingdom) took 61% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 191 other partners.
  • One product chapter, pharmaceutical products (HS 30), made up 36% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It was the world's largest exporter of 8 of its main products in 2024, among them Polypeptide hormones, protein hormones and… 2937.19.
  • Exports in 2025 were 4 times their 2000 level ($74B then, $288B now), in current dollars.
