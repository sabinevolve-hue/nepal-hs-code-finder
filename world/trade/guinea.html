What stands out: Guinea

  • Its three largest markets (🇨🇳 China, 🇬🇭 Ghana, 🇮🇳 India) took 72% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 95 other partners.
  • One product chapter, ores, slag and ash (HS 26), made up 86% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It was the world's largest exporter of Aluminium ores and concentrates 2606.00 in 2024.
  • Exports in 2025 were 11 times their 2000 level ($617M then, $6.5B now), in current dollars.
