Palau exported $3M of goods and imported $164M in 2020, a trade deficit of $162M. Its largest export market was 🇯🇵 Japan (47.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (38.1% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −66.5% and imports by −6.3%.
