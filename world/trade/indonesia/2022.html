Indonesia exported $292B of goods and imported $238B in 2022, a trade surplus of $54B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (22.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (28.6% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +26.2% and imports by +21.3%.
