Indonesia exported $163B of goods and imported $142B in 2020, a trade surplus of $22B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (19.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (28.0% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −2.7% and imports by −17.3%.
