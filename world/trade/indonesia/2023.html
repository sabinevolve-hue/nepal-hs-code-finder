Indonesia exported $259B of goods and imported $222B in 2023, a trade surplus of $37B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (25.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (28.4% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −11.4% and imports by −6.8%.
