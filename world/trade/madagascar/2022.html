Madagascar exported $3.5B of goods and imported $5.5B in 2022, a trade deficit of $2.0B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (16.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (21.6% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +31.0% and imports by +26.2%.
