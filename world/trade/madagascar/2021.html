Madagascar exported $2.7B of goods and imported $4.4B in 2021, a trade deficit of $1.7B. Its largest export market was 🇫🇷 France (19.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (22.8% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +38.9% and imports by +36.2%.
