🇨🇭 Switzerland – 🇺🇸 United States trade, 2025 and 2026

Switzerland exported $123B of goods to United States in 2025, and United States exported $72B to Switzerland. Trade between the two totalled $194B, with a $51B surplus for Switzerland. Against 2024, Switzerland's exports to United States changed by +67.0% and United States's to Switzerland by +186.7%.
