Maldives exported $151M of goods and imported $2.6B in 2021, a trade deficit of $2.4B. Its largest export market was 🇹🇭 Thailand (46.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇴🇲 Oman (13.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −7.1% and imports by +40.0%.
