Maldives exported $163M of goods and imported $1.8B in 2020, a trade deficit of $1.7B. Its largest export market was 🇹🇭 Thailand (43.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (14.6% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +3.0% and imports by −36.4%.
