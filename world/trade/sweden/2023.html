Sweden exported $195B of goods and imported $193B in 2023, a trade surplus of $2.1B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (10.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (16.7% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −0.2% and imports by −4.8%.
