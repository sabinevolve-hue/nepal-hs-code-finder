Sweden exported $155B of goods and imported $150B in 2020, a trade surplus of $4.7B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇴 Norway (10.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (18.1% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −2.8% and imports by −5.6%.
