Sweden exported $195B of goods and imported $203B in 2022, a trade deficit of $7.3B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇴 Norway (10.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (15.4% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +3.7% and imports by +8.2%.
