Sweden exported $188B of goods and imported $187B in 2021, a trade surplus of $1.2B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇴 Norway (10.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (17.0% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +21.8% and imports by +24.8%.
