What stands out: Azerbaijan

  • Its three largest markets (🇮🇹 Italy, 🇹🇷 Turkiye, 🇷🇺 Russia) took 64% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 118 other partners.

  • It was the world's largest exporter of Military weapons; other than revolvers… 9301.90 in 2024.
