Kenya exported $5.8B of goods and imported $17B in 2019, a trade deficit of $11B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇬 Uganda (10.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (20.9% of imports).

† Kenya had not reported some or all of 2019 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
