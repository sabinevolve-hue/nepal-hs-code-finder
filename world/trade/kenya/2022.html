Kenya exported $7.4B of goods and imported $21B in 2022, a trade deficit of $14B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇬 Uganda (11.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (18.2% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +9.2% and imports by +7.9%.
