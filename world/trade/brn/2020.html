Brunei exported $6.6B of goods and imported $5.3B in 2020, a trade surplus of $1.3B. Its largest export market was 🇯🇵 Japan (25.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇲🇾 Malaysia (17.9% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −8.9% and imports by +5.9%.
