Bulgaria exported $31B of goods and imported $35B in 2020, a trade deficit of $3.6B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (16.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (12.2% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −3.9% and imports by −6.8%.
