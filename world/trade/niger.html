What stands out: Niger

  • Its three largest markets (🇮🇹 Italy, 🇳🇱 Netherlands, 🇨🇳 China) took 76% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 62 other partners.
  • One product chapter, oil seeds and oleaginous fruits (HS 12), made up 45% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It was the world's largest exporter of Oil seeds; sesamum seeds, whether or not broken 1207.40 in 2024.
  • Exports in 2025 were 11 times their 2000 level ($197M then, $2.2B now), in current dollars.
