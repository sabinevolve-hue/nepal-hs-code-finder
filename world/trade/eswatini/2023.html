Eswatini exported $2.1B of goods and imported $2.0B in 2023, a trade surplus of $60M. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (67.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (73.8% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +5.3% and imports by −4.0%.
