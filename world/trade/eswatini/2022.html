Eswatini exported $2.0B of goods and imported $2.1B in 2022, a trade deficit of $131M. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (68.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (72.0% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −3.8% and imports by +0.0%.
