Latvia exported $21B of goods and imported $24B in 2021, a trade deficit of $3.0B. Its largest export market was 🇱🇹 Lithuania (16.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇱🇹 Lithuania (17.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +27.5% and imports by +31.0%.
