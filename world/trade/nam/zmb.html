🇳🇦 Namibia – 🇿🇲 Zambia trade, 2025 and 2026

Namibia exported $693M of goods to Zambia in 2025, and Zambia exported $233M to Namibia. Trade between the two totalled $925M, with a $460M surplus for Namibia. Against 2024, Namibia's exports to Zambia changed by +13.9% and Zambia's to Namibia by +64.9%.
