Lebanon exported $4.1B of goods and imported $11B in 2020, a trade deficit of $7.1B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (25.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (8.3% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −15.5% and imports by −42.9%.
