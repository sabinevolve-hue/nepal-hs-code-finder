Where the figures come from. Partners and products for 2024: BACI by CEPII, reconciled from UN Comtrade. Latest product detail: Eurostat for EU members, HM Revenue & Customs for the United Kingdom, and Brazil's trade ministry. Product names and codes: the Harmonized System, © World Customs Organization.
