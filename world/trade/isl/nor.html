🇮🇸 Iceland – 🇳🇴 Norway trade, 2025 and 2026

Iceland exported $260M of goods to Norway in 2025, and Norway exported $996M to Iceland. Trade between the two totalled $1.3B, with a $735M surplus for Norway. Against 2024, Iceland's exports to Norway changed by −30.7% and Norway's to Iceland by +1.3%.
