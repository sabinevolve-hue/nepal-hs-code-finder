🇮🇸 Iceland – 🇵🇱 Poland trade, 2025 and 2026

Iceland exported $218M of goods to Poland in 2025, and Poland exported $255M to Iceland. Trade between the two totalled $473M, with a $36M surplus for Poland. Against 2024, Iceland's exports to Poland changed by +32.2% and Poland's to Iceland by +12.9%.
