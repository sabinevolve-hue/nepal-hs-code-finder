Japan exported $638B of goods and imported $631B in 2020, a trade surplus of $7.0B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (22.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (26.0% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −9.6% and imports by −12.4%.
