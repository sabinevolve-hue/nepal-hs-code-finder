Japan exported $747B of goods and imported $897B in 2022, a trade deficit of $150B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (19.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (21.0% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −1.2% and imports by +16.7%.
