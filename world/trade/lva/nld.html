🇱🇻 Latvia – 🇳🇱 Netherlands trade, 2025 and 2026

Latvia exported $709M of goods to Netherlands in 2025, and Netherlands exported $1.5B to Latvia. Trade between the two totalled $2.2B, with a $761M surplus for Netherlands. Against 2024, Latvia's exports to Netherlands changed by +1.4% and Netherlands's to Latvia by +14.7%.
