Curacao exported $126M of goods and imported $1.3B in 2022, a trade deficit of $1.2B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (34.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (34.0% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −7.0% and imports by +24.0%.
