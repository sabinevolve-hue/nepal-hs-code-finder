Curacao exported $105M of goods and imported $1.2B in 2019, a trade deficit of $1.1B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (47.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (36.2% of imports).

† Curacao had not reported some or all of 2019 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
