Latin America & Caribbean · World rank #186 exporter, #175 importer of 202 economies (2023).

Curacao exported $94M of goods and imported $1.3B in 2023, a trade deficit of $1.2B. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (40.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (32.9% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −25.5% and imports by −0.4%.

† Curacao had not reported some or all of 2023 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
