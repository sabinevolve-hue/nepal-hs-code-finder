🇨🇼 Curacao: trade in 2020

Latin America & Caribbean · World rank #184 exporter, #172 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Curacao exported $81M of goods and imported $996M in 2020, a trade deficit of $915M. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (48.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (35.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −22.6% and imports by −16.0%.

† Curacao had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
