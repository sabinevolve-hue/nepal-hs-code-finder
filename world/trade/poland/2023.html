Poland exported $380B of goods and imported $367B in 2023, a trade surplus of $12B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (28.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (25.6% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +6.0% and imports by −3.2%.
