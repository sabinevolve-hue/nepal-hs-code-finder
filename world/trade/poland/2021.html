Poland exported $338B of goods and imported $341B in 2021, a trade deficit of $2.5B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (29.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (26.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +24.0% and imports by +30.8%.
