🇨🇳 China: trade in 2021

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #1 exporter, #2 importer of 202 economies (2021).

China exported $3.37T of goods and imported $2.68T in 2021, a trade surplus of $689B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (17.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was Taiwan (9.4% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +29.6% and imports by +30.0%.
