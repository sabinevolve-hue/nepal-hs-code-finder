China exported $3.42T of goods and imported $2.56T in 2023, a trade surplus of $859B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (14.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was Taiwan (7.8% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −5.1% and imports by −5.6%.
