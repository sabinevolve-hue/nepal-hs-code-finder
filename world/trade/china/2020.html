🇨🇳 China: trade in 2020

East Asia & Pacific · World rank #1 exporter, #2 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019
›

China exported $2.60T of goods and imported $2.06T in 2020, a trade surplus of $538B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (17.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was Taiwan (9.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +4.0% and imports by −0.4%.

† China had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
