China exported $3.58T of goods and imported $2.59T in 2024, a trade surplus of $993B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (14.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was Taiwan (8.4% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +4.6% and imports by +0.9%.
