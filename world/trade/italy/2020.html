Italy exported $494B of goods and imported $425B in 2020, a trade surplus of $69B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (13.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (16.5% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −6.8% and imports by −10.1%.
