🇬🇾 Guyana – 🇺🇸 United States trade, 2025 and 2026

Guyana exported $6.7B of goods to United States in 2025, and United States exported $1.5B to Guyana. Trade between the two totalled $8.3B, with a $5.2B surplus for Guyana. Against 2024, Guyana's exports to United States changed by +104.3% and United States's to Guyana by +15.8%.
