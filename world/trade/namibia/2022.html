🇳🇦 Namibia: trade in 2022

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #117 exporter, #125 importer of 202 economies (2022).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019

Namibia exported $4.7B of goods and imported $6.7B in 2022, a trade deficit of $2.0B. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (25.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (45.2% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −13.4% and imports by −10.3%.

† Namibia had not reported some or all of 2022 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
