🇪🇹 Ethiopia: trade in 2022

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #133 exporter, #89 importer of 202 economies (2022).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019

Ethiopia exported $3.1B of goods and imported $17B in 2022, a trade deficit of $13B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (10.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (30.0% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +0.9% and imports by +8.2%.

† Ethiopia had not reported some or all of 2022 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
