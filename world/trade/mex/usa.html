🇲🇽 Mexico – 🇺🇸 United States trade, 2025 and 2026

Mexico exported $552B of goods to United States in 2025, and United States exported $338B to Mexico. Trade between the two totalled $890B, with a $214B surplus for Mexico. Against 2024, Mexico's exports to United States changed by +7.7% and United States's to Mexico by +1.2%.
