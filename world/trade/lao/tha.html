🇱🇦 Laos – 🇹🇭 Thailand trade, 2025 and 2026

Laos exported $3.9B of goods to Thailand in 2025, and Thailand exported $5.9B to Laos. Trade between the two totalled $9.8B, with a $2.0B surplus for Thailand. Against 2024, Laos's exports to Thailand changed by +68.6% and Thailand's to Laos by +20.4%.
