🇯🇲 Jamaica: trade in 2021

Latin America & Caribbean · World rank #146 exporter, #125 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Jamaica exported $1.5B of goods and imported $6.0B in 2021, a trade deficit of $4.5B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (51.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (40.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +18.3% and imports by +25.3%.

† Jamaica had not reported some or all of 2021 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
