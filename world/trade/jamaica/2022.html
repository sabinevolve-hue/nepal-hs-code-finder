Jamaica exported $1.9B of goods and imported $7.7B in 2022, a trade deficit of $5.8B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (60.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (41.7% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +28.4% and imports by +29.5%.
