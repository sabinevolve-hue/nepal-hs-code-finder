Eswatini exported $1.8B of goods and imported $1.6B in 2020, a trade surplus of $149M. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (64.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (71.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −12.4% and imports by −12.4%.
