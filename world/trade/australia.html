Australia exported $338B of goods and imported $315B in 2025, a trade surplus of $23B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (33.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (26.9% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −1.1% and imports by +4.8%.

What stands out: Australia

  • Its three largest markets (🇨🇳 China, 🇯🇵 Japan, 🇰🇷 South Korea) took 53% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 191 other partners.
  • One product chapter, mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation (HS 27), made up 33% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It was the world's largest exporter of 6 of its main products in 2024, among them Iron ores and concentrates; non-agglomerated 2601.11.
  • Exports in 2025 were 5 times their 2000 level ($64B then, $338B now), in current dollars.
