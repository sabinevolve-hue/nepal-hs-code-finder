Central African Republic exported $118M of goods and imported $623M in 2022, a trade deficit of $506M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (40.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇲 Cameroon (40.2% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +95.9% and imports by +28.5%.
