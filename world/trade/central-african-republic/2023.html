Central African Republic exported $116M of goods and imported $736M in 2023, a trade deficit of $620M. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (67.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.1% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by −1.1% and imports by +18.1%.

† Central African Republic had not reported some or all of 2023 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
