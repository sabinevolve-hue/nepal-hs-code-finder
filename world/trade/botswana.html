Botswana exported $3.3B of goods and imported $7.1B in 2025, a trade deficit of $3.8B. Its largest export market was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (36.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (59.6% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −4.8% and imports by −4.7%.
