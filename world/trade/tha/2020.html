Thailand exported $229B of goods and imported $207B in 2020, a trade surplus of $22B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (14.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.2% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −6.6% and imports by −13.3%.
