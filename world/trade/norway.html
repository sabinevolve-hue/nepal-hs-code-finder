Norway exported $170B of goods and imported $105B in 2025, a trade surplus of $66B. Its largest export market was 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (17.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (12.2% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +1.5% and imports by +6.1%.

What stands out: Norway

  • Its three largest markets (🇬🇧 United Kingdom, 🇩🇪 Germany, 🇳🇱 Netherlands) took 45% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 195 other partners.
  • One product chapter, mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation (HS 27), made up 64% of what it exported in 2024.
