🇿🇲 Zambia: trade in 2021

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #87 exporter, #119 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Zambia exported $11B of goods and imported $7.1B in 2021, a trade surplus of $4.0B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇭 Switzerland (42.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (31.5% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +42.5% and imports by +33.4%.
