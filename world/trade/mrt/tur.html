🇲🇷 Mauritania – 🇹🇷 Turkiye trade, 2025 and 2026

Mauritania exported $193M of goods to Turkiye in 2025, and Turkiye exported $238M to Mauritania. Trade between the two totalled $430M, with a $45M surplus for Turkiye. Against 2024, Mauritania's exports to Turkiye changed by +642.7% and Turkiye's to Mauritania by +23.1%.
