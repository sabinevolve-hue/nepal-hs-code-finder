Guyana exported $4.3B of goods and imported $4.4B in 2021, a trade deficit of $55M. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (32.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇬 Singapore (38.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +68.1% and imports by +95.7%.
