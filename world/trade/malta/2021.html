🇲🇹 Malta: trade in 2021

Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan · World rank #130 exporter, #121 importer of 202 economies (2021).

Malta exported $2.9B of goods and imported $6.9B in 2021, a trade deficit of $4.0B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (14.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇹 Italy (24.2% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +5.4% and imports by +21.0%.
