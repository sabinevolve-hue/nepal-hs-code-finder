Malta exported $2.8B of goods and imported $5.7B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.9B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (17.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇹 Italy (22.9% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −6.6% and imports by −22.6%.
