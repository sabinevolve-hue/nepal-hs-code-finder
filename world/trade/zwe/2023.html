Zimbabwe exported $7.2B of goods and imported $10B in 2023, a trade deficit of $3.1B. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (30.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (35.7% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +9.7% and imports by +7.8%.
