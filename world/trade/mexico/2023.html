Mexico exported $593B of goods and imported $642B in 2023, a trade deficit of $49B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (82.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (43.1% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +2.6% and imports by −0.1%.
