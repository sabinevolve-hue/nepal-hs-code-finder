Iran exported $16B of goods and imported $55B in 2025, a trade deficit of $39B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (17.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (32.2% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +10.2% and imports by −15.6%.
