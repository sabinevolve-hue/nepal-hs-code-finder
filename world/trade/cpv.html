Cabo Verde exported $335M of goods and imported $705M in 2025, a trade deficit of $370M. Its largest export market was 🇪🇸 Spain (12.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇵🇹 Portugal (53.7% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +11.2% and imports by −21.5%.
