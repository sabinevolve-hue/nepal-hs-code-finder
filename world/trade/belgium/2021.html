Belgium exported $548B of goods and imported $526B in 2021, a trade surplus of $22B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (19.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (18.6% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +30.5% and imports by +32.8%.
