Belgium exported $420B of goods and imported $396B in 2020, a trade surplus of $24B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (17.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (16.9% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −5.3% and imports by −7.8%.
