Gabon exported $13B of goods and imported $4.1B in 2023, a trade surplus of $8.6B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (27.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇰🇷 South Korea (13.7% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +21.1% and imports by +3.4%.
