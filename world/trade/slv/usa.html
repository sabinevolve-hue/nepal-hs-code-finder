🇸🇻 El Salvador – 🇺🇸 United States trade, 2025 and 2026

El Salvador exported $2.1B of goods to United States in 2025, and United States exported $4.6B to El Salvador. Trade between the two totalled $6.7B, with a $2.5B surplus for United States. Against 2024, El Salvador's exports to United States changed by −2.5% and United States's to El Salvador by +0.9%.
