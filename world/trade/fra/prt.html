🇫🇷 France – 🇵🇹 Portugal trade, 2025 and 2026

France exported $8.4B of goods to Portugal in 2025, and Portugal exported $11B to France. Trade between the two totalled $19B, with a $2.1B surplus for Portugal. Against 2024, France's exports to Portugal changed by +3.9% and Portugal's to France by +2.3%.
