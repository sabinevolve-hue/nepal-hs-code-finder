🇫🇷 France – 🇲🇾 Malaysia trade, 2025 and 2026

France exported $2.2B of goods to Malaysia in 2025, and Malaysia exported $1.8B to France. Trade between the two totalled $3.9B, with a $394M surplus for France. Against 2024, France's exports to Malaysia changed by +47.2% and Malaysia's to France by +29.1%.
