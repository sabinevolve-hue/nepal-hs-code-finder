🇫🇷 France – 🇲🇱 Mali trade, 2025 and 2026

France exported $359M of goods to Mali in 2025, and Mali exported $31M to France. Trade between the two totalled $390M, with a $328M surplus for France. Against 2024, France's exports to Mali changed by +4.1% and Mali's to France by +162.4%.
