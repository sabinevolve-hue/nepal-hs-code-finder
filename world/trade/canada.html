What stands out: Canada

  • Its three largest markets (🇺🇸 United States, 🇬🇧 United Kingdom, 🇨🇳 China) took 83% of exports in 2025; the rest went to 197 other partners.
  • One product chapter, mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation (HS 27), made up 26% of what it exported in 2024.
  • It was the world's largest exporter of 8 of its main products in 2024, among them Metals; gold, semi-manufactured 7108.13.
