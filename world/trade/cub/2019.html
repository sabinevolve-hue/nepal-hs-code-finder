Cuba exported $2.1B of goods and imported $9.9B in 2019, a trade deficit of $7.8B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇦 Canada (22.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇻🇪 Venezuela (18.0% of imports).

† Cuba had not reported some or all of 2019 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
