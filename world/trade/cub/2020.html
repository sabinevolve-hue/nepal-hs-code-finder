🇨🇺 Cuba: trade in 2020

Latin America & Caribbean · World rank #139 exporter, #108 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Cuba exported $1.7B of goods and imported $7.2B in 2020, a trade deficit of $5.5B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇦 Canada (27.0% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇻🇪 Venezuela (14.8% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −17.5% and imports by −26.9%.

† Cuba had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
