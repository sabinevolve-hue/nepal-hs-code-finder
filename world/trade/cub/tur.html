🇨🇺 Cuba – 🇹🇷 Turkiye trade, 2025 and 2026

Cuba exported $7M of goods to Turkiye in 2025, and Turkiye exported $160M to Cuba. Trade between the two totalled $167M, with a $153M surplus for Turkiye. Against 2024, Cuba's exports to Turkiye changed by +15.5% and Turkiye's to Cuba by +30.3%.
