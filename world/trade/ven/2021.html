Venezuela exported $2.7B of goods and imported $10B in 2021, a trade deficit of $7.4B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (24.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (24.9% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −40.9% and imports by +12.4%.
