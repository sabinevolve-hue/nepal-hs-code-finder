Benin exported $1.1B of goods and imported $3.9B in 2023, a trade deficit of $2.9B. Its largest export market was 🇧🇩 Bangladesh (37.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇳 India (17.0% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +15.9% and imports by +7.1%.
