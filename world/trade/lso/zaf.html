🇱🇸 Lesotho – 🇿🇦 South Africa trade, 2025 and 2026

Lesotho exported $592M of goods to South Africa in 2025, and South Africa exported $1.5B to Lesotho. Trade between the two totalled $2.1B, with a $931M surplus for South Africa. Against 2024, Lesotho's exports to South Africa changed by +13.5% and South Africa's to Lesotho by +4.3%.
