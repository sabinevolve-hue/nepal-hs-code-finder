🇵🇹 Portugal – 🇺🇸 United States trade, 2025 and 2026

Portugal exported $5.2B of goods to United States in 2025, and United States exported $2.7B to Portugal. Trade between the two totalled $7.9B, with a $2.6B surplus for Portugal. Against 2024, Portugal's exports to United States changed by −8.8% and United States's to Portugal by −12.2%.
