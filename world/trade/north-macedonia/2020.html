🇲🇰 North Macedonia: trade in 2020

Europe & Central Asia · World rank #95 exporter, #101 importer of 202 economies (2020).

North Macedonia exported $6.6B of goods and imported $8.7B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.1B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (47.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (15.6% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −7.8% and imports by −7.7%.
