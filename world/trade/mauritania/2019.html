Mauritania exported $2.3B of goods and imported $3.5B in 2019, a trade deficit of $1.2B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (38.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (13.3% of imports).
