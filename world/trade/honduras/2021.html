🇭🇳 Honduras: trade in 2021

Honduras exported $5.2B of goods and imported $13B in 2021, a trade deficit of $8.3B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (31.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (33.3% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +21.6% and imports by +50.2%.
