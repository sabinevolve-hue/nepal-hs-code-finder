Djibouti exported $4.8B of goods and imported $5.3B in 2023, a trade deficit of $536M. Its largest export market was 🇪🇹 Ethiopia (83.4% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (17.6% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +3.7% and imports by +3.9%.
