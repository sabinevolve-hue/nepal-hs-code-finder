France exported $637B of goods and imported $784B in 2023, a trade deficit of $147B. Its largest export market was 🇩🇪 Germany (13.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇩🇪 Germany (15.5% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +4.7% and imports by −4.3%.
