Haiti exported $204M of goods and imported $3.0B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.8B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (61.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (31.0% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −16.1% and imports by +20.0%.

† Haiti had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
