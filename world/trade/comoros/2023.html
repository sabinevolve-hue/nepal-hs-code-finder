Comoros exported $196M of goods and imported $206M in 2023, a trade deficit of $9M. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (41.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (15.1% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +259.2% and imports by −10.0%.
