Senegal exported $5.2B of goods and imported $9.7B in 2021, a trade deficit of $4.5B. Its largest export market was 🇲🇱 Mali (20.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇫🇷 France (11.8% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +32.3% and imports by +23.9%.
