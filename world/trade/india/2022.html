India exported $453B of goods and imported $732B in 2022, a trade deficit of $279B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (17.7% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (14.0% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +14.9% and imports by +28.4%.
