India exported $443B of goods and imported $718B in 2024, a trade deficit of $275B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (18.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (15.2% of imports). Against 2023, exports changed by +2.4% and imports by +7.7%.
