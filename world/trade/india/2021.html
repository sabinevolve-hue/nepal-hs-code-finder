🇮🇳 India: trade in 2021

India exported $394B of goods and imported $570B in 2021, a trade deficit of $176B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (18.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (15.3% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +43.1% and imports by +54.9%.
