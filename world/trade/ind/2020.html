India exported $276B of goods and imported $368B in 2020, a trade deficit of $92B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (17.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (16.0% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −15.2% and imports by −23.3%.
