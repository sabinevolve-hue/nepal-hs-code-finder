🇮🇳 India – 🇺🇬 Uganda trade, 2025 and 2026

India exported $422M of goods to Uganda in 2025, and Uganda exported $151M to India. Trade between the two totalled $572M, with a $271M surplus for India. Against 2024, India's exports to Uganda changed by −9.1% and Uganda's to India by −67.2%.
