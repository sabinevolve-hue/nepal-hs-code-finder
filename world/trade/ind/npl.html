🇮🇳 India – 🇳🇵 Nepal trade, 2025 and 2026

India exported $7.5B of goods to Nepal in 2025, and Nepal exported $2.0B to India. Trade between the two totalled $9.5B, with a $5.4B surplus for India. Against 2024, India's exports to Nepal changed by +4.9% and Nepal's to India by +124.4%.
