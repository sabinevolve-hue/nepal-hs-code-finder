🇮🇳 India – 🇰🇷 South Korea trade, 2025 and 2026

India exported $6.0B of goods to South Korea in 2025, and South Korea exported $19B to India. Trade between the two totalled $25B, with a $13B surplus for South Korea. Against 2024, India's exports to South Korea changed by +0.8% and South Korea's to India by +2.9%.
