🇮🇳 India – 🇲🇿 Mozambique trade, 2025 and 2026

India exported $1.3B of goods to Mozambique in 2025, and Mozambique exported $1.6B to India. Trade between the two totalled $3.0B, with a $253M surplus for Mozambique. Against 2024, India's exports to Mozambique changed by −13.6% and Mozambique's to India by +9.0%.
