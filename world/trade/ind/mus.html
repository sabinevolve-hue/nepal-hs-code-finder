🇮🇳 India – 🇲🇺 Mauritius trade, 2025 and 2026

India exported $467M of goods to Mauritius in 2025, and Mauritius exported $41M to India. Trade between the two totalled $508M, with a $425M surplus for India. Against 2024, India's exports to Mauritius changed by −40.7% and Mauritius's to India by −8.6%.
