🇮🇳 India – 🇱🇰 Sri Lanka trade, 2025 and 2026

India exported $4.9B of goods to Sri Lanka in 2025, and Sri Lanka exported $1.0B to India. Trade between the two totalled $5.9B, with a $3.8B surplus for India. Against 2024, India's exports to Sri Lanka changed by −0.7% and Sri Lanka's to India by +17.6%.
