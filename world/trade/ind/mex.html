🇮🇳 India – 🇲🇽 Mexico trade, 2025 and 2026

India exported $5.6B of goods to Mexico in 2025, and Mexico exported $2.0B to India. Trade between the two totalled $7.5B, with a $3.6B surplus for India. Against 2024, India's exports to Mexico changed by −3.0% and Mexico's to India by −27.7%.
