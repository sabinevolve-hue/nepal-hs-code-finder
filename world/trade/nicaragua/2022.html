Nicaragua exported $3.9B of goods and imported $8.0B in 2022, a trade deficit of $4.1B. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (46.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (27.5% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by +10.4% and imports by +20.7%.
