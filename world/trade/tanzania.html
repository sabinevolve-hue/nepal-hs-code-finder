Tanzania exported $10B of goods and imported $16B in 2025, a trade deficit of $6.2B. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (28.8% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (35.5% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by +15.8% and imports by +3.5%.
