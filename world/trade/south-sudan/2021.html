South Sudan exported $4M of goods and imported $57M in 2021, a trade deficit of $53M. Its largest export market was 🇷🇺 Russia (86.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇲🇿 Mozambique (34.1% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by −97.7% and imports by +109.2%.
