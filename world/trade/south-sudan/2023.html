South Sudan exported $2.6B of goods and imported $824M in 2023, a trade surplus of $1.7B. Its largest export market was 🇲🇾 Malaysia (46.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇬 Uganda (32.5% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +14.5% and imports by +31.4%.
