Australia exported $252B of goods and imported $215B in 2020, a trade surplus of $37B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (39.9% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇨🇳 China (28.9% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −7.6% and imports by −5.4%.
