🇦🇺 Australia – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

Australia exported $13B of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $7.7B to Australia. Trade between the two totalled $21B, with a $5.7B surplus for Australia. Against 2024, Australia's exports to India changed by −18.7% and India's to Australia by −2.0%.
