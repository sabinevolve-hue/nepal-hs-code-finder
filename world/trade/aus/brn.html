🇦🇺 Australia – 🇧🇳 Brunei trade, 2025 and 2026

Australia exported $460M of goods to Brunei in 2025, and Brunei exported $2.4B to Australia. Trade between the two totalled $2.9B, with a $1.9B surplus for Brunei. Against 2024, Australia's exports to Brunei changed by −1.2% and Brunei's to Australia by +8.1%.
