🇦🇺 Australia – 🇹🇭 Thailand trade, 2025 and 2026

Australia exported $4.6B of goods to Thailand in 2025, and Thailand exported $12B to Australia. Trade between the two totalled $17B, with a $7.5B surplus for Thailand. Against 2024, Australia's exports to Thailand changed by −0.3% and Thailand's to Australia by −1.8%.
