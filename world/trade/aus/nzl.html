🇦🇺 Australia – 🇳🇿 New Zealand trade, 2025 and 2026

Australia exported $8.5B of goods to New Zealand in 2025, and New Zealand exported $10B to Australia. Trade between the two totalled $19B, with a $1.7B surplus for New Zealand. Against 2024, Australia's exports to New Zealand changed by +2.4% and New Zealand's to Australia by +89.3%.
