🇿🇼 Zimbabwe: trade in 2020

Sub-Saharan Africa · World rank #109 exporter, #110 importer of 202 economies (2020).

Year‹
2025–26 latest202420232022202120202019
›

Zimbabwe exported $4.4B of goods and imported $6.7B in 2020, a trade deficit of $2.4B. Its largest export market was 🇿🇦 South Africa (59.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇿🇦 South Africa (35.7% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by +20.7% and imports by +12.2%.

† Zimbabwe had not reported some or all of 2020 to the IMF when this was built, so those figures were derived by the IMF from its partners' records. Treat them as provisional.
