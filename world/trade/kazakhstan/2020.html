Kazakhstan exported $42B of goods and imported $22B in 2020, a trade surplus of $20B. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (16.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇷🇺 Russia (62.1% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −27.5% and imports by −42.2%.
