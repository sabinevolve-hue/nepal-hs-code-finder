New Caledonia exported $1.7B of goods and imported $2.5B in 2021, a trade deficit of $800M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (48.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇫🇷 France (45.5% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +2.2% and imports by +8.3%.
