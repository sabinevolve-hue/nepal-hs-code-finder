Guam exported $196M of goods and imported $1.5B in 2023, a trade deficit of $1.3B. Its largest export market was 🇫🇲 Micronesia, Fed. Sts. (83.3% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇸🇬 Singapore (34.7% of imports). Against 2022, exports changed by +13.0% and imports by +4.9%.
