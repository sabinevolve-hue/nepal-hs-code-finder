🇬🇧 United Kingdom – 🇲🇦 Morocco trade, 2025 and 2026

United Kingdom exported $1.9B of goods to Morocco in 2025, and Morocco exported $3.5B to United Kingdom. Trade between the two totalled $5.4B, with a $1.7B surplus for Morocco. Against 2024, United Kingdom's exports to Morocco changed by +38.2% and Morocco's to United Kingdom by +0.2%.
