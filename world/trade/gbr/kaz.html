🇬🇧 United Kingdom – 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan trade, 2025 and 2026

United Kingdom exported $398M of goods to Kazakhstan in 2025, and Kazakhstan exported $1.2B to United Kingdom. Trade between the two totalled $1.6B, with a $823M surplus for Kazakhstan. Against 2024, United Kingdom's exports to Kazakhstan changed by −22.1% and Kazakhstan's to United Kingdom by +190.3%.
