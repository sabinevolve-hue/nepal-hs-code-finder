🇬🇧 United Kingdom – 🇰🇷 South Korea trade, 2025 and 2026

United Kingdom exported $5.8B of goods to South Korea in 2025, and South Korea exported $7.2B to United Kingdom. Trade between the two totalled $13B, with a $1.3B surplus for South Korea. Against 2024, United Kingdom's exports to South Korea changed by +10.9% and South Korea's to United Kingdom by +7.9%.
