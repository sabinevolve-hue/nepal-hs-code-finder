Vanuatu exported $39M of goods and imported $284M in 2020, a trade deficit of $245M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (21.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇦🇺 Australia (21.4% of imports). Against 2019, exports changed by −16.7% and imports by −16.8%.
