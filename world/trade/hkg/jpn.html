🇭🇰 Hong Kong – 🇯🇵 Japan trade, 2025 and 2026

Hong Kong exported $12B of goods to Japan in 2025, and Japan exported $43B to Hong Kong. Trade between the two totalled $55B, with a $31B surplus for Japan. Against 2024, Hong Kong's exports to Japan changed by +16.6% and Japan's to Hong Kong by +19.1%.
