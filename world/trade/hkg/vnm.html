🇭🇰 Hong Kong – 🇻🇳 Vietnam trade, 2025 and 2026

Hong Kong exported $28B of goods to Vietnam in 2025, and Vietnam exported $18B to Hong Kong. Trade between the two totalled $46B, with a $9.6B surplus for Hong Kong. Against 2024, Hong Kong's exports to Vietnam changed by +40.2% and Vietnam's to Hong Kong by +45.7%.
