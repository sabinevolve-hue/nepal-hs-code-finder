🇭🇰 Hong Kong – 🇲🇾 Malaysia trade, 2025 and 2026

Hong Kong exported $9.7B of goods to Malaysia in 2025, and Malaysia exported $31B to Hong Kong. Trade between the two totalled $41B, with a $22B surplus for Malaysia. Against 2024, Hong Kong's exports to Malaysia changed by +58.9% and Malaysia's to Hong Kong by +60.6%.
