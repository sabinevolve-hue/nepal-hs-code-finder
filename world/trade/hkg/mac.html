🇭🇰 Hong Kong – 🇲🇴 Macao SAR, China trade, 2025 and 2026

Hong Kong exported $7.9B of goods to Macao SAR, China in 2025, and Macao SAR, China exported $1.0B to Hong Kong. Trade between the two totalled $8.9B, with a $6.8B surplus for Hong Kong. Against 2024, Hong Kong's exports to Macao SAR, China changed by −3.0% and Macao SAR, China's to Hong Kong by +7.9%.
