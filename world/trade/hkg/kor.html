🇭🇰 Hong Kong – 🇰🇷 South Korea trade, 2025 and 2026

Hong Kong exported $9.9B of goods to South Korea in 2025, and South Korea exported $35B to Hong Kong. Trade between the two totalled $45B, with a $25B surplus for South Korea. Against 2024, Hong Kong's exports to South Korea changed by +4.8% and South Korea's to Hong Kong by −0.5%.
