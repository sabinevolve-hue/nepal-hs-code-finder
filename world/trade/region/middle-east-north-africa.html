Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan: trade in 2025

22 economies, ranked by exports. Grouping follows the World Bank's regions.

The 22 economies of Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan exported $1.31T of goods and imported $1.54T in 2025, with exports +2.3% on 2024. That is 5.2% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site. 18.4% of the region's exports, $240B, went to other economies inside it.
