Latin America & Caribbean: trade in 2025

36 economies, ranked by exports. Grouping follows the World Bank's regions.

The 36 economies of Latin America & Caribbean exported $1.53T of goods and imported $1.60T in 2025, with exports +6.9% on 2024. That is 6.1% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site. 13.3% of the region's exports, $204B, went to other economies inside it.
