East Asia & Pacific: trade in 2025

36 economies, ranked by exports. Grouping follows the World Bank's regions.

The 36 economies of East Asia & Pacific exported $8.71T of goods and imported $7.33T in 2025, with exports +9.5% on 2024. That is 34.6% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site. 48.3% of the region's exports, $4.21T, went to other economies inside it.
