Sub-Saharan Africa: trade in 2025

48 economies, ranked by exports. Grouping follows the World Bank's regions.

The 48 economies of Sub-Saharan Africa exported $483B of goods and imported $450B in 2025, with exports +13.1% on 2024. That is 1.9% of the exports of all 202 economies on this site. 20.3% of the region's exports, $98B, went to other economies inside it.
