St. Kitts and Nevis exported $45M of goods and imported $259M in 2021, a trade deficit of $214M. Its largest export market was 🇺🇸 United States (38.6% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (54.4% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +31.3% and imports by −5.3%.
