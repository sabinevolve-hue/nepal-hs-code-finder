Curacao exported $136M of goods and imported $1.0B in 2021, a trade deficit of $914M. Its largest export market was 🇳🇱 Netherlands (52.5% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇺🇸 United States (29.8% of imports). Against 2020, exports changed by +67.0% and imports by +5.4%.
