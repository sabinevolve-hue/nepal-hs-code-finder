🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia – 🇾🇪 Yemen, Rep. trade, 2025 and 2026

Saudi Arabia exported $1.6B of goods to Yemen, Rep. in 2025, and Yemen, Rep. exported $276M to Saudi Arabia. Trade between the two totalled $1.9B, with a $1.4B surplus for Saudi Arabia. Against 2024, Saudi Arabia's exports to Yemen, Rep. changed by +16.2% and Yemen, Rep.'s to Saudi Arabia by −20.6%.
