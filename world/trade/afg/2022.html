Afghanistan exported $456M of goods and imported $3.2B in 2022, a trade deficit of $2.7B. Its largest export market was 🇮🇳 India (45.2% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇮🇷 Iran (24.4% of imports). Against 2021, exports changed by −47.3% and imports by −39.2%.
