🇦🇫 Afghanistan – 🇮🇳 India trade, 2025 and 2026

Afghanistan exported $342M of goods to India in 2025, and India exported $267M to Afghanistan. Trade between the two totalled $609M, with a $75M surplus for Afghanistan. Against 2024, Afghanistan's exports to India changed by +32.2% and India's to Afghanistan by −21.3%.
