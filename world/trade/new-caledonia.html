New Caledonia exported $782M of goods and imported $1.4B in 2025, a trade deficit of $651M. Its largest export market was 🇨🇳 China (67.1% of exports) and its largest supplier was 🇫🇷 France (42.6% of imports). Against 2024, exports changed by −27.1% and imports by +2.2%.

Where the figures come from. Partners and products for 2024: BACI by CEPII, reconciled from UN Comtrade. The latest year and the current year: the International Monetary Fund's trade-by-partner database, which carries each economy's own customs figures. Long-run totals: World Bank. Latest product detail: Eurostat for EU members, HM Revenue & Customs for the United Kingdom, and Brazil's trade ministry. Product names and codes: the Harmonized System, © World Customs Organization. All values are in current US dollars; each page names its source and year. About the data and method
