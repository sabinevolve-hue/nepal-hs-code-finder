United Kingdom import tariff

The official UK Global Tariff, as at 16 November 2026.

16,587Tariff lines with a rate
36%Duty-free lines
5.4%Average of percentage rates
1,745Lines charged per unit or mixed
Not the whole bill. These are the UK's third-country duties, the rate for goods from any country without a better arrangement. Goods from countries with a UK trade agreement, or under the UK's preference scheme for developing countries, may pay less if they meet the rules of origin. Quotas, anti-dumping and other trade remedy duties can apply to particular goods and origins, and import VAT is charged on top. None of these are included below.
