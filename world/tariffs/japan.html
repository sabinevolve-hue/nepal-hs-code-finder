Japan import tariff

The official Japan's Tariff Schedule, as of 31 October 2026.

8,333Tariff lines with a rate
36%Duty-free lines
4.2%Average of percentage rates
1,805Lines charged per unit or mixed
Not the whole bill. Japan's schedule prints a General rate, sometimes a Temporary rate that replaces it, and a WTO rate. Japan Customs explains that goods from WTO members pay the lower of the WTO rate and the General (or Temporary) rate; that is the rate shown here. Where the rates are per unit or mixed and cannot be compared, all three are shown as printed. Rates under Japan's economic partnership agreements and its preference scheme for developing countries are not included, and consumption tax is charged on top. The English edition is published for reference; the Japanese legal texts are authoritative. See the schedule on the Japan Customs site.
