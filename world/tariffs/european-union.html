European Union import tariff

The official EU Common Customs Tariff (third-country duties), as at 16 November 2026.

16,304Tariff lines with a rate
18%Duty-free lines
5.6%Average of percentage rates
2,137Lines charged per unit or mixed
Not the whole bill. These are the EU's third-country duties from its Common Customs Tariff, charged at the border of all 27 member states. Goods from countries with an EU trade agreement, or under the EU's preference scheme for developing countries, may pay less if they meet the rules of origin. Quotas, anti-dumping and other measures can apply to particular goods and origins, and import VAT and excise are set by each member state and charged on top. None of these are included below. Check a code in TARIC, the EU's official database. Member states and trade: European Union.
