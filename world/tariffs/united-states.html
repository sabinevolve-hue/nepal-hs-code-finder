United States import tariff

The official Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States, Revision 23 (2026), in force from 10 November 2026.

11,414Tariff lines with a rate
38%Duty-free lines
4.2%Average of percentage rates
1,236Lines charged per unit or mixed
Not the whole bill. These are the schedule's general rates, for goods from countries with normal trade relations. Chapter 99 of the same schedule lists 246 provisions that add duty on top of the general rate, many of them depending on the country of origin or the product; they are not included below. Lower rates under trade agreements and preference programmes are shown in the lookup as the schedule prints them. See chapter 99 on the official site.
