Australia import tariff

The official Customs Tariff Act 1995, Schedule 3, as at 16 November 2026.

6,750Tariff lines with a rate
62%Duty-free lines
1.8%Average of percentage rates
153Lines charged per unit or mixed
Not the whole bill. These are the general rates in Schedule 3 of Australia's Customs Tariff Act 1995. Where Schedule 3 itself prints a different rate for some origins, the lookup shows it with the schedule's own codes. Rates under Australia's free trade agreements are set in separate schedules of the same Act and are not shown; tariff concession orders can remove duty on particular goods, anti-dumping duties can apply, and import GST and other taxes are charged on top. See Schedule 3 on the official site.
